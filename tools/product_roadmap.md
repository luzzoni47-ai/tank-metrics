## Research: India mein log kis cheez ke paise DETE hain

### 1. Billing / GST software — subscription bik raha hai

| Product | Price |
|---|---|
| myBillBook | ₹1,499/saal se |
| Vyapar | ₹3,239/saal |
| Accountune | ₹4,999/saal (12,000+ businesses) |
| Refrens | ₹4,900/saal |
| MargBooks | ₹7,200/saal |
| Zoho Billing | ₹1,499/**mahina** |

**Sabak:** Indian small business **₹1,500-5,000/saal deta hai** — agar rozana kaam
aata hai. Aur **subscription** mein deta hai, ek baar nahi. Kyunki **GST rules
har saal badalte hain** -> har saal update chahiye -> har saal paisa.

### 2. Financial model — asli paise yahan hain

| Kahan | Price |
|---|---|
| Taylor Davidson (SaaS model) | $149 |
| Alexander Jarvis (SME SaaS) | $319 |
| Alexander Jarvis (Enterprise) | $1,299 |
| EY Finance Navigator | $30/mahina |
| **fundtq.com (INDIA)** | Pitch deck + financial model, ₹3 Cr-₹350 Cr raise ke liye |

**Sabak:** Financial model **$149-1,299 mein bikta hai** — yeh asli market hai.
Aur **fundtq.com India mein yahi kar raha hai** — matlab India mein demand hai.

### 3. CA ki fees — yeh humara "sasta vikalp" banata hai

| Kaam | CA fees |
|---|---|
| GST registration | ₹1,499 - ₹5,000 |
| GSTR-9 (annual) | ₹5,999 - ₹25,000 |
| Monthly compliance | ₹3,500 - ₹8,000/**mahina** |
| Tax audit | ₹25,000 - ₹1,50,000 |

**Sabak:** Agar hamara product ₹2,999 hai aur CA ₹8,000 leta hai, to **₹5,001
bachta hai**. Yahi bikta hai — "CA jitna achha nahi, par 1/3 daam par."

### 4. Bundles — singles se 20x zyada bikte hain

Ek Etsy seller ka asli data: bundle banane se pehle CA$101/mahina, baad mein
**CA$2,300/mahina**. Notion template $140 par $15,000/mahina. Canva templates
$1.4M.

**Sabak:** Alag-alag mat becho. **Bundle banao.**

### 5. India ke digital marketplace (yahan bech sakte hain)

ownstreet (UPI, 0% fee, ₹299/mo Pro), DigiVault, iDigitalCreator, DealsFortune,
MintLink. **Ye sab generic hain** — resume, Canva, notes. **Koi bhi Indian
business-finance product nahi bech raha.** Yahi humara gap hai.

## YEH LIST (research ke baad)

### TIER 1 — yeh banao, yeh bikega

#### 1. Indian Startup Fundraise Kit — ₹4,999 (ya ₹2,999)
Cap table + ESOP pool, 3-round dilution, ESOP grant letter, valuation 5 methods,
term sheet checklist, data room index, investor update template.

- **Kyun bikega:** Global mein $149-1,299. India mein sirf fundtq (wo services
  bechta hai, file nahi). **Yeh saaf gap hai.**
- **Kyun paid:** Investor ko yahi format chahiye. Galti yahan sabse mehngi.
- **Site par already:** 10 valuation calculators + ₹4,999 model
- **Note:** Ye ₹4,999 model ka **chhota bhai** — duplicate nahi, entry product

#### 2. GST Compliance Kit — ₹1,999/**saal** (subscription)
Monthly GSTR-1 & 3B reconciliation, ITC tracker, compliance calendar, HSN
summary, **har Budget ke baad update**.

- **Kyun bikega:** GST software ₹1,499-7,200/saal mein bik raha hai
- **Kyun subscription:** Rules har saal badalte hain -> update -> repeat paisa
- **Free invoice format se alag:** Woh format free hai. **Yeh reconciliation aur
  calendar hai** — jo free nahi milta.
- ⚠️ Invoice template **mat becho** — woh free hai

#### 3. Salary & Payroll Kit — ₹1,499/saal
CTC structure maker, salary slip, PF/ESI/gratuity/PT (state-wise), increment
letter, full & final settlement.

- **Demand: 7,274** (`salary calculator`)
- **Kyun bikega:** Har mahine chahiye + PF/ESI legal compliance hai
- **Repeat revenue:** Har saal slab badalte hain

#### 4. Income Tax Planner — ₹999/**saal**
Old vs new 3-saal comparison, deduction planner, advance tax, capital gains,
ITR checklist.

- **Demand: 4,821 + 1,329**
- **Kyun bikega:** Har saal Budget -> **har saal naya sale**
- **Benchmark:** ITR filing CA ₹999-10,000 leta hai
