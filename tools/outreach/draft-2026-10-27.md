Title: How do you actually work out ARPU, and what does a "good" number look like?

Body:

ARPU is the one number that tells you whether your users are worth more over
time or less. The arithmetic is a single division: total revenue in a period
divided by the average number of users in that same period. If you made
Rs 10,000 in a month and averaged 500 active users, your ARPU is Rs 20.

Two things people get wrong. First, they divide by total signups instead of
active users, which quietly flatters the number — a million dormant accounts
will make any revenue look tiny. Second, they mix the periods: monthly revenue
against daily users, or a quarter of revenue against a month of users.

ARPU on its own says very little. It only means something next to your CAC. If
you spend Rs 300 to acquire a user and earn Rs 20 a month, you need 15 months
just to break even, before you pay for anything else. The ratio, not the ARPU,
is the decision.

shows the steps so you can check it by hand.
