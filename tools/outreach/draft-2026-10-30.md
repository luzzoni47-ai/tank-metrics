Title: How to work out your income tax for FY 2025-26 without getting the slabs wrong

Body:

The mistake almost everyone makes is applying one rate to the whole income. Tax
in India is slab by slab: each rate applies only to the part of your income that
falls inside that band, not to the total.

Say your taxable income is Rs 10,00,000. Under the new regime the first Rs 4
lakh is nil, the next Rs 4 lakh is taxed at 5%, and only the last Rs 2 lakh
attracts 10%. The tax is nowhere near 10% of ten lakh.

Two things then decide the real number. The first is which regime you pick —
the old one allows deductions like 80C and 80D, the new one has lower rates but
almost none. On Rs 10,00,000 the new regime usually wins unless your deductions
are large. The second is the cess: 4% is added on top of whatever you owe.

I keep a free side-by-side calculator at
https://tankmetrics.in/income-tax-calculator-old-vs-new-regime — it shows both
regimes and the slab steps so you can check the maths yourself.
