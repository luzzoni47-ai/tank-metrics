Title: How HDFC fixed deposit interest is actually calculated

Fixed deposit interest is not "principal times rate". It is principal times
rate, compounded on a schedule, and then taxed — and the compounding schedule
is the part most people never check.

Say you put in Rs 1,00,000 for two years and the bank quotes 7%. On quarterly
compounding the maturity value is 1,00,000 x (1 + 0.07/4)^(4x2) = about
Rs 1,14,888. On simple interest the same deposit pays only Rs 1,14,000. That
Rs 888 gap is the compounding, and it grows with the tenure and the amount.

Three things change your answer: whether the bank compounds quarterly or
half-yearly (quarterly always pays a little more), whether you took the
cumulative option or a monthly payout, and the fact that the interest is taxed
at your slab rate every year even before maturity — so the post-tax return is
lower than the headline rate.

Senior citizens get an extra rate, usually 0.50%, which is worth checking
before you lock the money in.

value, total interest and the post-tax figure side by side.
