Title: How exit load is actually calculated when you redeem a mutual fund early

Body:

Exit load is the fee a fund house deducts if you redeem before the holding
period it set — usually 1% if you sell within 12 months, 0% after. It is
charged on the full redemption value, not on your profit, which is where most
people guess wrong.

Say you invested Rs 1,00,000 and it grew to Rs 1,20,000, and you redeem in
month eight. The load is 1% of Rs 1,20,000 — Rs 1,200 — so Rs 1,18,800 reaches
your bank, not Rs 1,20,000. Your gain is still taxed on top of that, at 20% for
equity funds held under a year.

Two things trip people up: some funds use a graded load (1% in year one, 0.5%
in year two), and liquid funds often measure the period in days, not months.
Check the scheme's own exit-load table before you sell.

I keep a free calculator at https://tankmetrics.in/exit-calculator that shows
the load and the net amount side by side.
