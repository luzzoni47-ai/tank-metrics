Title: Old vs new regime for FY 2025-26 — run the number, don't guess

People pick a regime by the size of the rebate, but the rebate is not the whole
answer. Under the new regime a salary of Rs 12,00,000 attracts no tax at all
once the standard deduction and the section 87A rebate are applied, so the
"which regime" question is really a question about your deductions, not your
income.

Do the comparison with the deductions you can actually claim. The old regime
only wins if the total of your 80C (up to Rs 1,50,000), 80D health insurance,
HRA exemption and home-loan interest is large enough to push your taxable
income below the break-even point — for most salaried people that is somewhere
around Rs 4,00,000 to Rs 4,50,000 of deductions. Below that, the new regime
usually wins; above it, the old one can.

Enter both sets of numbers rather than comparing one regime's tax to your
salary.

https://tankmetrics.in/income-tax-calculator-old-vs-new-regime that shows the
two regimes' tax side by side for the same income.
