Old vs New Tax Regime: Which Costs You Less?

Two regimes, one tax bill - find the cheaper one before you file.

Tax RulesNovember 28, 2026

Every year, before you file, you make one choice that changes your tax bill more than any investment: the old regime or the new one. The new regime charges lower rates but gives up almost every deduction. The old regime charges higher rates but lets you subtract 80C, 80D, HRA and home loan interest before the slabs begin.

Most people assume the new regime is always cheaper. For a salaried person with no investments that is usually true. For a shop owner, a freelancer or a founder with a home loan, the answer depends on two numbers, and this guide works both of them out.

What the two regimes actually change

Both regimes tax the same income. Only two things differ: the rate at which each slice of income is taxed, and how much you may remove before the slabs start.

  • New regime: lower slab rates, a larger standard deduction for salaried people, and almost no other deductions.
  • Old regime: higher slab rates, but 80C, 80D, the HRA exemption and home loan interest are all allowed.

Everything else is outside the choice. Business expenses, depreciation, presumptive taxation under 44AD or 44ADA, and the 4% health and education cess are the same either way.

The new regime slabs

In the new regime nothing is charged up to 4 lakh of taxable income, and each slice above that is taxed at its own rate:

  • Up to 4 lakh: nil
  • 4 to 8 lakh: 5%
  • 8 to 12 lakh: 10%
  • 12 to 16 lakh: 15%
  • 16 to 20 lakh: 20%
  • 20 to 24 lakh: 25%
  • Above 24 lakh: 30%

Two features make this regime generous at the bottom. Salaried taxpayers get a standard deduction of 75,000, and the 87A rebate wipes out the tax completely when taxable income is up to 12 lakh. That is why a salaried person at 12 lakh who invests nothing pays nothing at all.

The old regime slabs

The old slabs start higher and climb faster:

  • Up to 2.5 lakh: nil
  • 2.5 to 5 lakh: 5%
  • 5 to 10 lakh: 20%
  • Above 10 lakh: 30%

The 30% slab arrives at half the income it does in the new regime, which is the whole reason deductions matter here. You can subtract 1.5 lakh under 80C, 25,000 under 80D for health insurance, the HRA exemption if you are salaried and pay rent, and up to 2 lakh of home loan interest. The 87A rebate in this regime stops at 5 lakh of taxable income, so it helps only small incomes.

The only question that matters

Do not compare the headline rates. Compare two final numbers: the tax the new regime charges on your whole income, and the tax the old regime charges after your deductions. The smaller number wins.

The crossover sits high, because the new regime's first 12 lakh is effectively free for most taxpayers. For a business owner it sits even higher. You never had the HRA exemption or the standard deduction to lose, and your business costs are already subtracted from profit before tax is worked out. What you give up in the new regime is only the personal deductions: 80C, 80D, home loan interest and the extra NPS deduction under 80CCD(1B).

Worked example: 10 lakh salary, nothing invested

A salaried person earns 10 lakh and has no investments, no rent and no home loan. Both numbers, before the 4% cess:

  • New regime: taxable income is 10 lakh minus the 75,000 standard deduction, so 9.25 lakh. The slabs charge 20,000 on the 4 to 8 lakh slice and 12,500 on the 8 to 9.25 lakh slice, which is 32,500. The 87A rebate cancels it because taxable income is under 12 lakh. Tax: nil.
  • Old regime: taxable income is 10 lakh minus the 50,000 standard deduction, so 9.5 lakh. The slabs charge 12,500 on the 2.5 to 5 lakh slice and 90,000 on the 5 to 9.5 lakh slice. Tax: 1,02,500.

The new regime wins by 1,02,500. This is the case most people are in, and it is why the new regime is the default.

Worked example: 18 lakh salary with a home loan and rent

Now the case where the old regime can still win. Salary 18 lakh, rent paid to a landlord, one home loan, and a disciplined investment habit:

  • HRA exemption: 4,00,000
  • 80C investments: 1,50,000
  • 80D health insurance: 25,000
  • Home loan interest: 2,00,000
  • Standard deduction: 50,000

Total removed from the old regime: 8,25,000. Taxable income becomes 9,75,000, and the old slabs charge 12,500 on the first taxable slice plus 95,000 on the rest, which is 1,07,500.

Under the new regime those four deductions mostly vanish. Only the 75,000 standard deduction survives, so taxable income is 17,25,000. The slabs charge 20,000 up to 8 lakh, 40,000 up to 12 lakh, 60,000 up to 16 lakh and 25,000 on the last slice, which is 1,45,000.

The old regime wins by 37,500. Add the 4% cess to both and it stays ahead, because cess is charged on whichever tax is smaller.

Worked example: 15 lakh business income

A proprietor with 15 lakh of taxable business profit, a 1.5 lakh 80C investment, 25,000 of 80D and a 2 lakh home loan interest. There is no standard deduction and no HRA for business income, which is what changes the answer:

  • Old regime: deductions of 3,75,000 leave 11,25,000 taxable. The slabs charge 12,500 plus 1,25,000, which is 1,37,500.
  • New regime: deductions do not apply, so the whole 15,00,000 is taxed. The slabs charge 20,000 up to 8 lakh, 40,000 up to 12 lakh and 45,000 on the last 3 lakh, which is 1,05,000.

The new regime wins by 32,500, even with a home loan and a full 80C. Losing the 30% slab is worth more than the deductions are.

If you run a business, the choice is stickier

A salaried taxpayer can switch regimes in every return, so a bad year costs nothing. A taxpayer with business income cannot do that. Opting out of the new regime means filing Form 10-IEA with the return, and you may return to the new regime only once in your life. Treat it as a decision, not a yearly toggle.

Opt out only if the old regime wins by a margin you expect to keep for several years, and only if you will actually use the deductions. An 80C investment made in March to save tax usually earns less than the tax it saves, so make the investment for its own sake first and count the deduction second.

Mistakes that cost real money

  • Comparing slab rates instead of final tax. A lower rate on paper loses to a bigger deduction in practice.
  • Counting deductions you cannot claim. The old regime does not allow the standard deduction on business income, and HRA needs rent actually paid to a landlord.
  • Forgetting the cess. Both regimes add 4% health and education cess on the tax, so the final bill is 4% higher than the slab total.
  • Opting out for one good year. For business income the switch back is a once-in-a-lifetime move, so a single year of heavy investment is a poor reason.

How to decide in one minute

  1. Write down your taxable income under each regime, after every deduction you can genuinely claim.
  2. Apply each regime's slabs to its own taxable income.
  3. Add 4% cess to both.
  4. Pick the smaller number, and if you have business income, ask whether the gap will still be there next year.

Slabs, rebate limits and deduction caps are reset in each year's Budget, so confirm the table for your assessment year before you commit, and run your own numbers rather than a neighbour's.

Har saal ritarn faail karne se pehle aap ek faisala karte hain jo kisi bhi nivesh se zyada aapka tax badal deta hai: puraane rijeem ya nayi rijeem. Nayi rijeem mein ret kam hain, lekin lagabhag saare chhooten khatm. Puraane rijeem mein ret zyada hain, lekin 80C, 80D, HRA aur hom lon ka byaj slaib se pehle ghata sakte hain.

Zyadatar log samajhate hain ki nayi rijeem hamesha sasti hai. Bina nivesh wale sailerid insaan ke liye yeh akasar sach hai. Lekin dukaan, freelaansing ya staartaap chalaane wale ke liye jawab do aankdon par tika hai, aur yeh guide dono nikaal kar dikhati hai.

Dono rijeem mein asal farq kya hai

Dono rijeem ek hi aay par tax lagaate hain. Farq sirf do cheezon mein hai: har slaais par lagane wali dar, aur slaib shuru hone se pehle kitna ghata ja sakta hai.

  • Nayi rijeem: kam slaib daren, sailerid logon ke liye bada staindard didakshan, aur baaki chhooten lagabhag nahi.
  • Puraane rijeem: zyada slaib daren, lekin 80C, 80D, HRA chhoot aur hom lon byaj sab milate hain.

Baaki sab faisale se baahar hai. Karobar ke kharche, diprisieshan, 44AD ya 44ADA ke tahat prizamptiv taikseshan, aur 4% helth end ejukeshan ses dono mein ek jaise hain.

Nayi rijeem ke slaib

Nayi rijeem mein 4 laakh taiksebal aay tak kuch nahi lagta, aur usake baad har hisse par uski apni dar lagti hai:

  • 4 laakh tak: shoony
  • 4 se 8 laakh: 5%
  • 8 se 12 laakh: 10%
  • 12 se 16 laakh: 15%
  • 16 se 20 laakh: 20%
  • 20 se 24 laakh: 25%
  • 24 laakh se upar: 30%

Nichale hisse mein yeh rijeem khaas udaar hai. Sailerid logon ko 75,000 ka staindard didakshan milta hai, aur 87A ribet pura tax khatm kar deta hai jab taiksebal aay 12 laakh tak ho. Iseelie 12 laakh kamaane wala bina nivesh wala sailerid insaan kuch bhi nahi deta.

Puraane rijeem ke slaib

Puraane slaib oonche se shuru hote hain aur tezi se badhte hain:

  • 2.5 laakh tak: shoony
  • 2.5 se 5 laakh: 5%
  • 5 se 10 laakh: 20%
  • 10 laakh se upar: 30%

30% ka slaib yahaan utni hi aay par aa jaata hai jitane nayi rijeem mein dogune aay par aata hai, aur iseelie yahaan chhooten maayne rakhate hain. Aap 80C mein 1.5 laakh, helth bima ke liye 80D mein 25,000, sailerid hone par kiraaye ka HRA chhoot, aur hom lon byaj mein 2 laakh tak ghata sakte hain. Is rijeem ka 87A ribet 5 laakh taiksebal aay par ruk jaata hai, isliye chhoti aay ke alaava kame kaam aata hai.

Sirf ek sawal maayne rakhta hai

Upar ki daren dekh kar tulna na karein. Do aakhare aankde dekhein: nayi rijeem poore aay par jitna tax lete hai, aur puraane rijeem chhooton ke baad jitna lete hai. Jo chhota hai wahi jeetata hai.

Yeh mukaabala oonchaai par palatata hai, kyunki nayi rijeem ka pehla 12 laakh zyadatar logon ke liye lagabhag muft hai. Karobar chalaane wale ke liye yeh aur bhi ooncha hai. Aapke paas HRA chhoot ya staindard didakshan pehle se hi nahi tha, aur karobar ke kharche tax se pehle munafe se ghat chuke hote hain. Nayi rijeem mein aap sirf nije chhooten chhodte hain: 80C, 80D, hom lon byaj aur 80CCD(1B) ke tahat atirikt NPS kataute.

Udaharan: 10 laakh salary, koi nivesh nahi

Ek sailerid insaan 10 laakh kamata hai, koi nivesh nahi, kiraya nahi, hom lon nahi. 4% ses se pehle dono aankde:

  • Nayi rijeem: taiksebal aay 10 laakh mein se 75,000 staindard didakshan ghata kar 9.25 laakh. Slaib 4 se 8 laakh par 20,000 aur 8 se 9.25 laakh par 12,500 lagaate hain, jo 32,500 hai. Taiksebal aay 12 laakh se kam hai, isliye 87A ribet ise pura khatm kar deta hai. Tax: shoony.
  • Puraane rijeem: taiksebal aay 10 laakh mein se 50,000 staindard didakshan ghata kar 9.5 laakh. Slaib 2.5 se 5 laakh par 12,500 aur 5 se 9.5 laakh par 90,000 lagaate hain. Tax: 1,02,500.

Nayi rijeem 1,02,500 se jeetate hai. Zyadatar log isi haalat mein hote hain, aur iseelie nayi rijeem difolt hai.

Udaharan: 18 laakh salary, hom lon aur kiraya

Ab woh haalat, jahaan puraane rijeem ab bhi jeet sakate hai. Salary 18 laakh, makaan maalik ko kiraya, ek hom lon, aur niyam se nivesh:

  • HRA chhoot: 4,00,000
  • 80C nivesh: 1,50,000
  • 80D helth bima: 25,000
  • Hom lon byaj: 2,00,000
  • Staindard didakshan: 50,000

Puraane rijeem mein kul ghataute 8,25,000. Taiksebal aay 9,75,000 bachate hai, aur puraane slaib 12,500 pehle hisse par aur 95,000 baaki par lagaate hain, jo 1,07,500 hai.

Nayi rijeem mein ye chaaron chhooten lagabhag khatm ho jaati hain. Sirf 75,000 ka staindard didakshan bachta hai, isliye taiksebal aay 17,25,000 hai. Slaib 8 laakh tak 20,000, 12 laakh tak 40,000, 16 laakh tak 60,000 aur aakhare hisse par 25,000 lagaate hain, jo 1,45,000 hai.

Puraane rijeem 37,500 se jeetate hai. Dono par 4% ses jodein to bhi yeh aage rahate hai, kyunki ses jo tax chhota ho usi par lagta hai.

Udaharan: 15 laakh business aay

Ek propraaitar, 15 laakh taiksebal karobari munafa, 1.5 laakh 80C nivesh, 25,000 80D aur 2 laakh hom lon byaj. Karobari aay par staindard didakshan aur HRA nahi milta, aur yahi jawab badal deta hai:

  • Puraane rijeem: 3,75,000 chhooton ke baad 11,25,000 taiksebal bachta hai. Slaib 12,500 aur 1,25,000 lagaate hain, jo 1,37,500 hai.
  • Nayi rijeem: chhooten nahi lagateen, isliye pura 15,00,000 tax hota hai. Slaib 8 laakh tak 20,000, 12 laakh tak 40,000 aur aakhare 3 laakh par 45,000 lagaate hain, jo 1,05,000 hai.

Hom lon aur pura 80C hone par bhi nayi rijeem 32,500 se jeetate hai. 30% slaib chhodne ka fayda chhooton se bada hai.

Business chalate hain to faisala pakka hota hai

Sailerid taiksapeyar har ritarn mein rijeem badal sakta hai, isliye ek kharaab saal kuch nahi bigaadata. Karobari aay wala yeh nahi kar sakta. Nayi rijeem se baahar nikalane ka matlab hai ritarn ke saath form 10-IEA dena, aur zindage mein sirf ek baar nayi rijeem mein laut sakte hain. Ise saalana batan nahi, ek faisala samjhein.

Baahar sirf tab nikalen jab puraane rijeem kai saal tak aise faayade mein rahe, aur jab aap vaakaee chhooten istemal karein. Maarch mein sirf tax bachaane ke liye kiya gaya 80C nivesh aam taur par utna kamata nahi jitna tax bachaata hai, isliye nivesh pehle apne liye karein aur chhoot baad mein ginen.

Ve galatiyaan jo sach mein paisa khaate hain

  • Slaib daren dekhna, aakhare tax nahi. Kaaghaz par kam dar haqeeqat mein bade chhoot se haar jaati hai.
  • Aise chhooten ginana jo mil nahi sakateen. Puraane rijeem karobari aay par staindard didakshan nahi dete, aur HRA ke liye makaan maalik ko kiraya dena zaroore hai.
  • Ses bhoolana. Dono rijeem tax par 4% helth aur ejukeshan ses jodate hain, isliye aakhare bil slaib jod se 4% zyada hota hai.
  • Ek achhe saal ke liye baahar nikalana. Karobari aay mein vaapasi zindage mein ek hi baar hoti hai, isliye ek saal ka bhaari nivesh baahar nikalane ki vajah nahi ho sakta.

Ek minat mein faisala kaise karein

  1. Har rijeem mein apni taiksebal aay likhen, har aise chhoot ke baad jo sach mein mil sakate hai.
  2. Har rijeem ke slaib uski apni taiksebal aay par lagaaen.
  3. Dono par 4% ses jodein.
  4. Chhota aankda chunen, aur agar karobari aay hai to poochein ki yeh antar agle saal bhi rahega ya nahi.

Slaib, ribet seemaaen aur chhoot ki seemaaen har saal ke bajat mein badalate hain, isliye faisala karne se pehle apne assesament iyar ki taalika dekhein, aur padose ke aankdon ke bajaay apne aankde nikalein.
