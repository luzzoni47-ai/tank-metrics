FD Interest After TDS: What You Really Keep

The rate on the board is not the return you keep.

Everyday Money BasicsNovember 24, 2026

Most shop owners and freelancers treat a fixed deposit as the safe option: the rate is printed on the bank's board, the money cannot fall, and the interest arrives on a fixed date. What the board does not show is the tax that comes off that interest, and the penalty that applies if you need the money back early.

This guide walks through what an FD really pays after tax, using small round examples you can scale to your own amount, and explains the one decision that matters more than the rate: how long you can actually leave the money alone.

The rate on the board is not your return

FD interest is added to your income for the year and taxed at your slab rate. A shop's profit, a freelancer's fees and FD interest all sit in the same bucket, so the slab that applies to your business income also applies to the interest.

That single fact explains why two people booking the same FD at the same rate keep different amounts. The bank pays the same interest to both; the tax on it does not.

How the interest is actually calculated

Banks quote an annual rate but add interest every quarter, and the next quarter is calculated on the grown balance. So the effective yearly return is slightly above the quoted rate, and a longer tenure gains a little more.

Take ₹8,000 at 7.5% for one year. The quoted rate gives ₹600 of interest. Because the interest is compounded every quarter, the actual credit is a little more than ₹600, but ₹600 is close enough to plan with.

Keep the same ₹8,000 for two years and the simple total is ₹1,200. Compounding lifts it to roughly ₹1,300. That extra ₹100 is the whole benefit of a longer lock-in at the same rate.

TDS: the tax the bank takes before you see it

When the interest a bank pays you in a financial year crosses the limit set in the income tax rules, the bank deducts 10% as TDS before crediting the interest. The limit is revised in budgets, so check the current figure for the year you are booking in.

  • TDS is an advance against your final tax, not a separate charge. It shows in your Form 26AS and is adjusted when you file.
  • If your total income is below the taxable limit, give the bank Form 15G, or Form 15H if you are a senior citizen, so it does not deduct TDS.
  • In a cumulative FD the interest is added to the deposit instead of being paid out, and TDS still applies because the interest has accrued.
  • If your slab is higher than 10%, TDS covers only part of the tax; the rest is payable when you file.

What you keep, by slab

Stay with ₹600 of yearly interest and look at the tax on it in each slab, before the health and education cess:

  • At 5%, tax is ₹30 and you keep ₹570, or 95% of the interest.
  • At 20%, tax is ₹120 and you keep ₹480, or 80%.
  • At 30%, tax is ₹180 and you keep ₹420, or 70%.

A shop owner in the 30% slab keeps seven rupees of every ten the FD earns. That is why a 7.5% deposit behaves like a risk-free 5.25% for them, and why comparing an FD's rate with another product's rate without adjusting for tax gives the wrong answer.

The cost of breaking an FD early

If you close an FD before its tenure ends, banks usually cut the rate by one percentage point and recalculate the interest on the shorter period you actually stayed. On ₹8,000 at 7.5%, breaking the deposit after a year pays about 6.5% instead: roughly ₹520 instead of ₹600, so the penalty is about ₹80.

The trap is not the ₹80. It is that the money was never free to begin with: an emergency fund kept in a locked deposit becomes a penalty the moment it is used. Keep at least one month of running costs in a savings account or a sweep, and lock only what you can genuinely leave alone.

Where a shop's spare cash should sit

  • Money needed in the next 30 days: savings account or sweep, not an FD.
  • Money needed in 3 to 12 months: a short-tenure FD, so the maturity lands before the payment does.
  • Money not needed for a year or more: a ladder of FDs maturing on different dates, so something frees up every few months without breaking anything.
  • Money that must stay untouched for five years, and you are an individual: a tax-saving FD, which also counts towards the 80C deduction within its overall limit.

Two checks before you book. First, deposit insurance covers each bank deposit only up to the limit set by the DICGC, which is 5 lakh rupees per depositor per bank, so a large surplus spread across two banks is safer than one large deposit. Second, compare the post-tax rate rather than the board rate: multiply the FD rate by the share you keep after tax.

A checklist before you book

  1. Write down when you will actually need the money, then pick a tenure shorter than that.
  2. Multiply the rate by what you keep after tax, not by one.
  3. Ask whether the interest will cross the TDS limit in this financial year.
  4. If your income is below the taxable limit, submit Form 15G or 15H at the start of the year.
  5. Split deposits across banks instead of crossing the insurance limit at one.

Do those five and the FD does the job it is meant for: it holds cash safely, at a return you can predict after tax instead of one that only looks good on the board.

Bord par do cheezein nahi dikhateen — us byaj par lagane wala tax, aur paisa jaldi nikalne par lagane wala jurmaana.

Yeh guide bataate hai ki ephade tax ke baad asal mein kitna dete hai, chhote gol aankadon ke udaharan se jinhen aap apni rakam par badha sakte hain, aur us ek faisale par, jo byaj dar se bhi zyada maayne rakhta hai: paisa kitne samay tak sachamuch achhoota rah sakta hai.

Bord ki dar aapka ritarn nahi hai

Ephade ka byaj saal ke aapke aay mein judta hai aur aapke slaib ret par tax lagta hai. Dukaan ka munafa, phreelaansar ki fees aur ephade ka byaj teenon ek hi tokare mein aate hain, isliye aapki business aay par jo slaib lagta hai, wahi byaj par bhi lagta hai.

Yahi ek baat samjhati hai ki ek hi dar par ephade karaane wale do logon ke haath mein alag-alag rakam kyon bachate hai. Bank dono ko ek jaisa byaj deta hai; us par tax alag padta hai.

Byaj ka hisaab asal mein kaise banata hai

Bank saalana dar batate hain, par byaj har timaahe jodate hain, aur agli timaahe badhe huee rakam par banate hai. Isliye saal ka asli ritarn bataaee gayi dar se thoda upar rehta hai, aur lambi avadhi mein fayda thoda aur badh jaata hai.

₹8,000 ko ek saal ke liye 7.5% par rakhie. Bataaee gayi dar se ₹600 byaj banata hai. Timaahe kampaaunding ke kaaran asal mein ₹600 se thoda zyada jama hota hai, par yojana banane ke liye ₹600 kaafe hai.

Wahi ₹8,000 do saal ke liye rakhie to seedha jod ₹1,200 banata hai. Kampaaunding use lagabhag ₹1,300 tak le jaati hai. Yahi atirikt ₹100 usi dar par lambi avadhi ka pura fayda hai.

Teedees: woh tax jo bank aapase pehle kaat leta hai

Jab kisi vitt varsh mein bank ka diya byaj aayakar niyamon mein tay seema se upar jaata hai, to bank byaj jama karne se pehle 10% teedees kaat leta hai. Yeh seema bajat mein badalate rahate hai, isliye jis saal ephade kara rahe hain us saal ki maujooda seema dekh lijiye.

  • Teedees aapke antim tax ke evaz mein pehle se kata paisa hai, alag chaarj nahi. Woh aapke form 26AS mein dikhta hai aur ritarn bharate samay samaayojit ho jaata hai.
  • Agar aapki kul aay kar yogy seema se kam hai, to bank ko form 15jee deejie, ya varishth naagarik hain to form 15ech, taaki teedees na kate.
  • Kyoomuletiv ephade mein byaj aapke khaate mein nahi aata, jama mein hi judta hai, phir bhi teedees lagta hai kyunki byaj ban chuka hai.
  • Agar aapka slaib 10% se upar hai, to teedees tax ka sirf ek hissa dhakata hai; baaqe ritarn bharate samay dena padta hai.

Kis slaib mein kitna bachta hai

₹600 ke saalana byaj par hi rahie aur helth v ejukeshan ses se pehle har slaib ka tax dekhie:

  • 5% par tax ₹30 hai aur aapke paas ₹570 bachate hain, yani byaj ka 95%.
  • 20% par tax ₹120 hai aur aapke paas ₹480 bachate hain, yani 80%.
  • 30% par tax ₹180 hai aur aapke paas ₹420 bachate hain, yani 70%.

30% slaib wale dukaanadaar ke paas ephade ki har das rupaye kamai mein se saat rupaye bachate hain. Iseelie 7.5% ki ephade unake liye bina jokhim wale 5.25% jaise hai, aur iseelie tax ka hisaab lagaae bina ephade ki dar ki tulna kisi doosare product ki dar se karna ghalat jawab deta hai.

Ephade beech mein tudavaane ki qeemat

Avadhi poore hone se pehle ephade band karne par bank aam taur par dar mein ek pratishat ank ki kataute karte hain aur jitne samay tak paisa raha usi par byaj dobaara jodate hain. ₹8,000 par 7.5% ki jagah ek saal baad tudavaane par lagabhag 6.5% milta hai: ₹600 ki jagah qareeb ₹520, yani qareeb ₹80 ka nuqasaan.

Asli jaal ₹80 nahi hai. Asli baat yeh hai ki woh paisa kabhi mukt tha hi nahi: imarajense fand taalaaband jama mein rakha ho to istemal ke pehle hi pal woh jurmaana ban jaata hai. Mahine ka chalaana kam se kam ek mahine ka kharch bachat khaate ya sveep mein rakhie, aur sirf wahi paisa lok keejie jo sachamuch achhoota rah sakta hai.

Dukaan ka atirikt paisa kahan rakhein

  • Agle 30 din mein chahiye: bachat khaata ya sveep, ephade nahi.
  • 3 se 12 mahine mein chahiye: chhoti avadhi ki ephade, taaki maichyorite bhugataan se pehle aa jaye.
  • Ek saal ya zyada tak nahi chahiye: alag-alag taareekhon par maichyor hoti ephade ki seedhi, taaki har kuch mahinon mein kuch paisa bina kuch tode khul jaye.
  • Paanch saal tak achhoota rahe aur aap vyakti hain: tax-seving ephade, jo 80see ki seema ke bheetar kataute mein bhi gine jaati hai.

Ephade karaane se pehle do jaanch. Pehli, jama bima har bank ki jama par sirf deeaaeeseejeese ki tay seema tak laagoo hota hai, yani prati jamaakarta prati bank 5 laakh rupaye, isliye bade rakam do bainkon mein baantana ek bade jama se surakshit hai. Doosare, bord ki dar nahi, tax ke baad bachane wali dar ki tulna keejie: ephade ki dar ko tax ke baad bachane wale hisse se guna keejie.

Ephade karaane se pehle ki sooche

  1. Likhie ki paisa asal mein kab chahiye hoga, phir usse chhoti avadhi chunie.
  2. Dar ko tax ke baad bachane wale hisse se guna keejie, ek se nahi.
  3. Poochhie ki is vitt varsh mein byaj teedees ki seema paar karega ya nahi.
  4. Agar aay kar yogy seema se kam hai, to saal ki shuruaat mein form 15jee ya 15ech jama keejie.
  5. Ek hi bank mein bima seema paar karne ki jagah jama ko bainkon mein baantie.

Ye paanch kaam kar liye to ephade woh karti hai jisake liye woh bane hai: paisa surakshit rakhate hai, aise dar par jisaka tax ke baad ka hisaab aap pehle se laga sakte hain, na ki us dar par jo sirf bord par achhi lagti hai.
