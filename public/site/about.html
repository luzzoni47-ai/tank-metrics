🇮🇳 About Tank Metrics

Tank Metrics is not another boring finance site

It is a free set of 100+ business calculators built for the people who actually build things: first-time founders, D2C owners, college students, and anyone who has watched Shark Tank and wondered how valuation works.

About Tank Metrics

Profit margin? Valuation before a pitch? Is your LTV:CAC healthy? Or just splitting a bill with friends? Tank Metrics gives you the answer in 3 seconds. No login, no Excel sheet, no MBA jargon.

Every calculator comes with a real-world explanation in plain English. You get the number, what it means, and what to do next.

From core startup math like EBITDA, burn rate, runway and dilution to everyday money tools like EMI, SIP and tip split, Tank Metrics covers your full journey, from your first paycheck to your first pitch.

Our story

M
Mk Aamir

Founder, Tank Metrics, Bangalore, India

Mk Aamir has worked with early-stage startups and D2C founders across India on growth and financial modelling.

Tank Metrics started from a small desk in Bangalore, out of one frustration: why is every financial tool made only for finance experts?

So Mk Aamir built it for beginners. For students who have never opened a balance sheet. For founders who think in lakhs and crores, not dollars.

What started as 5 calculators for Shark Tank fans is now India's most relatable business math toolkit. It runs on one mission: make business math so simple that a 19-year-old first-time founder can make a board-level decision in 30 seconds.

We are remote-first, founder-focused, and obsessed with one thing: clarity over complexity.
