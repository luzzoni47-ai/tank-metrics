Zavya raises ₹22.6 crore at 32 times its sales

Silver jewellery brand Zavya raised ₹22.6 crore at a ₹191.5 crore valuation. Its ₹6 crore of FY25 sales left just ₹54.7 lakh of profit, or under 1% margin.

Small BusinessNovember 25, 2026

Zavya, a budget silver jewellery brand, has raised ₹22.6 crore in a pre-Series A round led by Daksham Growth Ventures, the growth investment arm of Daksham Capital. Existing investor Orios Venture Partners also put in money. The allotment values the five-year-old company at about ₹191.5 crore, on a post-money basis.

The brand was founded in 2021 by Poem Kabra and Ravi Malani. It sells 925 sterling silver rings, pendants, earrings and anklets to women between 22 and 35, priced between ₹699 and ₹2,299, and brings out more than 50 new styles a month. It sells through its own website and app, through quick commerce apps such as Blinkit and Zepto, and through marketplaces including Amazon, Myntra and Nykaa.

What the money is for

Kabra said the company would use the funds to strengthen its presence across D2C and quick commerce, invest in long-term brand building, and take its first steps into physical retail. That last phrase is the one to underline. A brand that has sold only online until now is about to take on rent, deposits, fit-outs and staff — the costs that a warehouse and a delivery app never charge. Physical retail can lift a brand's credibility and its average order value, but it also turns fixed costs on.

₹6 crore of sales, ₹55 lakh of profit

For FY25, Zavya reported revenue of ₹6 crore, up 38% from the previous year, and a net profit of ₹54.7 lakh. It says it has stayed EBITDA-positive while scaling. Both are respectable for a young consumer brand, and the revenue growth is the number the round is really paying for. But look at the margin. ₹54.7 lakh of profit on ₹6 crore of sales is about 0.9%: on every ₹1,000 of jewellery sold, the business keeps about ₹9. The rest goes to silver, making charges, packaging, marketplace commissions, ads, salaries and delivery.

That is not a criticism. Thin margins are normal in everyday jewellery, where the metal and the making are most of the price and the customer compares you with a neighbourhood silversmith. The question for an investor is whether volume and repeat purchases can carry the business while the brand gets strong enough to charge more.

Why 32 times sales is the real headline

₹191.5 crore against ₹6 crore of FY25 sales is about 32 times revenue. Against ₹54.7 lakh of profit, it is roughly 350 times earnings. A shop being valued at 32 times its annual sales is not being bought for what it earns today; it is being bought for the next five years of growth. For context, the round itself is about 12% of the new valuation, which tells you how little of the company the cheque buys and how much of the price is expectation.

The market story behind that expectation is simple. Credence Research puts India's 925 sterling silver jewellery D2C market at $614 million in 2025 and expects it to reach $2.28 billion by 2035, growing at about 14% a year. Online channels already carry 60% of the market's value, and quick commerce is one of the faster-growing routes. Zavya is competing with older silver names such as Beliram Legacy, TrueSilver and Silver Emporium, and with venture-backed brands such as Giva and Palmonas.

The ₹1,200 order and what it leaves

Take the price band and do the arithmetic. If the average order is about ₹1,200, then ₹6 crore of sales is roughly 50,000 orders a year, or about 140 orders a day. Spread the ₹54.7 lakh of profit across those 50,000 orders and each one leaves about ₹110 before the fixed costs of running the company. At ₹1,000 of sales the shop keeps about ₹9; at ₹6,000 it keeps about ₹55. Those are the numbers that decide whether a discount, a return or a courier charge turns the order into a loss.

Selling on marketplaces and quick commerce adds another layer. Each channel takes a commission, a payment fee or a delivery charge, and each one owns the customer relationship rather than the brand. A silver ring that sells for ₹1,200 may leave far less than ₹1,200 in the bank once the platform is paid. That is why brands push their own website and app, where the margin is better but the cost of finding the customer is theirs to carry.

What it means for you

If you run a shop or are building a brand, the lesson is in the shape of the numbers, not the ₹22.6 crore. Zavya grew 38% in a year and still kept under ₹1 in every ₹100 of sales. That is not failure; it is what a growing retail business looks like before scale makes the fixed costs small. The mistake to avoid is confusing sales with profit. A ₹10 lakh month at a 1% net margin leaves ₹10,000, which is one part-time salary, and a single bad discount month can wipe it out. Know your margin per order, know what each channel takes, and price for the profit you actually keep.

Paisa kis cheez ke liye hai

Kabra ne kaha ki company funds ka use D2C aur quick commerce mein apni presence badhane, long-term brand building mein invest karne, aur physical retail mein pehla kadam rakhne ke liye karegi. Aakhri baat par dhyan do. Jo brand ab tak sirf online bech raha hai, woh ab rent, deposit, fit-out aur staff — woh costs — uthane wala hai jo warehouse aur delivery app kabhi nahi lete. Physical retail brand ki credibility aur average order value badha sakta hai, lekin fixed costs bhi chalu kar deta hai.

₹6 crore ki sales, ₹55 lakh ka profit

FY25 mein Zavya ne ₹6 crore ka revenue bataya, pichhle saal se 38% zyada, aur ₹54.7 lakh ka net profit. Company kehti hai ki scaling ke beech woh EBITDA-positive rahi hai. Dono ek young consumer brand ke liye theek hain, aur revenue growth hi asli cheez hai jiske liye round hua hai. Lekin margin dekho. ₹6 crore ki sales par ₹54.7 lakh profit matlab lagbhag 0.9%: har ₹1,000 ki jewellery bikne par business ke paas lagbhag ₹9 bachte hain. Baaki paisa silver, making charges, packaging, marketplace commission, ads, salary aur delivery mein chala jaata hai.

Yeh koi burai nahi hai. Everyday jewellery mein patla margin normal hai, kyunki metal aur making hi price ka bada hissa hote hain aur customer aapko mohalle ke silversmith se compare karta hai. Investor ke liye sawaal yeh hai ki volume aur repeat purchase business ko kitna aage le ja sakte hain, jab tak brand itna strong ho ki zyada charge kar sake.

32 guna sales hi asli headline hai

₹191.5 crore valuation aur ₹6 crore ki FY25 sales, matlab lagbhag 32 guna revenue. ₹54.7 lakh profit ke saamne yeh lagbhag 350 guna earnings hai. Jo dukaan apni saal ki sales ke 32 gune par value ho, woh aaj ke profit ke liye nahi kharidi jaati; woh agle paanch saal ke growth ke liye kharidi jaati hai. Context ke liye, round khud naye valuation ka lagbhag 12% hai, jo batata hai ki cheque company ka kitna chhota hissa kharidta hai aur price mein kitni expectation hai.

Is expectation ke peeche market ki kahani simple hai. Credence Research ke mutabik India ka 925 sterling silver jewellery D2C market 2025 mein $614 million hai aur 2035 tak $2.28 billion pahunch sakta hai, lagbhag 14% saalana growth ke saath. Online channels pehle se market value ka 60% le rahe hain, aur quick commerce tezi se badhne wale routes mein se ek hai. Zavya ka muqabla Beliram Legacy, TrueSilver aur Silver Emporium jaise purane silver names se hai, aur Giva aur Palmonas jaise venture-backed brands se bhi.

₹1,200 ka order aur usme se kya bachta hai

Price band lo aur arithmetic karo. Agar average order lagbhag ₹1,200 hai, to ₹6 crore ki sales matlab saal mein lagbhag 50,000 orders, ya roz lagbhag 140 orders. ₹54.7 lakh profit ko 50,000 orders par baanto to har order se lagbhag ₹110 bachte hain, company chalane ki fixed costs se pehle. ₹1,000 ki sales par dukaan ke paas lagbhag ₹9 bachte hain; ₹6,000 par lagbhag ₹55. Yahi numbers decide karte hain ki ek discount, ek return ya ek courier charge order ko loss mein badal deta hai ya nahi.

Marketplace aur quick commerce par bechna ek aur layer jodta hai. Har channel commission, payment fee ya delivery charge leta hai, aur customer relationship brand ke paas nahi, channel ke paas rehta hai. ₹1,200 ka silver ring bechne ke baad platform ko paisa dene ke baad bank mein ₹1,200 se kaafi kam aata hai. Isliye brands apni website aur app push karte hain, jahan margin behtar hota hai lekin customer laane ka kharcha khud uthana padta hai.

Aapke liye iska matlab

Agar aap dukaan chalate hain ya brand bana rahe hain, to sabak numbers ke shape mein hai, ₹22.6 crore mein nahi. Zavya ek saal mein 38% badhi aur phir bhi har ₹100 ki sales par ₹1 se kam bachaya. Yeh failure nahi hai; badhti retail business aisi hi dikhti hai jab tak scale fixed costs ko chhota na kar de. Galti sales ko profit samajhne ki hai. 1% net margin par ₹10 lakh ka mahina ₹10,000 chhodta hai, jo ek part-time salary hai, aur ek kharab discount month use khatam kar sakta hai. Apna per-order margin jaano, har channel kya leta hai jaano, aur us profit ke liye price karo jo aap sach mein rakhte hain.
