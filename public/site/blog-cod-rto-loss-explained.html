COD and RTO: Where Your Profit Goes

Every returned COD parcel is paid for by the orders that did deliver.

Pricing & CostsNovember 25, 2026

An RTO — return to origin — is a parcel that never reaches the customer. The courier carries it out, tries the address, fails, and carries it back. You pay for both legs, you get no money, and the stock sits in your warehouse for a week or two before it can be sold again.

Most sellers count RTO as a delivery problem. It is a pricing problem. Every returned COD order is paid for by the orders that did get delivered, which means your real margin per order is lower than the one on your spreadsheet.

What one returned parcel actually costs

There is no single "RTO charge". Four separate costs land on you:

  • Forward freight — the courier already earned this when the parcel left your warehouse.
  • Return freight — often charged at the same rate, sometimes higher.
  • Packaging — the box, tape, filler and label are gone.
  • Handling — someone opens it, checks it, re-packs it, and the stock is blocked for days.

Add those up for your own shipments and you get one number: the loss per RTO order. That single figure is the input that turns an RTO rate into money.

A worked example: 100 COD orders

Take a seller with 100 COD orders in a month, an average order value of ₹1,000, a product cost of ₹500, forward shipping of ₹80, and a loss per RTO order of ₹250 (₹80 out, ₹80 back, ₹40 packaging, ₹50 repacking).

  1. RTO rate of 20% → 20 parcels come back.
  2. RTO loss: 20 × ₹250 = ₹5,000.
  3. Contribution on a delivered order: ₹1,000 − ₹500 − ₹80 = ₹420.
  4. 80 delivered orders × ₹420 = 33,600 earned.
  5. After the RTO loss: 33,600 − ₹5,000 = 28,600 left.

Five thousand rupees is roughly a seventh of the gross contribution, gone on parcels that never earned a rupee. The same seller at a 10% RTO rate keeps 35,300 instead of 28,600 — the rate matters more than most pricing decisions.

What RTO does to margin per delivered order

It is tempting to treat the ₹5,000 as an overhead and move on. But it was caused by the orders that shipped, so it belongs in the per-order margin.

₹5,000 spread over the 80 orders that did deliver is about ₹63 per order. Your true contribution is ₹420 − ₹63 = ₹357, not ₹420. Any ad spend, discount or COD fee you approve has to fit inside ₹357, or the month loses money even though every individual sale looked profitable.

This is why a shop can be busy, sell at a healthy markup, and still run short of cash at month end.

The prepaid trade-off, in numbers

The obvious fix is to push customers to prepay. The obvious fix is also a discount, so it needs arithmetic before you offer it.

Suppose 20 of the 100 orders move to prepaid at a ₹50 discount, and that this removes 10 of the 20 RTOs:

  1. Discount given: 20 × ₹50 = ₹1,000.
  2. RTO loss avoided: 10 × ₹250 = ₹2,500.
  3. Net gain: ₹2,500 − ₹1,000 = ₹1,500.

The trade only works because the loss per RTO order is five times the discount. If your loss per RTO order were ₹60, the same offer would cost you more than it saves. Run the numbers with your own figures before you announce anything.

Four places to cut the rate

  • Address quality. Ask for a landmark and a phone number that a rider can actually reach. Most failed attempts are address failures, not refusals.
  • COD limits. Cap COD by order value and by pincode. High-value parcels to weak-serviceability pincodes are where the loss concentrates.
  • Confirmation before dispatch. A WhatsApp or call confirmation on COD orders filters out fake and forgotten orders before freight is spent.
  • Honest delivery dates. A customer who is told five days and waits twelve has often already bought elsewhere when the rider calls.

What to track every month

Three numbers, nothing more: COD orders, RTO rate, and loss per RTO order. Multiply the first two and you have the count of returned parcels; multiply that by the third and you have the money you lost.

A rate under 10% is generally considered healthy, though it varies a lot by category — fashion returns far more than groceries. What matters more than the benchmark is the trend. A rate that drifts from 12% to 18% over two months is a pricing change you did not make on purpose.

Once the loss sits in your per-order margin, RTO stops being bad luck and becomes a cost you can price, cap and reduce.

RTO yani return to origin — woh paarsal jo graahak tak pahunchata hi nahi. Kooriyar use lekar nikalta hai, pata dhoondhata hai, nahi milta, aur paarsal vaapas laut aata hai. Dono taraf ka kiraya aap dete hain, paisa nahi milta, aur stok ek-do hafte godaam mein pada rah jaata hai.

Zyadatar selar RTO ko dileevare ki samasya maanate hain. Asal mein yeh pricing ki samasya hai. Har lauta hua COD order un ordaron se bhara jaata hai jo sach mein pahunche, yani aapka asli prati-order munafa spredasheet wale aankde se kam hai.

Ek laute paarsal ki asli lagat

"RTO chaarj" jaisa koi ek aankda nahi hota. Chaar alag laagaten aap par padti hain:

  • Jaane ka kiraya — paarsal nikalte hi kooriyar apna kiraya kama chuka.
  • Lautne ka kiraya — aksar utna hi, kabhi-kabhi zyada.
  • Paikejing — dibba, tep, bharaav aur lebal sab khatm.
  • Haindaling — koi kholata hai, jaanchata hai, dobaara paik karta hai, aur stok kuch din blok rehta hai.

Apni shipament par ye chaaron jodie aur ek aankda milega: prati RTO order nuksan. Yahi woh sankhya hai jo RTO dar ko paise mein badalate hai.

Ek udaharan: 100 COD order

Maan lijiye ek mahine mein 100 COD order hain, ausat order ₹1,000 ka, product lagat ₹500, jaane ka kiraya ₹80, aur prati RTO nuksan ₹250 (₹80 jaane ka, ₹80 lautne ka, ₹40 paikejing, ₹50 dobaara paik karne ka).

  1. 20% RTO dar → 20 paarsal laute.
  2. RTO nuksan: 20 × ₹250 = ₹5,000.
  3. Pahunche order par bachat: ₹1,000 − ₹500 − ₹80 = ₹420.
  4. 80 pahunche order × ₹420 = 33,600 ki kamai.
  5. RTO nuksan ghataane par: 33,600 − ₹5,000 = 28,600 bache.

Paanch hazaar rupaye yani kul bachat ka lagabhag saatavaan hissa, un paarsalon par gaya jinhonne ek rupaya bhi nahi kamaaya. Wahi selar 10% RTO dar par 28,600 ki jagah 35,300 bachaata hai — yeh dar zyadatar pricing faisalon se zyada maayne rakhate hai.

Pahunche order ke munafe par RTO ka asar

Man karta hai ki ₹5,000 ko oopare kharch maanakar aage badh jayein. Par woh unheen ordaron ki vajah se hua jo nikale the, to use prati-order munafe mein hi ginana chahiye.

₹5,000 ko un 80 ordaron par baanten jo pahunche, to lagabhag ₹63 prati order aata hai. Aapka sachcha munafa ₹420 − ₹63 = ₹357 hai, ₹420 nahi. Jo bhi vigyapan kharch, chhoot ya COD fees aap manzoor karein, use ₹357 ke andar hi rehna hoga, varana mahina ghaate mein jaaega, chaahe har bikri alag se faayademand dikhe.

Iseelie koi dukaan bhare-poore chalti hai, theek markup par bikate hai, aur phir bhi mahine ke aakhir mein nakade kam pad jaati hai.

Preeped ka sauda, aankdon mein

Seedha hal hai graahak ko pehle bhugataan ki taraf le jaana. Yeh hal chhoot bhi maangta hai, to laagoo karne se pehle hisaab lagaaie.

Maan lijiye 100 mein se 20 order ₹50 ki chhoot par preeped ho jaate hain aur isase 20 mein se 10 RTO hat jaate hain:

  1. Dee gayi chhoot: 20 × ₹50 = ₹1,000.
  2. Bacha RTO nuksan: 10 × ₹250 = ₹2,500.
  3. Shuddh fayda: ₹2,500 − ₹1,000 = ₹1,500.

Yeh sauda sirf isliye banata hai ki prati RTO nuksan chhoot se paanch guna hai. Agar prati RTO nuksan ₹60 hota, to yahi offer bachaane se zyada mahanga padta. Kuch bhi ghoshit karne se pehle apne aankdon par hisaab lagaaie.

Dar ghataane ke chaar thikaane

  • Pate ki gunavatta. Laindamaark aur woh fon number maangie jis par raaidar sach mein pahunch sake. Zyadatar asaphal dileevare pata galat hone se hoti hai, mana karne se nahi.
  • COD ki seema. Order vailyoo aur pinakod ke hisaab se COD seemit keejie. Oonche vailyoo ke paarsal aur kamzor sarvisebilite wale pinakod mein hi nuksan jama hota hai.
  • Nikalane se pehle pushti. COD order par WhatsApp ya kol se pushti kiraya kharch hone se pehle farze aur bhool gaye order chhaan dete hai.
  • Sachche dileevare taareekh. Jise paanch din bataya aur baarah lage, woh raaidar ke fon aane tak kaheen aur khareed chuka hota hai.

Har mahine kya dekhein

Teen aankde, bas: COD order, RTO dar, aur prati RTO nuksan. Pehle do ko guna karein to laute paarsalon ki sankhya milege; use teesare se guna karein to gaya hua paisa.

10% se kam dar aam taur par theek maane jaati hai, haalaanki yeh shrene ke hisaab se bahut badalate hai — faishan mein grosare se kaheen zyada lautata hai. Benchamaark se zyada rujhaan maayne rakhta hai. Dar do mahinon mein 12% se 18% ho jaye, to woh pricing mein hua woh badlav hai jo aapne jaan-boojhakar nahi kiya.

Jab yeh nuksan aapke prati-order munafe mein baith jaata hai, tab RTO kismat ki maar nahi rehta — woh lagat ban jaata hai jise aap tay kar sakte hain, seemit kar sakte hain aur ghata sakte hain.
