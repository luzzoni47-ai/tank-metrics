Refund Policy

This policy explains when a refund is available for the Indian Business Financial Model and how to ask for one. It applies to that digital product only. The free calculators and articles on Tank Metrics are free of charge, so no refund arises for them.

1. The seven-day window

You may request a full refund within seven days of your purchase date. The date of purchase is the date shown on your payment receipt. A request made after the seventh day has passed is normally not eligible, because by then the files have been delivered and cannot be recalled.

2. How to request a refund

Write to hello@tankmetrics.in with the subject “Refund request”, and include:

  • the email address you used at checkout;
  • your order number or payment reference;
  • the date of purchase; and
  • a short note about why it did not suit you — optional, but it genuinely helps improve the product.

You will get a reply within two working days.

3. How the refund is paid

Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method. Once we process the refund, the money is out of our hands; the time it takes to appear is set by your bank or payment provider, and typically runs from five to ten working days.

4. What happens to your licence

Every copy of the workbook and the manual is delivered through a personal licence key, and every copy is stamped with the buyer’s details. When a refund is approved, that licence is revoked. The next time the key is used, it stops working and shows “This licence is no longer active”. This is why a refund genuinely ends access: there is no public download link that could keep working after the refund.

5. What is not covered

  • Requests made more than seven days after purchase.
  • Requests where the licence key has been shared, resold or published, since that copy cannot be recalled.
  • Requests that ask to keep the files while also receiving the money back.
  • Complaints about a figure not matching your own accounts, where the model has been used with assumptions you chose. The model is a planning and learning aid, not a filing tool, and it is expected to differ from your audited numbers.

6. Faulty or missing files

If a file will not open, is corrupted, or never arrived, that is a different problem and the seven-day window does not apply. Write to the same address and the file will be re-sent or the purchase refunded, whichever you prefer.

7. Your statutory rights

Nothing in this policy limits any right you have under the Consumer Protection Act, 2019 or any other law that applies to you. Where the law gives you a stronger right than this policy, the law prevails.

8. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated to reflect changes in the product or the law. The “Last updated” date above always reflects the current version, and the version in force is the one published when you bought.

9. Contact us

For anything about a refund, or if any part of this page is unclear, write to hello@tankmetrics.in or use our Contact Us page.
