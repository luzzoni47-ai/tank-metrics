The problem

Most financial models aren’t built for your business

Generic templates assume one kind of company. A factory, a distributor, a consultancy and a SaaS product earn money in completely different ways, so a model built for software quietly gives the other four the wrong answer.

One model, five revenue drivers

A single dropdown changes how revenue is built. The sheet then asks for the numbers that actually drive your business:

  • Manufacturing — units, production cost and capacity
  • Trading — inventory, stock turnover and receivables
  • Services — billable hours and utilisation
  • Agencies — retainers, clients and delivery cost
  • SaaS — subscriptions, MRR, churn and ARR

Change an assumption, see the consequence

Every figure traces back to the Assumptions sheet. Move your growth rate, your price, your debtor days or your salary bill, and the P&L, the balance sheet, the cash flow and the valuation all move together — which is how you find out whether a decision is affordable before you make it.

The model also refuses to let you fool yourself: three automatic checks test the balance sheet, the cash balance and the debt schedule in all 60 months, and a red flag tells you which input to fix.

Questions

Straight answers

Does the model work in Google Sheets?
The file is .xlsx, and Google Sheets opens it with the formulas intact. A few formatting details shift, and very large workbooks can feel slow. For the full experience use desktop Excel, LibreOffice Calc or WPS Office. Whichever you choose, open the Checks sheet first and confirm every month still reads OK before you rely on the numbers — every spreadsheet application evaluates formulas slightly differently.
Do I need accounting knowledge?
No. The manual starts from the basics and explains each term the first time it is used. If you can read a bank statement and a GST invoice, you can use this model. Where a concept needs more depth, the relevant chapter is linked from the sheet.
Is it useful for a CA, or only for a business owner?
Both. It is built for owners who want to understand their own numbers, and accountants find it a useful starting structure to work from. It is a planning and decision tool — it does not file returns, and it does not replace your CA.
Which business types are supported?
Five: manufacturing, trading and distribution, services, agency and freelancers, and SaaS or subscription businesses. Each one changes the revenue driver in the model — units for manufacturing and trading, billable hours for services and agency, customers for SaaS.
How do I receive the product?
After your payment is confirmed you receive a licence key by email. Enter that key in the box on this page and the workbook and manual unlock for you. There is no public download link, which is what lets a refund genuinely cut off access.
What does the refund policy cover?
A seven-day refund window from the date of purchase, subject to the published terms. Write to hello@tankmetrics.in with your order number and the refund is processed on the same key that delivered your files.
Is this a tax filing tool?
No. It is a financial planning and educational aid. It helps you model GST, TDS, payroll cost, depreciation and tax as planning assumptions. It does not compute your statutory liability, file a return or substitute for professional advice. Confirm applicable rates with a qualified professional.
Are updates included?
Yes. When a statutory rate that the model uses changes — GST, income tax, PF or ESI limits — the workbook is revised and every buyer gets the updated file free. You keep lifetime access to the product you bought.
