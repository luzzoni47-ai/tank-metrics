What this is

Most financial models are built for software companies. This one is built for Indian businesses. Pick your business type from a dropdown and the model changes its own revenue driver — units for manufacturing and trading, billable hours for services and agency, customers for SaaS.

Questions

Does it work on Google Sheets?

The file is .xlsx. Google Sheets opens it, and the formulas work, but a few formatting details shift. For the full experience use Excel, LibreOffice Calc or WPS.

Do I need to know accounting?

No. The manual starts from the basics and explains each term. If you can read a bank statement, you can use this.

Is this for a CA or for me?

It is for you, and your CA will find it useful. It is a planning and decision tool, not a filing tool. Confirm rates with your CA before you file.
