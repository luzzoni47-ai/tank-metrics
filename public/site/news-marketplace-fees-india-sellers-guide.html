Why an ₹800 Sale on a Marketplace Often Pays You Only ₹580

Commission, shipping and closing fees quietly take a quarter of the selling price. Here is the arithmetic, and how to price so the fees do not eat your margin.

Small BusinessOctober 21, 2026

You list an item at ₹800. The order comes in. You pack it, ship it, and a week later the settlement lands. It is not ₹800. It is rarely even close.

This is not a scam. It is how marketplaces make money, and it is entirely knowable in advance. Most sellers just never add it up before setting the price.

Where the ₹220 goes

A typical order carries three or four separate charges. Commission is usually the largest, charged as a percentage of the selling price and varying by category — often higher for fashion and lower for electronics.

Shipping is charged on weight slabs, and the slab matters more than the actual weight. A 480-gram parcel and a 510-gram parcel can land in different slabs and different fees.

Closing or fixed fees apply per order regardless of size, which is why small-ticket items get punished hardest. A ₹200 order with a ₹25 fixed fee has already lost 12.5% before commission.

Payment gateway charges and, on cash-on-delivery orders, a COD handling fee round it out.

The number that actually matters

Not the fee. The effective payout percentage. If you keep ₹580 of an ₹800 sale, your payout ratio is 72.5%. Now compare that to your product cost.

Suppose the item costs you ₹420 to make or buy. You keep ₹580. Gross profit is ₹160, or 20% of the selling price. A single return wipes out three or four profitable orders.

How to price so fees do not eat you

Work backwards from your cost. Decide the margin you need, then gross up for the fee percentage rather than adding fees on top of a price you already chose.

Push order value up. Fixed fees punish small baskets. Bundling two items into one order spreads the fixed fee across more revenue and can lift your payout ratio several points.

Watch the weight slab. If your parcel is 510 grams and the next slab starts at 500, shaving packaging can drop you a slab and save on every single order.

Track your RTO rate. A returned cash-on-delivery order can cost you the forward shipping, the return shipping and the lost sale. Sellers with a high RTO rate often find their real margin is far below what the dashboard shows.

None of this needs software. It needs you to know your payout ratio before you set a price, not after the settlement report arrives.

Do this before your next listing

Open a spreadsheet, or the calculator above, and write down the payout ratio for your five best-selling items. Most sellers find one or two are quietly losing money at the current price, and a small correction fixes it before the losses pile up.

Aap ek item ₹800 mein list karte ho. Order aata hai. Aap pack karte ho, ship karte ho, aur ek hafte baad settlement aata hai. Woh ₹800 nahi hai. Zyadatar paas bhi nahi hai.

Ye scam nahi hai. Marketplaces aise hi paisa kamate hain, aur ye pehle se poori tarah pata kiya ja sakta hai. Zyadatar sellers bas price set karne se pehle ise jodte hi nahi.

Woh ₹220 kahan jaate hain

Ek aam order mein teen ya chaar alag charges hote hain. Commission aam taur par sabse bada hota hai, selling price ke percentage par, aur category ke hisaab se badalta hai — fashion par aksar zyada, electronics par kam.

Shipping weight slabs par lagti hai, aur slab asli weight se zyada matter karti hai. 480 gram ka parcel aur 510 gram ka parcel alag slab aur alag fee mein ja sakte hain.

Closing ya fixed fees har order par lagti hain, size chahe kuch bhi ho, isliye chhote ticket wale items sabse zyada pit-te hain. ₹200 ke order par ₹25 ki fixed fee commission se pehle hi 12.5% kha jaati hai.

Payment gateway charges aur cash-on-delivery orders par COD handling fee ise poora karte hain.

Asli number jo matter karta hai

Fee nahi. Effective payout percentage. Agar ₹800 ki sale par aapke paas ₹580 aata hai, to payout ratio 72.5% hai. Ab ise apni product cost se compare karo.

Maano item banane ya kharidne mein ₹420 lagta hai. Aapke paas ₹580 aata hai. Gross profit ₹160, yaani selling price ka 20%. Ek return teen-char profitable orders kha jaata hai.

Price kaise rakho ki fees kha na jaayein

Apni cost se peeche se chalo. Decide karo kitna margin chahiye, phir fee percentage ke liye gross up karo, na ki apne chune hue price par fees jodo.

Order value badhao. Fixed fees chhoti basket ko saza deti hain. Do items ek order mein bundle karne se fixed fee zyada revenue par bant jaati hai aur payout ratio kuch points badh sakta hai.

Weight slab dekho. Agar parcel 510 gram hai aur agli slab 500 se shuru hoti hai, to packaging thodi kam karne se aap ek slab neeche aa sakte ho aur har order par bacha sakte ho.

Apna RTO rate track karo. Return hua cash-on-delivery order forward shipping, return shipping aur khoi hui sale — teeno ka kharcha de sakta hai. Jinka RTO rate zyada hai unka asli margin aksar dashboard se kaafi neeche hota hai.

Ismein koi software nahi chahiye. Sirf ye chahiye ki price set karne se pehle apna payout ratio pata ho, settlement report aane ke baad nahi.

Agli listing se pehle ye karo

Ek spreadsheet kholo, ya upar wala calculator, aur apne paanch sabse zyada bikne wale items ka payout ratio likho. Zyadatar sellers ko pata chalta hai ki ek-do items chupke se current price par paisa kha rahe hain, aur thoda correction nuksaan badhne se pehle ise theek kar deta hai.
