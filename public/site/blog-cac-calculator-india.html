Frequently asked questions

How do I calculate CAC?

Divide total sales and marketing spend by the number of new customers won in the same period. ₹40 lakh of spend and 2,000 customers gives a CAC of ₹2,000.

What is a good LTV to CAC ratio?

Three or higher is the usual benchmark. Below 3 the growth is fragile, and below 1 the business loses money on every new customer.

Should founder salary be included in CAC?

Yes. If the founder spends time selling, that time has a cost and should be counted, or the CAC will look better than it really is.

What is customer acquisition cost calculator india?

A customer acquisition cost calculator India teams use answers the most uncomfortable question in marketing, what did it cost to win one customer? You take everything spent on sales and marketing in a period, which means ad budgets, salaries, agency fees and discount coupons, and divide it by the number of new customers won in that same period. If you spent ₹40 lakh and gained 2,000 customers, your CAC is ₹2,000. That single figure decides whether a business can scale or only grow its losses.

CAC only means something next to lifetime value, or LTV, which is the money an average customer brings in before they leave. The rule of thumb is an LTV to CAC ratio of at least 3. Below 3 the growth is fragile, and below 1 every new customer loses money. The calculator shows your ratio and also the highest CAC you could pay and still hold a 3x ratio, so you know your ceiling before you sign another ad contract.

Everything here is in ₹, the way Indian founders watch their Meta and Google bills. The tool ignores vanity metrics such as impressions and clicks and works only with money in and customers out. A founder who tracks CAC every month spots a rising cost long before it shows up in the bank account, and that early warning is often the difference between a business that scales and one that quietly runs out of cash.

Why Indian Calculation is Different (US vs India)

Indian digital marketing runs on far smaller budgets than a US template assumes, which makes CAC brutally sensitive to every rupee. A campaign that works in the United States at a high price point may be impossible in India at an Indian average order value, so the same tactic cannot simply be copied.

Indian founders also blur the line between sales and marketing. In many small businesses the founder is the sales team, and that salary is never counted in CAC. A true Indian CAC should include the founder's time, agency retainers and the free samples handed out at exhibitions, or the number stays too optimistic.

India's cash on delivery model adds a hidden cost. Returns and failed deliveries can wipe out the margin on a customer who looked profitable on paper, so a careful founder builds a return rate into the LTV before trusting the ratio, and watches the CAC separately for each channel rather than as one blended average.

Channel mix also matters. A brand that wins cheap customers through referrals and expensive ones through paid ads has a blended CAC that hides both stories. Splitting the number by channel shows which spend is working, and that is the only way to decide where the next rupee of marketing should go.

Common Mistakes

  • Counting only ad spend and leaving out agency fees and salaries. The true CAC is higher than the ad dashboard suggests.
  • Comparing CAC to revenue instead of LTV. A ₹2,000 CAC is fine only if lifetime value is well above it.
  • Ignoring returns and failed deliveries. They lower the real LTV and can turn a profitable-looking customer into a loss.
