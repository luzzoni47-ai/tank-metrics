Profit & Margins (8 calculators)

  • Gross Profit & Margin

    See how much money is left after covering the direct cost of what you sold.

  • EBITDA & Margin

    Your core operating profit, before interest, tax, depreciation and amortization.

  • Operating Margin

    How much of every rupee of sales turns into profit from your core business.

  • Net Profit Margin

    What actually lands in your pocket after every single expense and tax.

  • Contribution Margin

    How much each unit you sell contributes towards covering your fixed costs.

  • Markup vs Margin

    Two numbers people often mix up — get both instantly from cost and selling price.

  • Return on Investment (ROI)

    How much profit you made compared to the money you put in.

  • Return on Capital Employed

    How efficiently a business is using all the money invested in it (equity plus debt).

Customers & Unit Economics (9 calculators)

  • Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)

    How much you spend on marketing and sales to win one new customer.

  • Customer Lifetime Value (LTV)

    The total profit one customer is expected to bring over their whole relationship with you.

  • LTV : CAC Ratio

    One of the first things investors check — is your business actually sustainable?

  • CAC Payback Period

    How many months it takes to earn back what you spent acquiring a customer.

  • Average Order Value (AOV)

    The typical amount a customer spends in one order.

  • Repeat Rate, Retention & Churn

    How many past customers keep coming back, and how many drop off.

  • Return on Ad Spend (ROAS)

    For every rupee spent on ads, how much revenue came back.

  • CPC, CPL, CPA & Conversion Rate

    See your cost at every step of the marketing funnel — click, lead, and sale.

  • ARPU, MRR & ARR

    For subscription or app businesses — your monthly and yearly recurring revenue.

Growth & Cash (8 calculators)

  • Run Rate

    If last month's sales repeated all year, this is the yearly total you'd get.

  • Month-on-Month / Year-on-Year Growth

    How much something has grown compared to the previous period.

  • CAGR (Compound Annual Growth Rate)

    The smoothed-out average yearly growth over several years, ignoring ups and downs.

  • Burn Rate & Runway

    How fast you're spending cash each month, and how long your current cash will last.

  • Break-Even Point

    How many units or how much revenue you need before losses turn into profit.

  • Working Capital

    How much cash you actually have available to run day-to-day operations.

  • Cash Conversion Cycle

    How many days it takes from buying stock to getting the cash back in hand.

  • Straight-Line Depreciation

    How much value an asset loses each year, spread evenly over its useful life.
