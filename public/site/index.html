All 100 free business & money calculators

Tank Metrics has 100 instant calculators across 13 categories — startup valuation, EBITDA and margins, LTV:CAC and unit economics, burn rate and runway, EMI and loans, pricing, tax, real estate and everyday money. Every tool runs in your browser: no sign-up, no data leaves your device.

Everyday Money Basics (7 calculators)

  • Percentage Calculator

    Find out what a percentage of a number actually is — the tool everything else is built on.

  • Simple Interest

    The plain, no-compounding way interest is calculated — used for many short loans.

  • Compound Interest

    Interest that earns interest on itself over time — how savings and loans really grow.

  • Loan EMI Calculator

    Work out the fixed monthly payment for a loan, based on amount, rate and duration.

  • Savings Goal Planner

    Find out how many months it will take to reach a savings target.

  • Salary Take-Home Estimator

    A quick, simplified guess of your monthly in-hand pay after typical deductions.

  • Number to Words (Indian)

    Turn a number into words using the Indian Lakh / Crore system — handy for cheques and forms.

Profit & Margins (8 calculators)

  • Gross Profit & Margin

    See how much money is left after covering the direct cost of what you sold.

  • EBITDA & Margin

    Your core operating profit, before interest, tax, depreciation and amortization.

  • Operating Margin

    How much of every rupee of sales turns into profit from your core business.

  • Net Profit Margin

    What actually lands in your pocket after every single expense and tax.

  • Contribution Margin

    How much each unit you sell contributes towards covering your fixed costs.

  • Markup vs Margin

    Two numbers people often mix up — get both instantly from cost and selling price.

  • Return on Investment (ROI)

    How much profit you made compared to the money you put in.

  • Return on Capital Employed

    How efficiently a business is using all the money invested in it (equity plus debt).

Valuation & Deals (11 calculators)

  • Implied Valuation

    When someone says "50 lakh for 5%", find out what that says the whole company is worth.

  • Pre-Money & Post-Money Valuation

    See what the company was worth before the deal, and after new money comes in.

  • Revenue Multiple

    See how many times the yearly revenue a company is being valued at.

  • EBITDA Multiple

    See how many times the EBITDA a company is being valued at.

  • Dilution Calculator

    Find out how much ownership a founder keeps after giving away equity in a deal.

  • Ask Calculator

    If you know how much money you need, see how much equity that means at your valuation.

  • Debt + Equity Deal

    Split a deal that is part loan, part equity — very common on Shark Tank India.

  • Royalty Deal

    Instead of equity, an investor takes a % of revenue — see how many years to pay them back.

  • Cap Table & ESOP Pool

    After setting aside an employee option pool and investor equity, see what founders keep.

  • Co-founder Equity Split

    Split equity fairly between co-founders based on what each one is putting in.

  • Debt Service Coverage Ratio (DSCR)

    Check whether a business earns enough to cover its loan repayments. Lenders ask for this number before they sanction a term loan.

Customers & Unit Economics (9 calculators)

  • Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)

    How much you spend on marketing and sales to win one new customer.

  • Customer Lifetime Value (LTV)

    The total profit one customer is expected to bring over their whole relationship with you.

  • LTV : CAC Ratio

    One of the first things investors check — is your business actually sustainable?

  • CAC Payback Period

    How many months it takes to earn back what you spent acquiring a customer.

  • Average Order Value (AOV)

    The typical amount a customer spends in one order.

  • Repeat Rate, Retention & Churn

    How many past customers keep coming back, and how many drop off.

  • Return on Ad Spend (ROAS)

    For every rupee spent on ads, how much revenue came back.

  • CPC, CPL, CPA & Conversion Rate

    See your cost at every step of the marketing funnel — click, lead, and sale.

  • ARPU, MRR & ARR

    For subscription or app businesses — your monthly and yearly recurring revenue.

Growth & Cash (10 calculators)

  • Run Rate

    If last month's sales repeated all year, this is the yearly total you'd get.

  • Month-on-Month / Year-on-Year Growth

    How much something has grown compared to the previous period.

  • CAGR (Compound Annual Growth Rate)

    The smoothed-out average yearly growth over several years, ignoring ups and downs.

  • Burn Rate & Runway

    How fast you're spending cash each month, and how long your current cash will last.

  • Break-Even Point

    How many units or how much revenue you need before losses turn into profit.

  • Working Capital

    How much cash you actually have available to run day-to-day operations.

  • Cash Conversion Cycle

    How many days it takes from buying stock to getting the cash back in hand.

  • Straight-Line Depreciation

    How much value an asset loses each year, spread evenly over its useful life.

  • Market Share Calculator

    Two plain numbers in — how much of the market is sold, and how much of it is yours. Out comes your share, simple.

  • Inventory Turnover Ratio

    See how many times a year you sell through your stock. A low number means cash is sitting on the shelf instead of working for the business.
