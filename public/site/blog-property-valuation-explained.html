Property Valuation: Area × Rate Explained

Carpet area times the going rate, and the things that move that rate.

Real EstateNovember 20, 2026

₹48 lakh for a flat you have not measured is a guess. Property valuation is the arithmetic that turns a guess into a number you can defend: the carpet area, the going rate per square foot, and the handful of things that add to or cut that rate.

This guide shows the one formula banks, brokers and registered valuers all start from, works a flat through it, and explains why the figure your bank will lend against is usually lower than the price the seller is asking.

What property valuation actually answers

A property is worth what a willing buyer will pay a willing seller, with neither side forced. Nobody can calculate that exactly, so every valuation is an estimate built from evidence: recent sales of similar flats, the area, the condition and the location.

For a first-time buyer or a small owner, the practical question is narrower. Is the asking price in line with what similar flats nearby actually sold for? Area times rate answers that in one line, and it is the same line a bank's valuer starts from.

The formula: area times rate

Start with two numbers:

  • Area in square feet, measured as carpet area, the space inside your own walls.
  • Rate per square foot, taken from what similar flats in the same building or street recently sold for.

Multiply the two and you have a working value:

  • Estimated value = carpet area × rate per sq ft

Two rules keep it honest. Take the rate from completed sales, not from asking prices, which are wishes. And use the same area the rate is quoted on: mixing carpet area with a built-up rate understates the flat by a fifth.

A worked example: 800 sq ft at ₹6,000

Suppose a two-bedroom flat has 800 sq ft of carpet area, and three flats in the same building sold recently at about ₹6,000 per sq ft.

  • Carpet area: 800 sq ft
  • Rate: ₹6,000 per sq ft
  • Estimated value: 800 × ₹6,000 = ₹48 lakh
  • Stamp duty and registration at 6%: about ₹3 lakh
  • All-in cost: about ₹51 lakh

The seller is asking ₹48 lakh, so the price is in line. If the seller asks ₹60 lakh instead, the extra ₹12 lakh is not being paid for area or for rate. It is a premium that has to be justified some other way.

Carpet, built-up and super built-up

The same flat has three different areas, and the price looks different depending on which one the rate is quoted on.

  • Carpet area: inside your walls, the floor you can actually use.
  • Built-up area: carpet plus the inner walls, typically a tenth more.
  • Super built-up area: built-up plus your share of the lobby, stairs and lift, typically a quarter more than carpet.

Take that same flat of 800 sq ft carpet, now quoted as 1,000 sq ft super built-up at ₹4,800 per sq ft. The answer is still ₹48 lakh. The area changed, the rate changed, the value did not. A rate per square foot means nothing until you know which area it is measured on.

What moves the rate up or down

  • Location: the same flat two streets away can differ by a third.
  • Age and condition: a building past twenty years sells at a discount for the repairs ahead.
  • Floor and facing: a higher floor with light and an open view carries a premium.
  • Approvals: an unapproved layout or a missing occupancy certificate cuts the value and blocks a home loan.

None of these sit in the formula. They are the reason two flats with the same carpet area do not sell for the same money, and the reason you adjust the rate rather than the area.

Why the bank's number is lower

A bank values the flat for what it could recover if the loan failed, not for what you would happily pay. That produces a lower figure for three reasons: it takes the lower of the price you paid and its own valuer's rate, it discounts an old building more sharply, and it ignores furniture, fittings and any premium for a corner unit.

The gap matters because the loan is a share of the bank's figure, not of the price. If the bank values the flat at ₹44 lakh and lends 80% of that, the loan is about ₹35 lakh, and you have to find the other ₹13 lakh yourself.

The short version

Property value starts as carpet area times the going rate per square foot: 800 sq ft at ₹6,000 is ₹48 lakh. Check which area the rate is quoted on, adjust the rate for age, floor and approvals, and expect the bank to value it lower than you did. That lower figure is the one your loan is really based on.

Property valuation asal mein kya bataate hai

Property ki keemat wahi hai jo ek taiyar khareedaar taiyar vikreta ko de, koi bhi majaboor na ho. Ise koi theek-theek hisaab nahi kar sakta, isliye har valuation ek andaza hai jo saboot par tika hota hai: aasapaas ki haal ki bikri, area, haalat aur location.

Pehli baar phlait khareedane wale ya chhote maalik ke liye sawal chhota hai. Kya maanga ja raha daam usi jaise phlaiton ki haal ki bikri se mel khaata hai? Area guna rate isaka jawab ek laain mein deta hai, aur bank ka valuer bhi usi laain se shuru karta hai.

Formula: area guna rate

Do aankdon se shuru karein:

  • Area varg phut mein, carpet area ke taur par naapa gaya, yani aapki apni deevaaron ke andar ki jagah.
  • Rate prati sq ft, usi imaarat ya gale ke milate-julate phlaiton ki haal ki bikri se liya gaya.

Dono ko guna karein to ek chalane layak keemat milate hai:

  • Estimated value = carpet area × rate per sq ft

Do niyam ise imandar rakhte hain. Rate poore huee bikri se len, maange ja rahe daam se nahi, kyunki ve ichchhaaen hain. Aur wahi area len jis par rate bataya gaya hai: carpet area ko built-up rate se milaane par phlait ki keemat paanchavaan hissa kam aate hai.

Ek udaharan: 800 sq ft par ₹6,000

Maan lijiye do bedaroom ke phlait ka carpet area 800 sq ft hai, aur usi imaarat ke teen phlait haal mein kareeb ₹6,000 prati sq ft par bike.

  • Carpet area: 800 sq ft
  • Rate: ₹6,000 prati sq ft
  • Estimated value: 800 × ₹6,000 = ₹48 laakh
  • 6% stamp duty aur registration: kareeb ₹3 laakh
  • Kul lagat: kareeb ₹51 laakh

Vikreta ₹48 laakh maang raha hai, to daam theek hai. Agar woh ₹60 laakh maange, to atirikt ₹12 laakh area ya rate ke liye nahi diya ja raha. Woh premium hai jise kisi aur tarike se sahe thaharaana padega.

Carpet, built-up aur super built-up

Ek hi phlait ke teen alag area hote hain, aur rate kis par bataya gaya hai usake hisaab se daam alag dikhta hai.

  • Carpet area: aapki deevaaron ke andar, woh pharsh jo aap sach mein istemal karte hain.
  • Built-up area: carpet aur andar ki deevaaren, aam taur par dasavaan hissa zyada.
  • Super built-up area: built-up aur lobby, seedhi tatha lift mein aapka hissa, aam taur par carpet se ek chauthai zyada.

Wahi 800 sq ft carpet wala phlait ab 1,000 sq ft super built-up par ₹4,800 prati sq ft bataya jaye, to jawab phir ₹48 laakh hai. Area badala, rate badala, keemat nahi badale. Prati sq ft rate ka koi matlab nahi jab tak pata na ho ki woh kis area par naapa gaya hai.

Rate ko kya upar ya neeche karta hai

  • Location: do gale door wahi phlait ek tihaaee alag ho sakta hai.
  • Umr aur haalat: bees saal se puraane imaarat aane wali marammat ke liye chhoot par bikate hai.
  • Manzil aur disha: roshane aur khula nazaara wali oopare manzil ka premium hota hai.
  • Approvals: bina approval ka layout ya occupancy certificate ki kame keemat ghataate hai aur home loan rok dete hai.

Inmein se koi formula mein nahi baithata. Yahi kaaran hai ki ek hi carpet area wale do phlait ek daam par nahi bikate, aur yahi kaaran hai ki aap area nahi, rate theek karte hain.

Bank ka aankda kam kyon hota hai

Bank phlait ki woh keemat lagaata hai jo loan phel hone par woh vasool sake, woh nahi jo aap khushe se dein. Isase aankda teen kaaranon se kam aata hai: woh aapke chukaae daam aur apne valuer ke rate mein se kam leta hai, puraane imaarat par zyada chhoot deta hai, aur furniture, fittings tatha corner unit ke premium ko chhod deta hai.

Yeh farq maayne rakhta hai kyunki loan daam ka hissa nahi, bank ke aankde ka hissa hota hai. Agar bank phlait ki keemat ₹44 laakh lagaae aur uska 80% de, to loan kareeb ₹35 laakh hai, aur baaki ₹13 laakh aapko khud jutaane honge.

Chhoti baat

Property ki keemat carpet area guna chalan ka rate prati sq ft se shuru hoti hai: 800 sq ft par ₹6,000 yani ₹48 laakh. Dekhein ki rate kis area par bataya gaya hai, umr, manzil aur approvals ke liye rate theek karein, aur maan len ki bank aapase kam lagaaega. Wahi kam aankda hai jis par aapka loan sach mein tika hota hai.
