Landed Cost Meaning: What You Really Pay

Freight, insurance and duty all land on top of the invoice — here is what your goods really cost per unit.

If you import goods to resell in India, the price on the supplier's invoice is not what the goods cost you. Freight, insurance and customs duty all land on top before the carton reaches your shelf. The number you actually paid is the landed cost, and pricing from the invoice instead of the landed cost is one of the most common ways a small importer sells at a loss without noticing.

This guide explains what goes into a landed cost, works through one small order step by step, and shows where the hidden costs hide in a marketplace or a wholesale deal.

What landed cost means

Landed cost is the total you pay to get a unit onto your shelf, ready to sell. It has four parts:

  • Product cost — what the supplier charges for the goods.
  • Freight — what it costs to move them to you.
  • Insurance — the cover on the goods while they travel.
  • Customs duty — the government's charge for bringing them into India.

Add those four and divide by the number of units, and you have your cost per unit. Everything you decide about price comes after this number, not before it.

A worked example

Say you buy 50 phone cases from a supplier abroad for ₹4,000. Freight is ₹300 and insurance is ₹200. Customs duty comes to ₹800. Only the duty figure depends on the product; the point here is how the four parts add up.

  1. Product cost: ₹4,000.
  2. Freight: ₹300.
  3. Insurance: ₹200.
  4. Customs duty: ₹800.
  5. Total landed cost: ₹4,000 + ₹300 + ₹200 + ₹800 = ₹5,300.
  6. Cost per case: ₹5,300 ÷ 50 = ₹106.

Here is the part that catches people out. The invoice said ₹4,000, which is ₹80 a case. The landed cost is ₹106 a case — ₹26 more, or about a third again. If you had priced at ₹95 believing you had a healthy margin, you would be losing ₹11 on every sale and never see it in a spreadsheet that only tracked the invoice.

Why the duty rate is never one number

Customs duty is charged as a percentage of the product value, but the percentage depends on the HSN code of what you are importing. Two items that look identical on a shelf can sit in different categories with very different rates. There is no single "import duty percentage" in India, which is exactly why you should confirm the rate for your own HSN code with a customs broker before you price anything.

What the exchange rate does to landed cost

If you pay in dollars, the rupee rate is another variable, and it does not sit still. A weaker rupee raises the rupee value of the same dollar invoice, and the duty is then charged on that larger figure too. The exchange rate does not just change your product cost — it changes the duty that sits on top of it.

How to price against landed cost

Price against the landed cost, never the invoice. On the ₹5,300 order above, a 40% markup on the landed cost gives a selling price of ₹148 a case (₹106 × 1.4 = ₹148.40, rounded). That is a very different number from the ₹112 you would get from marking up the ₹80 invoice figure by the same 40%.

The habit worth building is simple: work out the landed cost per unit first, write it down, and only then decide the selling price. Every later decision — discounts, marketplace fees, returns — eats into the gap between price and landed cost, not the gap between price and invoice.

Where landed cost hides in a marketplace

If you sell through an online marketplace, the platform's commission and shipping fee come out of the sale price, and they are not part of landed cost. Landed cost is what you paid to get the goods; marketplace fees are what you pay to sell them. Keep the two separate or you will double-count one and forget the other. A ₹9,200 sale that pays ₹2,000 in marketplace fees and carried a landed cost of ₹5,300 leaves ₹1,900 before your own time — not the ₹3,900 the invoice maths suggested.

What to do before you order

Three things, in this order. Get the HSN code for your product and confirm the duty rate with a broker. Ask the supplier for the freight and insurance figures in writing, not a vague estimate. Then compute the landed cost per unit and check it against the price you intend to sell at. If the margin disappears at the landed cost, it will not come back after the order is placed.

Agar aap bhaarat mein dobaara bechane ke liye maal aayaat karte hain, to saplaayar ke bil par likhe keemat aapki asli lagat nahi hai. Kaartan aapki shelph tak pahunchane se pehle us par fret, bima aur kastams dyoote bhi judate hai. Jo rakam aapne vaakaee chukaaee, wahi lainded kost hai, aur bil ke aadhaar par daam tay karna chhote aayaatak ke ghaate mein bikne ka sabse aam kaaran hai.

Yeh guide bataate hai ki lainded kost mein kya-kya aata hai, ek chhote order ka hisaab kadam-dar-kadam karti hai, aur dikhati hai ki maarketaples ya thok saude mein chhipe laagaten kahan chhipate hain.

Lainded kost ka matlab kya hai

Lainded kost woh kul rakam hai jo aap ek yoonit ko bechane ke liye taiyar haalat mein apni shelph tak laane mein chukaate hain. Isamen chaar hisse hote hain:

  • Product kost — saplaayar maal ke liye jo vasoolata hai.
  • Fret — maal aap tak pahunchaane ka kharch.
  • Bima — safar ke dauraan maal par liya gaya kavar.
  • Kastams dyoote — maal bhaarat mein laane par sarakaar ka shulk.

In chaaron ko jodkar yoonit ki sankhya se bhaag deejie, to prati yoonit lagat mil jaati hai. Keemat par har faisala is sankhya ke baad hota hai, pehle nahi.

Ek udaharan

Maan lijiye aap videsh se 50 fon kes ₹4,000 mein khareedate hain. Fret ₹300 aur bima ₹200 hai. Kastams dyoote ₹800 baithate hai. Sirf dyoote ka aankda product par nirbhar hai; yahaan baat yeh hai ki chaaron hisse kaise judate hain.

  1. Product kost: ₹4,000.
  2. Fret: ₹300.
  3. Bima: ₹200.
  4. Kastams dyoote: ₹800.
  5. Kul lainded kost: ₹4,000 + ₹300 + ₹200 + ₹800 = ₹5,300.
  6. Prati kes lagat: ₹5,300 ÷ 50 = ₹106.

Yahin log chakama khaate hain. Bil par ₹4,000 likha tha, yani ₹80 prati kes. Lainded kost ₹106 prati kes hai — ₹26 zyada, yani lagabhag ek-tihaaee adhik. Agar aapne ₹95 ka daam tay kiya yeh sochakar ki margin theek hai, to har bikri par ₹11 ka ghaata ho raha hoga, aur us sheet mein yeh kabhi nahi dikhega jo sirf bil ka hisaab rakhate hai.

Dyoote ki dar kabhi ek sankhya nahi hoti

Kastams dyoote product ki keemat ke pratishat ke roop mein lagti hai, lekin woh pratishat is baat par nirbhar karta hai ki aap kya aayaat kar rahe hain, yani usake HSN kod par. Do cheezein dukaan mein ek jaise dikh sakate hain par alag shreniyon mein aa sakate hain, aur daren bahut alag ho sakate hain. Bhaarat mein "aayaat shulk ka pratishat" naam ki koi ek sankhya nahi hai, isliye daam tay karne se pehle apne HSN kod ki dar kastams brokar se pakke kar len.

Eksachenj ret lainded kost ka kya karta hai

Agar aap dolar mein bhugataan karte hain, to rupaye ki dar ek aur char hai, aur woh sthir nahi rahate. Kamzor rupaya usi dolar bil ka rupaye mein mooly badha deta hai, aur phir dyoote bhi usi bade sankhya par lagti hai. Eksachenj ret sirf product kost nahi badalta — us par baithe dyoote bhi badal deta hai.

Lainded kost par daam kaise tay karein

Daam lainded kost par tay keejie, bil par kabhi nahi. Upar ke ₹5,300 ke order par, lainded kost par 40% maarkaap bikri keemat ₹148 prati kes deta hai (₹106 × 1.4 = ₹148.40, gol karke). Yeh us ₹112 se bahut alag hai jo ₹80 ke bil par wahi 40% jodne se milta.

Aadat seedhe hai: pehle prati yoonit lainded kost nikaalie, likh lijiye, aur tabhe bikri keemat tay keejie. Aage ka har faisala — chhoot, maarketaples fees, vaapasi — keemat aur lainded kost ke beech ke farq par lagta hai, keemat aur bil ke beech ke farq par nahi.

Maarketaples mein lainded kost kahan chhipate hai

Agar aap online maarketaples se bechte hain, to pletaform ka commission aur shiping fees bikri keemat mein se katate hain, aur ve lainded kost ka hissa nahi hain. Lainded kost woh hai jo aapne maal paane ke liye chukaaya; maarketaples fees woh hai jo aapne bechane ke liye chukaaya. Dono ko alag rakhie varana ek ko do baar gin lenge aur doosare ko bhool jayenge. ₹9,200 ki bikri jismein ₹2,000 maarketaples fees jaati hai aur lainded kost ₹5,300 the, aapke apne samay se pehle ₹1,900 chhodti hai — ve ₹3,900 nahi jo bil ke hisaab se dikhte the.

Order dene se pehle kya karein

Teen kaam, isi kram mein. Apne product ka HSN kod lekar dyoote ki dar brokar se pakke keejie. Saplaayar se fret aur bima ke aankde likhit mein maangie, koi anumaan nahi. Phir prati yoonit lainded kost nikaalakar use us keemat se milaaie jis par aap bechna chahte hain. Agar lainded kost par margin gaayab hai, to order dene ke baad woh vaapas nahi aaega.
