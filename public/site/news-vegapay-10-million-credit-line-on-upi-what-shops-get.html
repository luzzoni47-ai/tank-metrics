Credit Line on UPI: What a $10 Mn Round Means for Shops

Vegapay raised $10 Mn to put bank credit lines on UPI QR codes. Here is the arithmetic of what a single credit-paying customer is worth at a shop counter.

Small BusinessNovember 26, 2026

Mumbai-based fintech Vegapay has raised $10 Mn (about ₹92 Cr) in a Series A round co-led by Vertex Ventures Southeast Asia and India and Prosus. Existing investors Elevation Capital and Eximius Ventures also put in money. The company sells software, not loans: it lets a bank switch on a credit line that its customers can spend at any UPI QR code. That single sentence is the reason a shopkeeper should read past the funding headline.

What Credit Line on UPI Actually Is

A normal UPI payment moves money out of a bank account. A Credit Line on UPI payment moves money out of a credit line the bank has already sanctioned to that customer. The customer does not need a new card and does not need to download a second app. They open the UPI app they already use, choose the credit line as the payment source, and scan your QR code exactly as they do today. The bank pays you; the customer owes the bank.

For the shop, nothing changes at the counter. Same QR, same app, same confirmation screen. What changes is who can pay. A customer whose account is empty on the 25th of the month but who has a sanctioned limit can still buy from you.

The Numbers in the Round

Vegapay was founded in 2022 by Gaurav Mittal, Himanshu Agrawal and Puneet Sharma. Its platform is live with YES BANK, Unity Small Finance Bank, Suryoday Small Finance Bank and BOBCARD, and it powers credit programmes for Paytm, BharatPe, Kiwi UPI, Scapia and EbixCash World Money. The company says it holds a majority of India's Credit Line on UPI market as of November 2026.

The round mixes primary money (which goes into the company) with secondary money (which buys shares from existing holders), and more than 40 employees were allowed to sell part of their vested stock options. The new money is earmarked for onboarding more banks, adding secured credit lines backed by fixed deposits, gold and mutual funds, and building out card and forex programmes.

"UPI was India's payments revolution. Credit Line on UPI is the UPI moment for credit, and banks should lead it," said cofounder and chief executive Gaurav Mittal.

What It Means for Your Counter

Take a shop doing ₹2,00,000 of UPI sales a month. Suppose one in five of those customers pays from a credit line instead of a bank balance. That is ₹40,000 a month arriving through a route that did not exist for your shop a few years ago. If even a tenth of that is business you would otherwise have lost to a customer who was short of cash, you have added ₹4,000 of sales a month without spending a rupee on marketing.

Now look at the risk side. You are not lending. The bank sanctions the limit, the bank carries the default, and the bank chases the recovery. Your job is the same as with any UPI payment: hand over the right goods, keep the invoice, and reconcile your settlement report. A refund on a credit-line payment goes back to the customer's credit line, not to a bank account, so your staff should know that before they promise a cash refund at the counter.

The one thing worth checking with your payments provider is the charge. UPI on a bank account and UPI on a credit line are different products, and the pricing on the credit version has been changing as the network matures. Ask for the merchant discount rate on credit-line transactions in writing, in basis points, and compare it with what you pay on a card swipe. A 1% charge on ₹40,000 a month is ₹400 a month, or ₹4,800 a year, which is real money for a single-counter shop.

What to Do This Week

Three small things. First, ask your payments provider whether credit-line payments already work on your QR, and what they cost. Second, brief the counter staff so they do not turn away a customer who says the money is coming from a limit rather than a balance. Third, put the credit-line collections on their own line in your daily sales sheet for one month, so you can see whether they are adding sales or simply replacing cash payments you would have got anyway.

That last point is the whole game. A new payment method only helps a shop if it brings in a sale that would not have happened. Funding headlines tell you the plumbing is being built. Your sales sheet tells you whether the water is reaching your tap.

Credit Line on UPI Asal Mein Kya Hai

Normal UPI payment bank account se paisa nikalta hai. Credit Line on UPI payment us credit line se paisa nikalta hai jo bank ne customer ko pehle se sanction kar rakhi hai. Customer ko naya card nahi chahiye aur na hi doosri app download karni hai. Woh apni purani UPI app kholta hai, payment source mein credit line chunta hai, aur aapka QR aise hi scan karta hai jaise aaj karta hai. Bank aapko paisa deta hai; customer bank ka karzdaar rehta hai.

Dukaan par kuch nahi badalta. Wahi QR, wahi app, wahi confirmation screen. Badalta hai yeh ki kaun pay kar sakta hai. Jis customer ke account mein mahine ki 25 tareekh ko paisa nahi hai, lekin limit sanctioned hai, woh bhi aapse khareed sakta hai.

Round Ke Numbers

Vegapay 2022 mein Gaurav Mittal, Himanshu Agrawal aur Puneet Sharma ne shuru ki thi. Iska platform YES BANK, Unity Small Finance Bank, Suryoday Small Finance Bank aur BOBCARD ke saath live hai, aur yeh Paytm, BharatPe, Kiwi UPI, Scapia aur EbixCash World Money ke credit programmes chalata hai. Company ka kehna hai ki November 2026 tak India ke Credit Line on UPI market ka bada hissa iske paas hai.

Round mein primary paisa (jo company ke andar jaata hai) aur secondary paisa (jo purane shareholders se shares kharidta hai) dono hain, aur 40 se zyada employees ko apne vested stock options ka kuch hissa bechne ka mauka mila. Naya paisa zyada banks jodne, fixed deposit, sona aur mutual fund ke against secured credit line launch karne, aur card aur forex programmes badhane par kharch hoga.

"UPI was India's payments revolution. Credit Line on UPI is the UPI moment for credit, and banks should lead it," cofounder aur chief executive Gaurav Mittal ne kaha.

Aapke Counter Ke Liye Iska Matlab

Maan lijiye ek dukaan mahine mein ₹2,00,000 ki UPI sale karti hai. Farz kijiye in mein se har paanchva customer bank balance ki jagah credit line se pay karta hai. Yeh mahine ke ₹40,000 hain jo aapki dukaan tak ek aise raaste se aa rahe hain jo kuch saal pehle tha hi nahi. Agar iska dasva hissa bhi woh business hai jo aapko warna kho dena padta, to bina marketing kharch ke aapki sale mahine ke ₹4,000 badh gayi.

Ab risk ki taraf dekhiye. Aap loan nahi de rahe. Limit bank sanction karta hai, default bank uthata hai, recovery bank karta hai. Aapka kaam wahi hai jo kisi bhi UPI payment mein hai: sahi maal do, invoice rakho, aur settlement report milao. Credit line se hui payment ka refund customer ki credit line mein waapas jaata hai, bank account mein nahi, isliye counter staff ko yeh pata hona chahiye pehle ki woh counter par cash refund ka waada kare.

Ek cheez apne payments provider se zaroor poochhein: charge kitna hai. Bank account wali UPI aur credit line wali UPI do alag product hain, aur credit version ka pricing network ke badhne ke saath badalta raha hai. Credit line transactions ka merchant discount rate likhit mein, basis points mein maangein, aur card swipe ke charge se compare karein. Mahine ke ₹40,000 par 1% charge mahine ke ₹400 hai, saal ka ₹4,800, jo ek single counter dukaan ke liye asli paisa hai.

Is Hafte Kya Karein

Teen chhoti cheezein. Pehla, apne payments provider se poochhein ki credit line payment aapke QR par chalu hai kya, aur uska charge kya hai. Doosra, counter staff ko bata dein taaki woh us customer ko lautayein na jo kahe ki paisa balance se nahi, limit se aa raha hai. Teesra, ek mahine tak apni roz ki sales sheet mein credit line collections ko alag line par likhein, taaki aap dekh sakein ki yeh nayi sale laa raha hai ya sirf wahi cash payment replace kar raha hai jo aapko waise bhi milta.

Asli baat yahi hai. Naya payment method dukaan ko tabhi fayda deta hai jab woh aisi sale laata hai jo warna hoti hi nahi. Funding headlines batate hain ki plumbing ban rahi hai. Aapki sales sheet batati hai ki paani aapke nal tak pahunch raha hai ya nahi.
