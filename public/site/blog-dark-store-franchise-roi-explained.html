What a Dark Store Franchise Really Earns

One order's margin, the fixed costs that run anyway, and the payback in months.

Small BusinessNovember 26, 2026

A dark store is a small warehouse that exists only to fill quick-commerce orders. No customers walk in, there is nothing to browse, just racks, a cold room and pickers racing a ten-minute delivery promise. Platforms increasingly run these stores through partners, and the pitch is simple: you put up the money, we bring the orders.

The money part is real and mostly gone before the first order arrives. The profit part depends on three numbers you do not control on your own. This page does the arithmetic the way an owner would do it on paper, so you can tell a workable offer from an expensive one.

What a dark store franchise actually is

You are not buying a shop. You are buying the right to run one location for a platform: you lease the space, hire the pickers, stock the racks and take the risk, and the platform sends the orders through its app. In most partner models the platform keeps the customer relationship and the pricing, and you keep a slice of each order.

That split is the whole business. Every rupee of cost you carry is yours alone, and every rupee of margin is shared with the platform on terms you are agreeing to for years.

What it costs to open one

For a 1,200 square foot store in a tier-2 city, a realistic opening bill looks like this:

  • Security deposit, usually six months of rent — ₹6 lakh
  • Racking, shelving, air conditioning and fit-out — ₹10 lakh
  • Cold storage and deep freezers — ₹5 lakh
  • Opening stock, bought before you sell a single unit — ₹8 lakh
  • Onboarding or franchise fee — ₹5 lakh
  • Working capital kept liquid for the first quarter — ₹2 lakh

That is ₹36 lakh. Only the deposit comes back to you, and only at the end. The stock comes back as stock, slowly. Everything else is spent on the day you open.

What a single order earns

Work per order, not per month. On an average order of ₹400, a partner store typically keeps a gross margin of about 15%, which is ₹60. That ₹60 has to cover the picker's time, the packing and the cold-chain electricity — but not the rent, because the rent is fixed.

So ₹60 is not profit. It is the contribution each order makes towards a monthly bill that arrives whether 40 orders come in or 400. This is the same contribution margin that decides any shop, and it is worth running your own figures through the Contribution Margin calculator before you argue about rent.

The fixed costs that run anyway

  • Rent for 1,200 square feet — ₹1 lakh
  • Staff, six pickers and a manager — ₹1.5 lakh
  • Electricity, internet and packing material — ₹0.4 lakh
  • Platform or royalty fee, just under 5% of sales — ₹2.3 lakh

That is ₹5.2 lakh a month. At a contribution of ₹60 an order, the store must ship about 290 orders a day before it earns a single rupee of profit. That is the real entry price, and it is the number to hold against the platform's own order forecast.

Worked example: from orders to payback

Take 400 orders a day, a realistic figure for a store that has found its catchment:

  1. 400 orders a day is 12,000 orders a month.
  2. 12,000 orders at ₹60 each is ₹7.2 lakh of gross margin.
  3. Take away ₹5.2 lakh of fixed costs and the store keeps ₹2 lakh a month.

Against ₹36 lakh to open, that is an 18-month payback. Put the same two numbers into the Franchise / Store ROI calculator and it returns the same answer, because it is investment divided by monthly profit.

Eighteen months is only acceptable if the agreement runs well past it. A three-year term with an 18-month payback gives you 18 months of earning, and every month you spend stuck at 300 orders eats one of them.

Three numbers that quietly break the model

  • Orders per day. At 300 orders instead of 400, gross margin falls to ₹5.4 lakh and the store keeps ₹0.2 lakh a month. On paper the payback stretches to fifteen years — same shop, one forecast that was 25% too optimistic.
  • Margin per order. If discounting and shrinkage pull the margin from ₹60 to ₹48, monthly profit drops to ₹0.56 lakh. A small change per order is multiplied by 12,000 every month.
  • Waste and returns. Fresh and dairy spoil, and damaged packs cannot be sold. Losing 2% of sales that way is ₹1 lakh a month off the profit line, which is half of the store's expected profit.

What to ask before you sign

  • Who bears shrinkage, expiry and damaged stock, the partner or the platform?
  • Is the platform fee a percentage of sales or a fixed monthly charge, and how much notice before it changes?
  • What is the payout cycle, and how much of my own money sits tied up in it at any point?
  • Is there an order guarantee, or an exclusivity radius that protects my catchment from a second store?
  • What happens to the deposit, the fit-out and the stock if the agreement ends early?

The one-page check

Before you sign, write down four numbers: the opening cost, the contribution per order, the fixed cost per month, and the orders per day you would need to cover it. Divide the opening cost by the monthly profit and compare the answer with the length of the agreement. If the payback is longer than two-thirds of the term, the store is working for the platform, not for you.

Then check the whole thing at 75% of the order forecast, because that is the version of the plan you are most likely to live through.

Daark stor ek chhota godaam hota hai jo sirf kvik-komars ke order bharane ke liye chalta hai. Yahaan koi graahak nahi aata, dekhane ke liye kuch nahi hota — sirf raik, ek kold room aur das minat mein dileevare ka vaada pura karne ke liye bhaagate pikar. Pletaform ab ye stor paartanar ke zarie chalate hain, aur pich bahut seedhe hai: paisa aap lagaaie, order hum laaenge.

Paise wala hissa sach hai aur pehla order aane se pehle hi zyadatar kharch ho chuka hota hai. Munafe wala hissa teen aankadon par tika hai jo akele aapke haath mein nahi hain. Yeh pej wahi hisaab kaagaz par karta hai jaise koi maalik karta, taaki aap kaam layak offer aur mahange offer mein farq kar saken.

Daark stor frainchaaize asal mein kya hai

Aap dukaan nahi khareed rahe. Aap ek jagah par pletaform ke liye stor chalaane ka adhikaar le rahe hain: jagah kiraae par lena, pikar rakhana, raik bharana aur jokhim uthaana aapka kaam hai, aur order pletaform apne aip se bhejata hai. Zyadatar paartanar modal mein graahak aur keemat pletaform ke paas rehte hain, aur aapko har order ka ek hissa milta hai.

Yahi bantavaara pura karobar hai. Lagat ka har rupaya akele aapka hai, aur margin ka har rupaya pletaform ke saath bantata hai — un sharton par jo aap saalon ke liye maan rahe hain.

Kholane mein kitna lagta hai

Tiyar-2 shahar mein 1,200 varg phut ke stor ke liye shuruaati bil asal mein aisa dikhta hai:

  • Sikyorite dipozit, aam taur par chhah mahine ka kiraya — ₹6 laakh
  • Raik, shelf, eyar kandeeshaning aur fit-out — ₹10 laakh
  • Kold storej aur deep freezar — ₹5 laakh
  • Shuruaati stok, ek bhi yoonit bechane se pehle khareeda gaya — ₹8 laakh
  • Onabording ya frainchaaize fees — ₹5 laakh
  • Pehli timaahe ke liye taiyar rakha varking kaipital — ₹2 laakh

Kul ₹36 laakh. Isamen se sirf dipozit vaapas aata hai, woh bhi aakhir mein. Stok stok ke roop mein lautata hai, dheere-dheere. Baaki sab khulane ke din kharch ho chuka hota hai.

Ek order se kitna kamaate hain

Hisaab mahine ka nahi, order ka keejie. ₹400 ke ausat order par paartanar stor ko aam taur par kareeb 15% gros margin milta hai, yani ₹60. Us ₹60 mein se pikar ka samay, paiking aur kold-chen ki bijli nikalti hai — lekin kiraya nahi, kyunki kiraya tay hai.

To ₹60 munafa nahi hai. Yeh woh yogadaan hai jo har order us maasik bil ki taraf deta hai jo 40 order aayein ya 400, aata hi hai. Yahi kantreebyooshan margin kisi bhi dukaan ka faisala karta hai, aur kiraae par bahas karne se pehle apne aankade kantreebyooshan margin kailakuletar mein daal lena chahiye.

Ve tay kharch jo phir bhi chalte hain

  • 1,200 varg phut ka kiraya — ₹1 laakh
  • Staff, chhah pikar aur ek mainejar — ₹1.5 laakh
  • Bijli, intaranet aur paiking saamaan — ₹0.4 laakh
  • Pletaform ya royalte fees, bikri ka 5% se kuch kam — ₹2.3 laakh

Kul ₹5.2 laakh mahina. ₹60 ke yogadaan par stor ko munafe ka ek rupaya kamaane se pehle roz kareeb 290 order bhejane padate hain. Yahi asli entre keemat hai, aur yahi aankada pletaform ke order anumaan ke saamane rakhana chahiye.

Udaharan: order se pebaik tak

Roz 400 order lijiye, jo us stor ke liye theek hai jise apna ilaaqa mil gaya ho:

  1. Roz 400 order yani mahine ke 12,000 order.
  2. 12,000 order ₹60 par yani ₹7.2 laakh gros margin.
  3. Usamen se ₹5.2 laakh tay kharch nikaalie, stor ke paas ₹2 laakh mahina bachta hai.

₹36 laakh ke kharch ke saamane yeh 18 mahine ka pebaik hai. Wahi do aankade frainchaaize aur stor ROI kailakuletar mein daalie, woh bhi yahi jawab deta hai, kyunki woh nivesh ko maasik munafe se baantata hai.

18 mahine tabhe theek hain jab egreement usse kaafe aage tak chale. Teen saal ki avadhi mein 18 mahine ka pebaik yani kamaane ke liye 18 mahine, aur 300 order par atake har mahine mein ek mahina chala jaata hai.

Teen aankade jo modal chupchaap tod dete hain

  • Roz ke order. 400 ki jagah 300 order par gros margin ₹5.4 laakh rah jaata hai aur stor ke paas ₹0.2 laakh mahina bachta hai. Kaagaz par pebaik pandrah saal tak khinch jaata hai — wahi stor, bas ek anumaan 25% zyada gulachharre wala.
  • Prati order margin. Agar discount aur shortej margin ko ₹60 se ₹48 par le aayein, to maasik munafa ₹0.56 laakh rah jaata hai. Prati order ka chhota badlav har mahine 12,000 se guna hota hai.
  • Vestej aur ritarn. Taaza aur deyare saamaan kharaab hota hai, aur toote paik nahi bikate. Is tarah bikri ka 2% jaana yani munafe ki laain se ₹1 laakh mahina, jo stor ke apekshit munafe ka aadha hai.

Sign karne se pehle kya poochein

  • Shortej, eksapaayare aur toote stok ka nuksan kaun uthaaega, paartanar ya pletaform?
  • Pletaform fees bikri ka pratishat hai ya tay maasik shulk, aur badalane se pehle kitna notis?
  • Peaaut saaikil kya hai, aur kisi bhi samay mera kitna paisa usamen phansa rehta hai?
  • Koi order gaarante hai ya egzakloosivite daayara jo mere ilaaqe mein doosara stor rokata hai?
  • Agar egreement jaldi khatm ho to dipozit, fit-out aur stok ka kya hoga?

Ek pej ka chek

Sign karne se pehle chaar aankade likh lijiye: kholane ki lagat, prati order yogadaan, mahine ka tay kharch, aur roz ke ve order jo use dhak dein. Kholane ki lagat ko maasik munafe se baantie aur jawab ko egreement ki avadhi se milaaie. Agar pebaik avadhi ke do-tihaaee se zyada hai, to stor pletaform ke liye kaam kar raha hai, aapke liye nahi.

Phir pura hisaab order anumaan ke 75% par bhi dekhie, kyunki zindage mein aapko wahi varzan jhelana padega.
