Frequently asked questions

What calculators are in Real Estate Toolkit?

Real Estate Toolkit holds 10 calculators: Property Valuation Calculator, Home Loan EMI Calculator, Rental Yield Calculator, Rent vs Buy Calculator, Stamp Duty Calculator, Brokerage / Agent Commission, Property ROI Calculator, Down Payment Calculator, Built-up Area vs Carpet Area and Land Area Unit Converter. Each one has its own page with the formula and a worked Indian example.

Are the Real Estate Toolkit calculators free?

Yes. Every one of them is free, needs no account and shows no paywall. There is no limit on how often you use them.

Is my data sent anywhere when I use these tools?

No. Every calculation runs inside your browser. The numbers you type are never uploaded, stored or sold.

Do these use Indian lakh and crore formatting?

Yes. Amounts are shown in the Indian numbering system, and the tax and salary tools use current Indian slabs and rules rather than a converted US template.
