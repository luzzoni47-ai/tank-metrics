Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Tank Metrics ("we", "us", "our", accessible at this website) treats information when you use our free business calculators. We built Tank Metrics to be as privacy-friendly as possible — here is exactly how it works.

1. The calculators don't send your numbers anywhere

Every calculator on Tank Metrics runs entirely inside your own browser using JavaScript. When you type a number into any field — revenue, valuation, CAC, EMI amount, or anything else — that number is used only to calculate the result on your screen. It is never transmitted to our servers, stored in a database, or seen by us. Closing or refreshing the page erases it completely.

2. Information we do collect

Like almost every website, we automatically receive some standard technical information when you visit, through our hosting provider and analytics tools:

  • Pages visited, time spent, and general navigation behavior
  • Approximate location (country/city level), device type and browser
  • Referring website or search engine

If you use our Contact Us form, we collect the name, email address and message you choose to submit, solely to respond to you.

3. Cookies and similar technologies

We may use cookies or local storage for basic site functionality, and third-party services (such as analytics and advertising partners) may set their own cookies to understand aggregate traffic patterns and serve relevant ads.

4. Advertising (Google AdSense)

Tank Metrics may display advertisements served by Google AdSense and other ad networks. These third parties may use cookies, device identifiers, or similar technologies to collect information about your visits to this and other websites in order to provide advertisements about goods and services of interest to you.

Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the Internet. You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings, and you can learn more about how Google uses data at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

5. Third-party links

Our blog and calculators may link to external resources. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of any third-party website you reach from a link on Tank Metrics.

6. Children's privacy

Tank Metrics is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Your choices

You can disable cookies in your browser settings, use ad-blocking or tracking-protection extensions, and clear your local storage at any time — none of this will affect your ability to use any calculator on this site.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy occasionally to reflect changes in our practices or for legal reasons. The "Last updated" date above will always reflect the latest revision.

9. Contact us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please reach out via our Contact Us page.
