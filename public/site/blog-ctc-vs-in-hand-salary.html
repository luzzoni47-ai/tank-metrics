CTC vs In-Hand Salary: What You Really Get

One number on the offer letter, a smaller one in the bank — here is the whole chain between them.

Everyday Money BasicsNovember 12, 2026

An offer letter quotes one number — the CTC — and your bank account shows a smaller one. Between the two sit the employee's PF share, professional tax, and the employer's own PF and gratuity contributions, which are counted inside CTC but never reach the employee at all. This guide walks the whole chain: what the company really pays, what comes out of the gross, and what actually lands in hand.

It is written for the first-time founder quoting a salary, and for anyone trying to read their own payslip. The arithmetic is simple once the two sides are kept apart.

CTC is not your salary

CTC, or cost to company, is the total yearly cost of one employee to the business. It is not what the employee receives. It bundles four things: the gross salary, the employer's PF contribution, a gratuity provision, and any other benefits such as insurance. Only the first of those is the salary the employee negotiated.

  • Gross salary — the monthly figure on the offer letter.
  • Employer PF — the company's own contribution, paid on top of gross.
  • Gratuity provision — money set aside for a future payout, not cash today.
  • Other benefits — insurance, meal cards, and similar add-ons.

What comes out of your gross pay

From the gross salary, the employee's own deductions come off before the money is credited. The two that appear on almost every payslip are the employee's PF share, at 12% of basic, and professional tax, a small state levy. TDS, the income tax deducted at source, comes off too once the salary crosses the taxable limit.

  • Employee PF — 12% of basic, matched by the employer.
  • Professional tax — a small monthly state levy.
  • TDS — income tax deducted at source, if any is due.

What remains after these deductions is the in-hand pay, the number that reaches the bank.

What the employer adds on top

The employer pays its own PF on top of gross, plus a gratuity provision of roughly 4.81% of basic, plus the cost of benefits. None of this appears in the employee's account, which is why the two numbers look so far apart. It is real money the business spends, so it belongs in the hiring budget.

  • Employer PF — 12% of basic, over and above gross.
  • Gratuity — about 4.81% of basic, provided for each year served.
  • Benefits — insurance and other perks carried by the company.

A worked example: a 30,000 rupee salary

Take a monthly gross salary of 30,000 rupees, with basic pay at half of gross. The employee's PF at 12% of basic is ₹1,800, and professional tax is ₹200. Nothing is due as TDS at this level, so the in-hand pay is 30,000 minus ₹1,800 minus ₹200, which is 28,000 rupees a month.

Now the employer's side. On the same salary the company pays its own PF of ₹1,800 and other benefits of ₹1,200. The monthly CTC is 30,000 plus ₹1,800 plus ₹1,200, which is 33,000 rupees. Scaled to a year, the CTC is about 3.96 lakh.

Put the two together and the picture is clear. The employee takes home 28,000 rupees a month, about 3.36 lakh a year, while the company spends 33,000 rupees a month, about 3.96 lakh a year. The gap is 60,000 rupees a year. Part of it is the employer's PF and benefits, which the company pays but the employee never receives; the rest is the employee's own PF and professional tax, which come off before the money is credited.

Why the CTC and in-hand numbers never match

Three things keep the two figures apart, and none of them is a mistake. PF is counted twice, once as the employee's share and once as the employer's. Gratuity is a provision set aside for the future, not cash paid today. And CTC is a yearly figure, so a monthly salary is only a twelfth of it, which hides the additions.

What this means when you hire your first person

Budget for the CTC, not the gross. A 30,000 rupee gross salary is a 33,000 rupee monthly cost, and the yearly commitment is the figure that should sit in the plan. When you quote a salary, say plainly what the in-hand will be, because a candidate who hears one number and receives a smaller one feels misled even when the arithmetic is correct.

The short version

  • CTC is the employer's total cost; in-hand is the gross minus the employee's deductions.
  • The employer's PF and gratuity provision are inside CTC but never reach the bank.
  • Budget the CTC when you hire, and quote the in-hand when you offer.

Beech mein karmachaare ka PF hissa, propheshanal tax, aur company ka apna PF aur grechyute hote hain, jo CTC ke andar gine jaate hain par karmachaare ke haath tak kabhi nahi pahunchte. Yeh guide poore kade dikhati hai: company asal mein kitna kharch karti hai, gros mein se kitna katata hai, aur haath mein kya bachta hai.

Yeh pehli baar salary tay karne wale phaaundar ke liye hai, aur unake liye bhi jo apni salary slip samajhana chahte hain. Hisaab aasan hai, bas dono taraf ko alag rakhana zaroore hai.

CTC aapki salary nahi hai

CTC, yani kost too company, ek karmachaare par saal bhar ka kul kharch hai. Yeh woh paisa nahi hai jo karmachaare ko milta hai. Isamen chaar cheezein jude hoti hain: gros salary, company ka PF, grechyute ka praavadhaan, aur bima jaise doosare suvidhaaen. Inmein se sirf pehli cheez woh salary hai jis par baatcheet huee the.

  • Gros salary — ophar letar par likha mahine ka aankda.
  • Company ka PF — company ka apna hissa, jo gros ke upar diya jaata hai.
  • Grechyute praavadhaan — aage ke bhugataan ke liye alag rakha paisa, aaj ka nakad nahi.
  • Doosare suvidhaaen — bima, khaane ka kaard aur aise hi faayade.

Gros salary mein se kya katata hai

Gros salary mein se karmachaare ke apne kataute pehle hatate hain, tab paisa khaate mein aata hai. Lagabhag har slip par do cheezein dikhate hain: karmachaare ka PF hissa, jo besik ka 12% hota hai, aur propheshanal tax, jo raajy ka ek chhota shulk hai. Kar yogy seema paar hone par TDS bhi katata hai.

  • Karmachaare ka PF — besik ka 12%, jitna company bhi milaate hai.
  • Propheshanal tax — mahine ka ek chhota raajy shulk.
  • TDS — srot par kata aayakar, agar banata ho.

In katautiyon ke baad jo bachta hai wahi in-haind paisa hai, jo bank tak pahunchata hai.

Company upar se kya jodate hai

Company gros ke upar apna PF dete hai, besik ka kareeb 4.81% grechyute ka praavadhaan jodate hai, aur suvidhaaon ka kharch bhi uthaate hai. Isamen se kuch bhi karmachaare ke khaate mein nahi dikhta, iseelie dono aankde itane door lagte hain. Yeh asli paisa hai jo karobar kharch karta hai, isliye yeh haayaring bajat mein gina jaana chahiye.

  • Company ka PF — besik ka 12%, gros ke upar.
  • Grechyute — besik ka kareeb 4.81%, har saal ki seva ke liye.
  • Suvidhaaen — bima aur doosare faayade, jo company uthaate hai.

Ek udaharan: 30,000 rupaye ki salary

Maan lijiye mahine ki gros salary 30,000 rupaye hai, aur besik gros ka aadha hai. Karmachaare ka PF, besik ka 12%, ₹1,800 banata hai, aur propheshanal tax ₹200 hai. Is star par TDS nahi banata, isliye in-haind paisa 30,000 mein se ₹1,800 aur ₹200 ghataakar 28,000 rupaye mahina rehta hai.

Ab company ki taraf dekhie. Usi salary par company apna PF ₹1,800 aur doosare suvidhaaen ₹1,200 dete hai. Mahine ka CTC 30,000 jama ₹1,800 jama ₹1,200, yani 33,000 rupaye banata hai. Saal bhar mein CTC kareeb 3.96 laakh hota hai.

Dono ko saath rakhane par tasveer saaf ho jaati hai. Karmachaare ke haath mein mahine ke 28,000 rupaye, yani saal ke kareeb 3.36 laakh aate hain, jabaki company mahine ke 33,000 rupaye, yani saal ke kareeb 3.96 laakh kharch karti hai. Farq saal ka kareeb 60,000 rupaye hai — company ka PF aur grechyute praavadhaan, jo kharch ka hissa hain par salary ka kabhi nahi.

CTC aur in-haind kabhi baraabar kyon nahi hote

Teen cheezein dono aankdon ko door rakhate hain, aur inmein se koi galat nahi hai. PF do baar gina jaata hai, ek baar karmachaare ke hisse mein aur ek baar company ke. Grechyute bhavishy ke liye alag rakha praavadhaan hai, aaj diya gaya nakad nahi. Aur CTC saal bhar ka aankda hai, isliye mahine ki salary uska baarahavaan hissa bhar hai, jisase judaav chhip jaata hai.

Pehla karmachaare rakhte samay isaka kya matlab hai

Bajat CTC par banaaie, gros par nahi. 30,000 rupaye ki gros salary mahine ka 33,000 rupaye ka kharch hai, aur saal bhar ki pratibaddhata wahi aankda hai jo yojana mein baithana chahiye. Jab salary tay karein to saaf bataaie ki haath mein kitna aaega, kyunki jo ummeedavaar ek aankda sunata hai aur usse kam paata hai, woh galat mahasoos karta hai, chaahe hisaab sahe ho.

Chhota jawab

  • CTC company ka kul kharch hai; in-haind gros mein se karmachaare ki katautiyaan hataane ke baad bachta hai.
  • Company ka PF aur grechyute praavadhaan CTC ke andar hain par bank tak kabhi nahi pahunchte.
  • Haayaring mein CTC ka bajat banaaie, aur ophar mein in-haind bataaie.
