Inventory Turnover Ratio: What It Tells You

How many times a year your stock sells through — and what slow stock costs you.

Growth & CashNovember 9, 2026

Stock on a shelf is cash you have already spent but not yet earned back. The faster goods move from the shelf to the customer, the sooner that money comes home. Inventory turnover is the one number that tells you how fast that happens.

This guide explains what inventory turnover measures, how to work it out from two figures you already have, and what a slow ratio quietly costs a small shop.

What inventory turnover measures

Inventory turnover counts how many times you sell through your whole stock in a year. It asks a simple question: for every rupee sitting in stock, how many rupees of goods did you sell against it?

  • A high number means stock moves quickly and your money is working.
  • A low number means goods sit on the shelf, and so does your cash.

How to work it out

You need two figures: your yearly cost of goods sold (what the stock you sold actually cost you) and your average inventory (the stock you hold on a typical day).

  1. Take your yearly cost of goods sold.
  2. Divide it by your average inventory.
  3. The answer is your turnover — how many times a year the shelf empties and refills.

To turn that into days, divide 365 by the turnover. That tells you how long one full round of stock takes to sell.

A worked example

Say a small shop sells goods that cost it ₹9,000 over a year. On a slow month its shelf holds ₹3,000 of stock on average.

  1. Yearly cost of goods sold: ₹9,000.
  2. Average inventory: ₹3,000.
  3. Turnover: 9,000 ÷ 3,000 = 3.0 times a year.
  4. Days to sell: 365 ÷ 3.0 = about 122 days.

Now suppose the owner trims the shelf to ₹1,500 of average stock, holding the same range:

  1. Average inventory: ₹1,500.
  2. Turnover: 9,000 ÷ 1,500 = 6.0 times a year.
  3. Days to sell: 365 ÷ 6.0 = about 61 days.

Nothing about sales changed, yet the stock now turns twice as fast and ₹1,500 of cash is back in the business instead of parked on the shelf.

What a good turnover looks like

There is no single perfect number — a jeweller and a grocer live in different worlds. As a rough guide:

  • Below 4 times a year: stock is slow, and cash is trapped.
  • Around 6 times a year: a healthy rhythm for most small product businesses.
  • Very high: great for cash, but check you are not running so thin that you miss sales.

What slow stock costs you

Dead stock is not free. It ties up the money you paid for it, it takes up shelf space you could give to a faster seller, and it can lose value while it waits. A shop that holds twice the stock it needs is, in effect, lending that cash to the warehouse.

Three ways to turn stock faster

  1. Order less, more often, so less cash sits in stock at any one time.
  2. Move the slowest lines to the front, discount them, or bundle them with fast sellers.
  3. Track the number every month, so a slow drift shows up before it becomes dead stock.

The one number to watch each month

Watch turnover the way you watch your bank balance. When it slips, cash is quietly draining into the shelf — and it usually slips long before the profit line shows it.

Yeh guide samjhati hai ki inventare tarnaovar kya naapata hai, aapke paas pehle se maujood do aankdon se ise kaise nikalein, aur dheema tarnaovar ek chhoti dukaan se chupchaap kya chheen leta hai.

Inventare tarnaovar kya naapata hai

Inventare tarnaovar ginata hai ki saal mein aapka pura stok kitni baar bikta hai. Yeh sawal poochhta hai: stok mein pade har rupaye ke saamane aapne kitne rupaye ka maal becha?

  • Ooncha aankda matlab maal tez chalta hai aur paisa kaam kar raha hai.
  • Neecha aankda matlab maal shelph par pada hai, aur paisa bhi.

Ise kaise nikalein

Aapko do aankde chahiye: saal bhar ki bike maal ki lagat, aur ausat stok — yani aam din mein jitna maal pada rehta hai.

  1. Saal bhar ki bike maal ki lagat lijiye.
  2. Use ausat stok se bhaag deejie.
  3. Jawab hai aapka tarnaovar — saal mein kitni baar shelph khaale huee aur bhare.

Ise dinon mein badalane ke liye 365 ko tarnaovar se bhaag deejie. Yeh batata hai ki maal ka ek pura chakkar bikne mein kitne din lagte hain.

Ek udaharan

Maan lijiye ek chhoti dukaan saal bhar mein ₹9,000 ka maal bechti hai. Dheeme mahine mein usake shelph par ausatan ₹3,000 ka stok pada rehta hai.

  1. Saal bhar ki bike maal ki lagat: ₹9,000.
  2. Ausat stok: ₹3,000.
  3. Tarnaovar: 9,000 ÷ 3,000 = saal mein 3.0 baar.
  4. Bikne ke din: 365 ÷ 3.0 = kareeb 122 din.

Ab maan lijiye dukaanadaar wahi renj rakhte hue stok ghataakar ₹1,500 kar deta hai:

  1. Ausat stok: ₹1,500.
  2. Tarnaovar: 9,000 ÷ 1,500 = saal mein 6.0 baar.
  3. Bikne ke din: 365 ÷ 6.0 = kareeb 61 din.

Bikri mein koi badlav nahi hua, phir bhi stok dugane tezi se ghoomata hai aur ₹1,500 ka paisa shelph ke bajaay karobar mein vaapas aa jaata hai.

Achha tarnaovar kaisa hota hai

Koi ek sahe aankda nahi hai — jauhare aur kiraana dukaan do alag duniya hain. Mote taur par:

  • Saal mein 4 baar se kam: stok dheema hai, aur paisa phansa hai.
  • Kareeb 6 baar: zyadatar chhote product karobar ke liye swasth lay.
  • Bahut ooncha: paise ke liye achha, lekin dekhie ki itana patla stok rakhakar bikri na chhoot jaye.

Dheema stok aapase kya chheenata hai

Pada hua maal muft nahi hai. Woh us paise ko rok leta hai jo aapne us par diya, woh shelph ki jagah leta hai jo tez chalane wale maal ko mil sakate the, aur intazaar mein uski keemat ghat sakate hai. Jo dukaan zaroorat se dugana stok rakhate hai, woh asal mein woh paisa godaam ko udhaar de rahi hai.

Stok tez ghumaane ke teen tarike

  1. Kam maal, baar-baar mangaaie, taaki kisi bhi samay kam paisa stok mein pada rahe.
  2. Sabse dheeme cheezon ko aage rakhie, un par chhoot deejie, ya tez bikne wale maal ke saath bandal keejie.
  3. Har mahine yeh aankda dekhie, taaki dheeme giraavat pade maal mein badalane se pehle dikh jaye.

Har mahine dekhane wala ek aankda

Tarnaovar ko vaise hi dekhie jaise apna bank balance dekhte hain. Jab yeh girata hai, paisa chupchaap shelph mein jaata rehta hai — aur yeh aam taur par munafe ki laain par dikhane se bahut pehle girane lagta hai.
