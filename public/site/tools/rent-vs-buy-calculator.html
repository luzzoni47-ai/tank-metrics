What this calculator is for

Rent vs Buy weighs the rent you would pay over a set number of years against what buying the same home costs before any loan. The rent side is the monthly rent multiplied by twelve and then by the years you are comparing. The buy side is the home price plus the one-time upfront costs — stamp duty, registration, brokerage, the moving-in expenses that never come back. The tool does not model a home loan, so it answers the narrower question of which side is smaller when you are paying cash or setting the loan aside for the moment.

How the maths works

Total rent = monthly rent x 12 x years compared. Buy cost before loan = home price + upfront buy costs. The tool shows both and names the smaller one as the lower cost. It deliberately leaves out the EMI and the interest, so the buy figure is the cash you commit on day one and not the total you pay over the holding period.

When to reach for it

  • You are renting and want to see the break-even against buying with cash.
  • You want the one-time cost of a purchase, separate from the loan.
  • You are explaining to someone why a lower rent can still lose over five years.

Common mistakes

  • Reading the buy figure as the full cost of owning — the loan interest is not in it.
  • Comparing a five-year rent total with a buy price for a home you would hold for twenty years.
  • Forgetting that stamp duty and registration sit inside the upfront number, not in the price.

Ye calculator kis kaam ka hai

Rent vs Buy ek tay saal ke rent ko usi ghar ko khareedne ki cost se milata hai, loan ke bina. Rent side simple hai: monthly rent ko 12 se, phir compare karne wale saalon se guna karo. Buy side me home price plus ek baar ka upfront cost aata hai — stamp duty, registration, brokerage, shifting ka kharcha, jo wapas nahi aata. Tool home loan model nahi karta, isliye yeh chhota sawaal dekhta hai ki cash dene par kaunsi side chhoti padti hai.

Ganit kaise chalta hai

Total rent = monthly rent x 12 x years compared. Buy cost before loan = home price + upfront buy costs. Tool dono dikhata hai aur jo chhota ho usko lower cost kehta hai. EMI aur interest jaan-boojh kar chhod diye jaate hain, isliye buy figure day-one ka cash hai, poora holding cost nahi.

Kab use karein

  • Jab aap rent par ho aur cash se khareedne ka break-even dekhna chahte ho.
  • Jab aapko purchase ka one-time cost chahiye, loan se alag.
  • Jab aap kisi ko samjha rahe ho ki kam rent bhi paanch saal me haar sakta hai.

Aam galtiyan

  • Buy figure ko poora ownership cost samajhna — loan interest usme nahi hai.
  • Paanch saal ka rent total ek ghar ki price se compare karna jise aap bees saal rakhoge.
  • Bhool jaana ki stamp duty aur registration upfront number me hain, price me nahi.
