What this calculator is for

ROAS is how much revenue every rupee of ad spend brought back, and it is the fastest read on whether a campaign is paying for itself. Spend Rs 1,00,000 on ads, book Rs 4,00,000 of revenue, and ROAS is 4x. What catches people out is reading 4x as profit: ROAS uses revenue, not margin, so a 4x ROAS on a product with a 20% margin is not four rupees of profit, it is eighty paise. This calculator divides ad revenue by ad spend and returns the multiple, so two channels can be compared on the same footing.

How the maths works

ROAS = ad revenue / ad spend, written as a multiple such as 3x. Spend 1,00,000 and book 4,00,000 and ROAS is 4,00,000 / 1,00,000 = 4x. The tool returns that multiple alone, on purpose, because revenue is the number you can measure cleanly. To turn ROAS into a break-even test, divide 1 by your contribution margin: at a 25% margin the break-even ROAS is 1 / 0.25 = 4x, so anything below 4x loses money even while it looks profitable.

When to reach for it

  • You are comparing two ad channels and want one number that puts them on the same scale.
  • A campaign is running and you want to know the spend it needs to at least break even.
  • You are setting a target ROAS before launch and want it grounded in your real margin.

Common mistakes

  • Reading ROAS as profit. It is revenue divided by spend, so a 4x ROAS on a thin-margin product can still lose money.
  • Comparing ROAS across products with different margins. Break-even ROAS is 1 divided by margin, so a 20% margin needs 5x and a 50% margin needs 2x.
  • Leaving out the costs outside ad spend: returns, shipping, marketplace fees and payment-gateway charges.

Ye calculator kis kaam ka hai

ROAS batata hai ki ad spend ke har rupee ne kitna revenue wapas laaya, aur yahi sabse tez tarika hai ye dekhne ka ki campaign khud ka kharcha nikal raha hai ya nahi. Rs 1,00,000 ads par lagaye aur Rs 4,00,000 revenue aaya to ROAS 4x hai. Ganit kaise chalta hai

ROAS = ad revenue / ad spend, 3x jaise multiple me. 1,00,000 lagaye aur 4,00,000 revenue aaya to ROAS = 4,00,000 / 1,00,000 = 4x. Tool bas yahi multiple deta hai, jaan-boojh kar: revenue wo number hai jo saaf measure hota hai. ROAS ko break-even test banane ke liye 1 ko apne contribution margin se divide karo - 25% margin par break-even ROAS = 1 / 0.25 = 4x, to 4x se neeche sab kuch paisa khota hai, chahe munaafa lage.

Kab use karein

  • Jab do ad channels compare kar rahe ho aur dono ko ek hi scale par dekhna hai.
  • Jab campaign chal rahi ho aur jaanna hai ki kam se kam break-even ke liye kitna spend chahiye.
  • Jab launch se pehle target ROAS set kar rahe ho aur usko apne asli margin par tikaana chahte ho.

Aam galtiyan

  • ROAS ko profit padhna. Ye revenue / spend hai, to patle margin wale product par 4x ROAS bhi nuksaan de sakta hai.
  • Alag margin wale products ka ROAS aapas me compare karna. Break-even ROAS = 1 / margin, to 20% margin ko 5x chahiye aur 50% ko 2x.
  • Ad spend ke bahar ki costs chhod dena - returns, shipping, marketplace fees, payment-gateway charges.
