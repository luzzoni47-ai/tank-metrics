Agency economics turn on utilisation and churn. A full team on low-value work and a steady leak of clients will undo a healthy-looking margin.

What this calculator is for

CPM to CPC Converter covers the economics of running client work. Convert impressions, CPM, and clicks into an estimated cost per click. Agencies fail on two things: too much unbilled time, and clients who leave faster than new ones arrive.

How the maths works

Profit is the fees you bill minus the cost of delivering the work, which is mostly people's time. Utilisation is billable hours divided by available hours. Churn is the clients lost divided by the clients you started with, and a churn rate doubles as a rough measure of how long a client stays. The tool shows each of these so you can see which one is leaking.

When to reach for it

  • You are pricing a retainer and want to know the margin before you quote.
  • A month felt full but the profit was thin.
  • You want to know how many clients you must win just to replace the ones leaving.

Common mistakes

  • Quoting a retainer from a monthly cost without allowing for unbilled revision rounds.
  • Treating a client as permanent. At 3% monthly churn, half are gone in under two years.
  • Counting total hours worked instead of billable ones.
