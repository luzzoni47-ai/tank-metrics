How this calculator works

Notice that the total interest is close to the amount borrowed. On a long loan, the interest is not a small add-on — it is nearly a second property.

What this calculator is for

The Loan EMI Calculator shows what a loan really costs per month and over its whole life. The EMI is the fixed amount you pay every month; it contains both interest and a repayment of the amount borrowed. Early payments are almost all interest, which is why a loan feels like it is not shrinking for the first few years.

How the maths works

The EMI is calculated so that every payment, compounded at the monthly rate over the full term, exactly clears the loan. The monthly rate is the annual rate divided by twelve, and the term is the number of months. Total interest is the EMI multiplied by the number of months, minus the amount borrowed. A longer term lowers the EMI but raises the total interest, which is the trade the calculator lets you test.

When to reach for it

  • You are comparing two loan offers with different rates or terms.
  • You want to know the monthly payment before you commit to a property.
  • You are deciding whether a longer term is worth the extra interest.

Common mistakes

  • Choosing the offer with the lowest EMI without checking the total interest.
  • Forgetting the processing fee and insurance, which are added to the cost of borrowing.
  • Assuming the rate is fixed for the whole term when it is linked to a benchmark that moves.

Ganit kaise chalta hai

EMI ka formula hai: P x r x (1+r)^n ÷ ((1+r)^n - 1), jahan P loan amount hai, r monthly interest rate (saalana rate ÷ 12 ÷ 100), aur n kitne mahine. Kyunki EMI me byaaj aur principal dono hote hain, shuru ke mahinon me zyadatar hissa byaaj jaata hai. Isliye tenure badhane se EMI kam hoti hai lekin kul byaaj kaafi zyada ho jaata hai.

Kab use karein

  • Jab aap home, car ya personal loan lene se pehle monthly commitment jaanna chahte ho.
  • Jab aap dekhan chahte ho ki tenure badhane se EMI kitni girti hai aur kul byaaj kitna badhta hai.
  • Jab aap do banks ke offers compare kar rahe ho.

Aam galtiyan

  • Sirf EMI dekhna aur kul byaaj na dekhna. Lambi tenure sasti lagti hai, hoti nahi.
  • Ye bhool jaana ki floating rate hone par EMI ya tenure aage badal sakti hai.
  • Budget me sirf EMI rakhna, processing fee aur insurance ke liye jagah na chhodna.
