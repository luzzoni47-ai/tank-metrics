What this calculator is for

Down Payment Calculator runs the numbers on a property purchase or a rental. Find the upfront amount needed from a property price and loan-to-value percentage. Property is the largest purchase most families make, and the costs that surprise people are the ones paid on the day of registration.

How the maths works

The cost of buying is the price plus stamp duty, registration and any brokerage. Rental yield is the annual rent divided by what the property cost, shown as a percentage. Return on a sale is the sale price minus the purchase price and costs, divided by the money you put in. A loan changes the picture: the EMI includes interest, so early payments barely reduce the principal.

When to reach for it

  • You are comparing two properties and want to see which one earns more.
  • You want to know the cash you need on the day of registration, not just the price.
  • You are deciding between buying and continuing to rent.

Common mistakes

  • Forgetting stamp duty and registration, which together can add 6% to 8% to the price.
  • Comparing gross rent with the EMI. Rent is before maintenance, tax and vacancy.
  • Assuming prices only rise, and leaving no room for a slow year.
