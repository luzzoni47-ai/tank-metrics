What this calculator is for

A land area unit converter turns square feet into the units Indian land is actually sold in - square yards, square metres, and the traditional units that still rule property deals in many states: guntha, cent, bigha and acre. This matters because a plot quoted in one unit and compared against another is not a fair comparison, and the traditional units differ from state to state, so a bigha in one state is not a bigha in the next.

How the maths works

1 square yard = 9 square feet. 1 square metre = 10.764 square feet. 1 acre = 43,560 square feet, which is 4,840 square yards. 1 guntha = 1,089 square feet, so 40 guntha make an acre. 1 cent = 435.6 square feet, so 100 cents make an acre. A 2,400 square feet plot is 266.7 square yards or about 223 square metres - useful when the buyer and the seller are quoting different units for the same land.

When to reach for it

  • A plot is quoted in one unit and you need to compare it with another.
  • You are reading a sale deed or a 7/12 extract and need the area in a unit you understand.
  • You are converting between traditional units and square feet for a listing.

Common mistakes

  • Assuming a bigha or a guntha is the same everywhere in India. These units vary by state and sometimes by district.
  • Mixing up square feet and square yards, which differ by a factor of nine.
  • Converting the area but not checking the survey number and boundaries against the deed.
