What this calculator is for

A SIP is a fixed amount invested in a mutual fund every month. The point of the calculator is compounding: 10,000 rupees a month for 20 years at 12% is not 24 lakh of contributions, it is about 99 lakh, and almost three quarters of that is growth you never deposited. The calculator separates your contribution from the growth, which is the number that makes people start a SIP rather than think about it.

How the maths works

Future Value = P x [((1 + i)^n - 1) / i] x (1 + i), where P is the monthly amount, i is the monthly rate (annual / 12 / 100) and n is the number of instalments. Contribution = P x n; Growth = Future Value - Contribution.

When to reach for it

  • You are deciding whether to increase a SIP by 5,000 rupees a month and want to see the 20-year difference.
  • You want to know how many years a goal needs, given what you can afford monthly.
  • You are choosing between a 10% and a 12% fund and want to see whether the gap is worth the risk.

Common mistakes

  • Using the fund's last-year return as the assumption. SIPs are planned on a long-run average, not last year's best.
  • Stopping the SIP in a falling market, which is exactly when you buy the most units per rupee.
  • Ignoring the expense ratio. A 1% higher ratio costs roughly a tenth of a 20-year corpus.

Ye calculator kis kaam ka hai

SIP matlab har mahine ek fixed amount mutual fund me daalna. Calculator ka poora point compounding hai: 10,000 rupaye mahina 20 saal 12% par 24 lakh ka contribution nahi, lagbhag 99 lakh banta hai, aur usme se teen-chauthai wo growth hai jo aapne kabhi jama hi nahi ki. Calculator aapka paisa aur growth alag dikhata hai, aur yahi number logon ko SIP shuru karwata hai.

Ganit kaise chalta hai

Future Value = P x [((1 + i)^n - 1) / i] x (1 + i), jahan P monthly amount, i monthly rate (annual / 12 / 100) aur n instalments. Contribution = P x n; Growth = Future Value - Contribution.

Kab use karein

  • Aap SIP 5,000 rupaye badhane ka soch rahe ho aur 20 saal ka farak dekhna chahte ho.
  • Aapko jaanna hai ki jo afford kar sakte ho usse goal kitne saal me pura hoga.
  • 10% aur 12% fund me choose kar rahe ho aur dekhna chahte ho ki risk ke layak gap hai ya nahi.

Aam galtiyan

  • Fund ke pichhle saal ke return ko assumption bana lena. SIP long-run average par plan hoti hai, pichhle saal ke best par nahi.
  • Girte market me SIP band kar dena - aur yahi waqt hai jab har rupee par sabse zyada units milte hain.
  • Expense ratio ignore karna. 1% zyada ratio 20 saal ke corpus ka lagbhag dasva hissa kha jata hai.
