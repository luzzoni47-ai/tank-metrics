What this calculator is for

CAC payback period is how many months of a customer's profit it takes to earn back what you spent winning them. It is the cash-flow version of the LTV:CAC ratio: a healthy ratio can still hide a payback so long that the business runs out of money before it profits. Enter the acquisition cost, the monthly revenue per customer and the gross margin, and you get the number of months. A payback under twelve months lets you reinvest quickly; three years means every new customer is a bet on money you will not see for a long time.

How the maths works

Payback Period (months) = CAC ÷ (Monthly Revenue per Customer × Gross Margin %). If a customer costs ₹5,000 to win, pays ₹1,000 a month and you keep 40% as margin, the payback is ₹5,000 ÷ ₹400 = 12.5 months. The margin is what makes the payback real; on revenue alone the same customer looks like a five-month payback that never arrives.

When to reach for it

  • You are deciding how aggressively to spend on acquisition.
  • You have raised money and want to know how fast it recycles into new customers.
  • You are comparing two customer segments with very different payback speeds.

Common mistakes

  • Using revenue instead of gross-margin profit in the denominator, which halves the apparent payback.
  • Forgetting that a long payback is only survivable if the cash to fund it is already in the bank.
  • Ignoring churn - customers who leave before the payback month never repay the cost.

Ye calculator kis kaam ka hai

CAC payback period batata hai ki customer ko jeetne me jo kharch hua, wo us customer ke profit ke kitne mahine me wapas aata hai. Ye LTV:CAC ratio ka cash-flow version hai: accha ratio bhi itna lamba payback chhupa sakta hai ki paisa profit se pehle khatam ho jaaye. Acquisition cost, per customer monthly revenue aur gross margin daaliye - kitne mahine, ye mil jaata hai. Barah mahine se kam payback se aap jaldi dobara invest kar sakte ho; teen saal matlab har naya customer us paise pe daanv hai jo der se aayega.

Ganit kaise chalta hai

Payback Period (months) = CAC ÷ (Monthly Revenue per Customer × Gross Margin %). Customer jeetne me ₹5,000 lage, wo ₹1,000 mahina deta hai aur 40% margin rakhte ho toh payback ₹5,000 ÷ ₹400 = 12.5 mahine. Margin hi payback ko asli banata hai; sirf revenue pe wahi customer paanch mahine ka payback dikhata hai jo kabhi aata hi nahi.

Kab use karein

  • Tay kar rahe ho ki acquisition pe kitna aggressive kharch karna hai.
  • Paisa raise kiya hai aur jaanna hai ki kitni jaldi naye customers me badlega.
  • Do segments compare kar rahe ho jinki payback speed alag hai.

Aam galtiyan

  • Denominator me revenue use karna gross-margin profit ke bajaye, jisse payback aadha dikhta hai.
  • Ye bhoolna ki lamba payback tabhi chalega jab funding ka paisa bank me pehle se ho.
  • Churn ignore karna - jo customer payback ke mahine se pehle chala gaya, wo cost kabhi wapas nahi karta.
