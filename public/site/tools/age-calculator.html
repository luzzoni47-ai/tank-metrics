What this calculator is for

The Age Calculator settles the question a form asks, not the one a birthday card asks: as of a given date, how many completed years has this person lived? You give it one date of birth and it answers in whole years and whole months, counting the way a school admission register, a government application or a pension rule counts. That makes it the tool for a cut-off — a class-entry date, an exam age limit, a scheme that opens at eighteen or closes at sixty — where the day of the month, not just the year, decides who qualifies. It is deliberately not a "how old am I" novelty. Because the answer is tied to today's date, the same birth date reads differently before and after the birthday, and a month-end birthday is counted from the real calendar rather than assumed to be thirty days. When you need the age on a date that is not today — the closing date of an application, the first day of a school year — open the tool on that date instead of trusting a figure you noted last month.

How the maths works

The calculator compares the birth date with today one part at a time: year, then month, then day. Whole years = today's year − birth year, and if today's month is earlier than the birth month, subtract one; if the months are equal but today's day is earlier than the birth day, subtract one as well. The remaining whole months = today's month − birth month, plus twelve if that went negative, minus one when today's day is before the birth day. A 15 June 1995 birth read on 21 October 2026 gives 31 years and 4 months, because the June birthday has passed and July to October is four whole months.

When to reach for it

  • You are filling a school, exam, pension or government form that asks for age in completed years, and that exact figure decides eligibility.
  • You are checking whether someone clears an age cut-off on a fixed date — a class-entry date, a scheme's upper limit, a retirement rule.
  • You need the precise age in years and months for a document, a visa form or an application, not a rounded-up guess.

Common mistakes

  • Subtracting the two years and stopping there. That overstates the age by a full year for anyone whose birthday has not yet arrived this year.
  • Rounding up to the next birthday. A form that asks for completed years wants the age already reached, so rounding up can disqualify an application that was actually eligible.
  • Working from an "about 31" figure for a cut-off when a single day on either side of the deadline flips the answer.

Ye calculator kis kaam ka hai

Age Calculator wo sawaal nipta deta hai jo birthday card par nahi, form par aata hai: kisi fixed date tak is insaan ne kitne poore saal poore kiye hain? Aap ek date of birth daalte ho aur tool saal aur mahine me jawab deta hai, poore mahine usi tarah gin kar jaise school admission register, sarkari application ya pension rule ginta hai. Yahi wajah hai ki ye cut-off ke liye sahi tool hai — class-entry date, exam ki age limit, ya koi scheme jo athara par khulti hai ya saath par band hoti hai — jahan saal ke saath mahine ki taareekh bhi tay karti hai ki kaun qualify karta hai. Ye jaan-boojh kar "main kitna bada hoon" wala shauk nahi hai. Kyunki jawab aaj ki date se bandha hai, wahi date of birth birthday se pehle aur baad me alag figure deti hai, aur month-end birthday asli calendar se gina jaata hai, ye maan kar nahi ki har mahina tees din ka hai. Agar aapko aaj ke alawa kisi date — application ki closing date, school saal ke shuruaat — par umar chahiye, to tool usi din dekho, pichhle mahine likha figure par bharosa na karo.

Ganit kaise chalta hai

Tool birth date ke teen hisse aaj se ek-ek karke milata hai: pehle saal, phir mahina, phir din. Poore saal = aaj ka saal − birth year, aur agar aaj ka mahina birth month se pehle hai to ek kam; agar mahine barabar hain par aaj ki taareekh birth day se pehle hai to bhi ek kam. Bache hue poore mahine = aaj ka mahina − birth month, plus barah agar ye negative ho gaya, aur ek kam jab aaj ki taareekh birth day se pehle ho. 15 June 1995 ki date 21 October 2026 ko 31 saal aur 4 mahine deti hai, kyunki June ka birthday nikal gaya aur July se October chaar poore mahine hain.

Kab use karein

  • Aap school, exam, pension ya sarkari form bhar rahe ho jo poore saal me umar maangta hai aur yahi figure eligibility tay karta hai.
  • Aap check kar rahe ho ki koi fixed date par age cut-off clear karta hai — class-entry date, scheme ki upper limit, retirement rule.
  • Aapko kisi document, visa form ya application ke liye saal aur mahine me sahi umar chahiye, andaaza nahi.

Aam galtiyan

  • Dono saal ghatakar wahi rok dena. Isse us har vyakti ki umar ek poora saal zyada dikhti hai jiska birthday is saal abhi aaya nahi.
  • Agle birthday tak round up kar dena. Jo form poore saal maangta hai use ab tak ki umar chahiye, isliye round up karne se eligible application bhi reject ho sakti hai.
  • Cut-off ke liye "1995 me paida, matlab lagbhag 31" jaisa andaaza lagana, jabki deadline se ek din aage-peeche jawab palat deta hai.
