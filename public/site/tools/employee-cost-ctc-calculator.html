What this calculator is for

Employee Cost (CTC) Calculator prices the things a small team spends on people. See the real monthly and yearly cost of an employee, beyond just their take-home salary. The salary you agree is not the cost of the hire, and the invoice you pay is not the cost of the freelancer.

How the maths works

The full cost of an employee is the salary plus provident fund, insurance, equipment, software and the time spent managing them. For a freelancer, it is the rate times the hours, plus the hours lost to briefing and revision. Utilisation — the share of paid hours that are actually billable — is what decides whether a team makes money.

When to reach for it

  • You are about to make a first hire and want the real monthly commitment.
  • You are comparing a freelancer against a full-time salary.
  • You want to know how many billable hours your team must sell to cover its own cost.

Common mistakes

  • Budgeting for the salary only. The full cost is often 1.2 to 1.4 times the number you agreed.
  • Assuming a freelancer bills every hour you pay for.
  • Hiring ahead of the revenue that will pay for the hire.
