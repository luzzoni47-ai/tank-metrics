What this calculator is for

The COD & RTO Loss Calculator puts a rupee figure on the cash-on-delivery orders that come back. RTO — return to origin — is the parcel a courier carries to the customer, fails to deliver, and brings back to you; you pay the forward freight, the return freight and often the packaging, and you never make the sale. The tool turns your COD volume and RTO rate into the number of parcels that bounce and the money those bounces take out of the month. Open it when a category looks profitable on paper but the bank balance disagrees, because RTO is the cost that never appears in a margin sheet until it is added by hand.

How the maths works

RTO orders = total COD orders x RTO rate / 100. Total loss = RTO orders x loss per RTO order. Loss per RTO order is the all-in cost of one bounce — forward freight plus return freight plus packaging plus any re-attempt charge — not just the courier bill you see first. The tool multiplies the two so you can watch the loss scale with volume.

When to reach for it

  • Your COD share is high and you want to know what the returns cost before you cut it.
  • You are deciding whether a prepaid discount is worth moving customers off COD.
  • You are negotiating freight rates and want to show the courier what RTO is costing you.

Common mistakes

  • Counting only the return freight and forgetting the forward leg, which you pay either way.
  • Treating RTO as a share of all orders rather than of COD orders, which understates it.
  • Ignoring the second sale a returned parcel kills, since the stock sits out of the market for a week.

RTO — return to origin — wo parcel hai jise courier customer tak le gaya, deliver nahi kar paya aur wapas aapke paas le aaya; forward freight, return freight aur aksar packaging aap bharte ho, aur sale hui hi nahi. Tool aapke COD volume aur RTO rate se nikaalta hai ki kitne parcel bounce hue aur unse mahine me kitna paisa gaya. Ganit kaise chalta hai

RTO orders = total COD orders x RTO rate / 100. Total loss = RTO orders x loss per RTO order. Loss per RTO order matlab ek bounce ki poori cost — forward freight plus return freight plus packaging plus re-attempt charge — sirf wahi courier bill nahi jo pehle dikhta hai. Tool dono ko multiply karta hai taki volume ke saath loss kitna badhta hai ye dikhe.

Kab use karein

  • Jab aapka COD share zyada ho aur cut karne se pehle jaanna ho ki returns kitna kha rahe hain.
  • Jab aap soch rahe ho ki prepaid discount de kar customers ko COD se hataana chahiye ya nahi.
  • Jab aap freight rate negotiate kar rahe ho aur courier ko dikhana ho ki RTO aapko kitna mehnga pad raha hai.

Aam galtiyan

  • Sirf return freight ginna aur forward leg bhool jaana, jo aap har haal me bharte ho.
  • RTO ko saare orders ka percent maanna, sirf COD orders ka nahi, jisse aankda kam dikhta hai.
  • Ye na dekhna ki wapas aaya parcel ek doosri sale bhi maarta hai, kyunki stock ek hafte market se bahar rehta hai.
