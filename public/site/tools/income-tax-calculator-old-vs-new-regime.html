What this calculator is for

Income Tax Calculator (Old vs New Regime) estimates tax and long-term saving figures. Compare a simple estimated tax under India’s old and new tax regimes. The rules change, and they treat different kinds of income differently, so these tools show the arithmetic rather than a fixed answer.

How the maths works

Income tax is worked out slab by slab: the rate applies only to the part of your income that falls in each band, not to the whole amount. Deductions reduce the income the slabs apply to. For savings, the future value is the amount you put in plus the interest or returns it earns, with the returns compounding if they are reinvested. The tool shows the total you contribute and the total it grows to, so you can see the split.

When to reach for it

  • You are choosing between the old and new tax regimes.
  • You want to know what a monthly saving becomes over ten or twenty years.
  • You are checking a payslip or a Form 16 against the rules.

Common mistakes

  • Assuming a higher slab taxes all your income at that rate. It does not.
  • Comparing a tax-free option with a taxed one without adjusting for the tax.
  • Treating a projected return as guaranteed.
