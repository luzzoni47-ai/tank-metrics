What this calculator is for

An old vs new regime calculator tells you which of India's two income tax systems takes less from you this year, and the answer changes with almost every salary. The new regime gives lower slab rates but throws away most deductions. The old regime has higher rates but lets you subtract 80C, 80D, HRA and home loan interest before tax is worked out. So the comparison is not really about rates at all - it is about whether your deductions are large enough to beat the rate cut. Someone with no investments is almost always better off in the new regime; someone carrying a big home loan and full 80C is often better off in the old one.

How the maths works

Old regime: tax on (Income − Deductions) using the old slabs. New regime: tax on Income using the new slabs, with almost no deductions. Whichever tax is lower wins. A plain ₹15 lakh salary with no investments is usually cheaper in the new regime, because there is nothing much to deduct. Add a ₹2 lakh home loan interest and a full ₹1.5 lakh of 80C, and the old regime can pull ahead again. Finding that crossover is what this calculator is for.

When to reach for it

  • Your employer asks you to pick a regime at the start of the year and you do not want to guess.
  • You have just taken a home loan or started 80C investments and the answer may have changed.
  • You are comparing two job offers with different salary structures.

Common mistakes

  • Assuming the new regime is always better because the rates look lower. It is only better when your deductions are small.
  • Forgetting that the new regime still gives the standard deduction but not 80C, 80D or HRA.
  • Comparing last year's numbers with this year's slabs. Both regimes and their slabs have changed more than once in the last few years.

Ye calculator kis kaam ka hai

Old vs new regime calculator batata hai ki is saal India ke do income tax systems mein se kaunsa aapse kam leta hai - aur jawab lagbhag har salary ke saath badalta hai. New regime slab rates kam deta hai par zyadatar deductions chheen leta hai. Old regime ke rates zyada hain par 80C, 80D, HRA aur home loan interest tax se pehle ghata sakte ho. Isliye comparison rates ka nahi hai - ye dekhna hai ki aapke deductions rate cut ko beat karte hain ya nahi. Jiske paas investments nahi, woh lagbhag hamesha new regime mein better hai; jiske paas bada home loan aur poora 80C hai, woh aksar old regime mein.

Ganit kaise chalta hai

Old regime: (Income − Deductions) par purane slabs se tax. New regime: Income par naye slabs se tax, lagbhag bina deductions. Jo tax kam, wahi jeeta. Saada ₹15 lakh salary, koi investment nahi: new regime aksar kam hota hai, kyunki ghata-ne ke liye zyada kuch hai hi nahi. ₹2 lakh home loan interest aur poora ₹1.5 lakh 80C jodo, toh old regime aage nikal sakta hai. Yahi crossover dhoondhna calculator ka kaam hai.

  • Pichhle saal ke numbers is saal ke slabs se compare karna. Dono regimes aur unke slabs pichhle kuch saalon mein ek se zyada baar badle hain.
