What this calculator is for

Break-even ad spend is the most you can pay for advertising before a campaign stops making money. It is the ceiling, not a target, and it is set by your own margin - not by what competitors bid or what a platform suggests. A product with ₹300 of contribution per sale can afford to spend almost all of that to win the sale and still break even, but every rupee above it is a loss. Knowing this number stops you from bidding your way into a loss.

How the maths works

Contribution per Sale = Revenue per Sale − Product Cost per Sale. Break-Even Ad Spend = Contribution per Sale × Expected Conversions. Sell at ₹1,000 with a product cost of ₹600 and the contribution is ₹400. Expecting 20 conversions, you can spend up to ₹8,000 and break even - an ₹8,000 ad budget, or a maximum cost per acquisition of ₹400. Spend ₹10,000 and the campaign loses ₹2,000.

When to reach for it

  • You are setting a maximum bid or a daily ad budget.
  • A campaign is running and you want to know when to switch it off.
  • You are comparing two products and want to see which can support more ad spend.

Common mistakes

  • Using revenue instead of contribution. The product cost has to come out before ad spend is affordable.
  • Treating break-even as the target. At break-even you make nothing; you need to spend below it.
  • Forgetting delivery, packaging and payment fees, which reduce the contribution and therefore the ceiling.

Ganit kaise chalta hai

Contribution per Sale = Revenue per Sale − Product Cost per Sale. Break-Even Ad Spend = Contribution per Sale × Expected Conversions. ₹1,000 mein becho, product cost ₹600, contribution ₹400. 20 conversions expect karte ho toh ₹8,000 tak kharch karke break even - ₹8,000 ad budget, ya maximum ₹400 cost per acquisition. ₹10,000 kharch karo toh campaign ₹2,000 khota hai.

Kab use karein

  • Maximum bid ya daily ad budget set kar rahe ho.
  • Campaign chal rahi hai aur jaanna hai kab band karni hai.
  • Do products compare kar rahe ho aur dekhna hai kaunsa zyada ad spend jhel sakta hai.

Aam galtiyan

  • Contribution ki jagah revenue use karna. Ad spend afford karne se pehle product cost nikalni padti hai.
  • Break-even ko target samajh lena. Break-even par kuch nahi kamate; usse neeche kharch karna hai.
  • Delivery, packaging aur payment fees bhool jaana, jo contribution ghatate hain aur ceiling bhi.
