What this calculator is for

A GST calculator does the two things you need constantly: adds GST to a price to get the final bill, and pulls GST out of an amount that already includes it. The second direction is the one that catches people, because a price quoted as inclusive of GST hides a smaller base than it looks. In India the rate is split into CGST and SGST for sales inside a state, or a single IGST for sales across states, but the total is the same.

How the maths works

GST Amount = Base Price × GST Rate ÷ 100, and the final price is Base + GST. A ₹1,000 base at 18% has ₹180 of GST and a ₹1,180 bill. Reverse it: if a bill is ₹1,180 inclusive of 18% GST, the base is 1,180 ÷ 1.18 = ₹1,000 and the tax is ₹180. The reverse direction uses division, not subtraction - subtracting 18% from ₹1,180 gives ₹967.60, which is wrong.

When to reach for it

  • You are raising an invoice and need the GST amount and the total.
  • A price is quoted as inclusive and you need the base and the tax separated.
  • You are comparing a GST-inclusive price with a GST-exclusive one.

Common mistakes

  • Subtracting the GST rate from an inclusive price instead of dividing by (1 + rate). It always understates the base.
  • Forgetting that GST is charged on the discounted price when a discount is given before supply, but on the full price when it is given afterwards.
  • Treating GST collected as your money. It is held for the government and has to be paid.

Ye calculator kis kaam ka hai

GST calculator wahi do kaam karta hai jo aapko hamesha chahiye: price par GST jodkar final bill nikalna, aur aisi amount se GST nikaalna jisme woh pehle se shaamil hai. Doosri direction hi logon ko phasati hai, kyunki GST-inclusive price ke andar base chhota hota hai jitna woh dikhta hai. India mein rate state ke andar bikri par CGST aur SGST mein bat jaata hai, ya state ke bahar bikri par ek IGST - par total wahi rehta hai.

Ganit kaise chalta hai

GST Amount = Base Price × GST Rate ÷ 100, aur final price Base + GST hai. ₹1,000 base par 18% GST = ₹180 aur ₹1,180 ka bill. Ulta: agar bill 18% GST ke saath ₹1,180 hai, toh base 1,180 ÷ 1.18 = ₹1,000 aur tax ₹180 hai. Ulta direction division use karti hai, subtraction nahi - ₹1,180 se 18% ghatao toh ₹967.60 aata hai, jo galat hai.

Kab use karein

  • Invoice bana rahe ho aur GST amount aur total chahiye.
  • Price inclusive batayi gayi hai aur base aur tax alag karne hain.
  • GST-inclusive price ko GST-exclusive se compare karna hai.

Aam galtiyan

  • Inclusive price se GST rate ghata dena, (1 + rate) se divide karne ki jagah. Isse base hamesha kam aata hai.
  • Ye bhool jaana ki supply se pehle discount dene par GST discounted price par lagti hai, par baad mein dene par poori price par.
  • GST ko apna paisa samajh lena. Woh sarkar ke liye rakha jaata hai aur dena padta hai.
