What this calculator is for

GST Calculator works backwards from what you can charge to what you keep. Add GST to a price, or find the base price hidden inside a GST-included amount. Pricing is the fastest lever a small business has — a one-rupee change per unit drops straight to profit — and the one most owners set by guesswork.

How the maths works

Start with the price the customer pays, then subtract everything between that price and your bank account: marketplace commission, shipping, packaging, payment fees, returns and discounts. What is left is your contribution. Subtract the cost of the goods and you have the profit on one unit. The tool shows each subtraction so you can see which one is doing the damage.

When to reach for it

  • You are launching a product and need a price that leaves a real margin.
  • A marketplace raised its commission and you want to know how much price must move.
  • You are about to run a discount and want to see what it does to profit.

Common mistakes

  • Setting a price from cost plus a markup, without checking what the market will pay.
  • Ignoring returns. In some categories, a fifth of orders come back and the shipping is yours to pay twice.
  • Discounting the price without checking the extra volume needed to stand still.
