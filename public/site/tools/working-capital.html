What this calculator is for

Working capital is the money tied up in running the business day to day: stock on the shelf, plus what customers owe you, minus what you owe suppliers. A business can be profitable and still run out of cash, because profit is an accounting number and working capital is cash. The calculator turns the three lines into one figure and a cycle length, so you can see how many days your money is locked up before it comes back.

How the maths works

Working Capital = Current Assets - Current Liabilities. Cash Conversion Cycle = Days Inventory + Days Receivable - Days Payable.

When to reach for it

  • You are profitable but the bank account keeps going to zero and you need to see where the money sits.
  • A customer asks for 60-day terms and you want to price what that costs you.
  • You are applying for a working-capital limit and need the number a bank will ask for.

Common mistakes

  • Counting only stock and forgetting receivables. Slow-paying customers tie up more cash than slow-moving stock.
  • Treating a negative working capital as always bad. For a subscription business with prepaid plans it is healthy.
  • Ignoring the cycle length. 50 lakh tied up for 90 days is a very different business from the same amount tied up for 15.

Ye calculator kis kaam ka hai

Working capital wo paisa hai jo business ke rozana kaam me bandha hota hai: shelf par stock, plus jo customers aapko dena hai, minus jo aap suppliers ko dena hai. Business profitable ho sakta hai aur phir bhi cash khatam ho jaye, kyunki profit accounting ka number hai aur working capital cash ka. Calculator teen lines ko ek figure aur ek cycle length me badal deta hai, taaki dikhe ki aapka paisa kitne din ghoom kar wapas aata hai.

Ganit kaise chalta hai

Working Capital = Current Assets - Current Liabilities. Cash Conversion Cycle = Days Inventory + Days Receivable - Days Payable.

Kab use karein

  • Aap profitable ho par bank account baar-baar zero hota hai aur dekhna hai paisa kahan baitha hai.
  • Customer 60 din ki terms maangta hai aur aapko uska cost price karna hai.
  • Aap working-capital limit ke liye apply kar rahe ho aur bank jo number poochega wo chahiye.

Aam galtiyan

  • Sirf stock ginna aur receivables bhoolna. Slow-paying customers slow stock se zyada cash bandhte hain.
  • Negative working capital ko hamesha kharab maanna. Prepaid plans wale subscription business ke liye ye healthy hai.
  • Cycle length ignore karna. 50 lakh 90 din ke liye bandha aur wahi raqam 15 din ke liye - do alag business hain.
