What this calculator is for

Your Western zodiac sign is fixed by your birth date and the sun's position on that date, which is why it is the one sign you can look up from a date alone. The twelve signs run on the same calendar every year - Aries starts around 21 March, Cancer around 21 June - with a shift of about a day between leap years. This tool returns the sign for the date you enter and names the exact range it used, so a birthday on a cusp is settled rather than guessed.

How the maths works

Each sign owns a fixed band of the calendar: Capricorn runs 22 December to 19 January, Aquarius 20 January to 18 February, Pisces 19 February to 20 March, Aries 21 March to 19 April, Taurus 20 April to 20 May, Gemini 21 May to 20 June, Cancer 21 June to 22 July, Leo 23 July to 22 August, Virgo 23 August to 22 September, Libra 23 September to 22 October, Scorpio 23 October to 21 November, Sagittarius 22 November to 21 December. The tool compares your month and day against these boundaries and returns the sign whose band contains the date.

When to reach for it

  • You need your sun sign for a horoscope and have only the date.
  • A birthday sits on a cusp and you want to know which sign the date alone points to.
  • You are filling a profile that asks for a zodiac sign and do not want to look up a chart.

Common mistakes

  • Reading a cusp birthday as certain. Within a day of a boundary the sign depends on the birth time, not the date.
  • Using the Western sign for a Vedic question. Rashi uses the moon, not the sun, and needs the birth time.
  • Assuming the date ranges never move. A leap year pushes the boundaries by about a day.

Ye calculator kis kaam ka hai

Western zodiac sign aapki birth date aur us din sooraj ki position se tay hota hai, isliye yahi ek sign hai jo sirf date se nikal jaata hai. Barah signs ka calendar har saal same chalta hai - Aries lagbhag 21 March, Cancer lagbhag 21 June - leap year me ek din ka farq aata hai. Ye tool aapki date ka sign dikhata hai aur wahi exact range bhi likhta hai jo usne use ki, taki cusp wala birthday guess na ho, settle ho jaye.

Ganit kaise chalta hai

Har sign ka calendar me ek fixed band hai: Capricorn 22 December se 19 January, Aquarius 20 January se 18 February, Pisces 19 February se 20 March, Aries 21 March se 19 April, Taurus 20 April se 20 May, Gemini 21 May se 20 June, Cancer 21 June se 22 July, Leo 23 July se 22 August, Virgo 23 August se 22 September, Libra 23 September se 22 October, Scorpio 23 October se 21 November, aur Sagittarius 22 November se 21 December. Tool aapke month aur day ko in boundaries se compare karta hai aur wahi sign deta hai jiske band me date aati hai.

Kab use karein

  • Birthday cusp par hai aur jaanna hai ki date akeli kis sign ki taraf ishara karti hai.

  • Cusp wale birthday ko pakka maan lena. Boundary ke ek din andar sign birth time par depend karta hai.

  • Ye maan lena ki date ranges kabhi nahi hilti. Leap year boundaries ko lagbhag ek din khiska deta hai.
