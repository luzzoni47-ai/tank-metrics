Growth figures are only useful next to a cash figure. A business can grow quickly and still run out of money, which is what the runway number is there to catch.

What this calculator is for

CAGR (Compound Annual Growth Rate) tracks the two things that kill young businesses: running out of cash, and growing faster than the cash can follow. The smoothed-out average yearly growth over several years, ignoring ups and downs. Growth is a headline; cash is the oxygen.

How the maths works

Burn is the cash a business spends each month beyond what it earns. Runway is the cash you hold divided by that monthly burn — the number of months before the money is gone. Run rate takes one period's revenue and scales it to a year, which is useful for comparison but assumes the period repeats. Break-even is the sales level at which contribution covers your fixed costs, so profit is zero and every extra sale is profit.

When to reach for it

  • You want to know how many months you have before you must raise or cut.
  • You are deciding between hiring now and waiting a quarter.
  • A month was unusually strong and you want to compare it fairly with the rest of the year.

Common mistakes

  • Measuring runway from revenue instead of cash in the bank.
  • Treating run rate as a forecast. It is a snapshot, not a promise.
  • Cutting the spending that wins customers first. That shortens runway now and shrinks the business later.

Ye calculator kis kaam ka hai

CAGR (Compound Annual Growth Rate) un do cheezon ko track karta hai jo young businesses ko maarti hain: cash khatam hona, aur cash se zyada tez badhna. The smoothed-out average yearly growth over several years, ignoring ups and downs. Growth ek headline hai; cash oxygen hai.

Ganit kaise chalta hai

Burn matlab wo cash jo business har mahine kamai se zyada kharch karta hai. Runway matlab aapke paas ka cash divided by wo monthly burn — yaani kitne mahine baad paisa khatam. Run rate ek period ka revenue utha kar poore saal tak scale karta hai, jo compare karne me kaam ka hai lekin maanta hai ki wo period dohrayega. Break-even wo sales level hai jahan contribution aapki fixed costs cover kar deta hai, isliye profit zero hota hai aur har extra sale profit ban jaati hai.

Kab use karein

  • Jab aap jaanna chahte ho ki raise karne ya cut karne se pehle kitne mahine bache hain.
  • Jab aap decide kar rahe ho ki abhi hire karein ya ek quarter ruk jaayein.
  • Jab ek mahina asaadharan strong tha aur aap usse saal ke baaki hisson se theek se compare karna chahte ho.

Aam galtiyan

  • Runway ko revenue se naapna, bank me pade cash se nahi.
  • Run rate ko forecast maan lena. Ye ek snapshot hai, koi promise nahi.
  • Sabse pehle wahi kharch kaatna jo customers laata hai. Isse abhi runway chhota hota hai aur baad me business bhi.
