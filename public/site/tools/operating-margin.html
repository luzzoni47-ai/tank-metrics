What this calculator is for

Operating Margin shows what a business actually keeps, not what it collects. Revenue is the easy number and the least useful one on its own. How much of every rupee of sales turns into profit from your core business. This tool puts the costs back in, so you can see the margin that funds the next order, the next hire and the next bad month.

How the maths works

Profit is revenue minus every cost that went into earning it. Margin is that profit divided by revenue, shown as a percentage. A 20% margin means twenty paise of every rupee of sales stays with you; the rest is eaten by goods, ads, salaries, fees and tax. Change one cost and the margin moves, which is why the tool lets you vary each line separately rather than only the total.

When to reach for it

  • You are setting a price and want to know if the margin survives your real costs.
  • A month looked busy but the bank balance did not move, and you want to find out why.
  • You are comparing two products or two clients to decide where to put your effort.

Common mistakes

  • Counting revenue as profit. A crore of sales at a 2% margin is twenty lakh of profit — and one bad month can wipe it out.
  • Forgetting the costs that arrive late: GST, marketplace fees, returns, payment-gateway charges.
  • Setting a price by copying a competitor whose costs you cannot see.
