The margin is the number to watch. A high revenue with a thin margin leaves little room for a bad month, a discount war or a late payment.

What this calculator is for

Operating Margin answers whether the business makes money from trading, before the cost of the money it borrowed and the tax it owes. Revenue goes in, operating profit — what is left after the day-to-day costs of running the business — goes in, and the tool shows what share of each rupee of sales survives. It is the cleanest single read on whether the core operation works, because financing and one-off items stay out of it. What matters is what “operating profit” leaves out. Interest on a loan is a cost of how the business is funded, not of what it sells; a tax charge depends on the whole company's position; a one-time asset sale is not trading. Strip those away and the margin shows the engine on its own. That is why it sits beside EBITDA rather than replacing it: EBITDA adds depreciation and amortisation back, so the two move together for an asset-light business and part ways for a factory.

How the maths works

Operating Margin = Operating Profit ÷ Revenue × 100. Revenue (rev) is the top line; Operating Profit (ebit) is what remains after the cost of goods sold and the operating expenses, but before interest and tax. ₹18,000 of operating profit on ₹45,000 of revenue is a 40% margin. Above 10% usually signals a healthy core; a negative margin means the operation itself is losing money and no amount of financing will fix it.

When to reach for it

  • A busy month left little in the bank and you want to know whether the problem is the operation or the financing.
  • You are comparing your business with a competitor and want the one figure that ignores how each is funded.
  • You are preparing for a buyer or a lender and want to show the core business standing on its own.

Common mistakes

  • Putting net profit in the operating-profit field. Interest and tax are not operating costs, so using net profit understates the margin.
  • Comparing the margin across two companies without checking what each counts as operating. One may treat marketing as operating expense and another as a one-off.
  • Reading a single quarter as the trend. One-off costs and seasonality move the margin; trust the direction over several periods.

Ye calculator kis kaam ka hai

Operating Margin batata hai ki business apne trading se paisa bana raha hai ya nahi — us paise ki lagat aur tax se pehle jo usne udhaar liya. Revenue aur operating profit daalo, aur tool dikhata hai ki sales ke har rupee ka kitna hissa bacha. Core operation chalta hai ya nahi, iska ye sabse saaf single reading hai, kyunki financing aur one-off items isse bahar rehte hain. Farq ye hai ki “operating profit” kya chhodta hai. Loan ka interest is baat ki lagat hai ki business funded kaise hua, na ki wo kya bechta hai; tax company ki poori position par depend karta hai; ek baar wali asset sale trading nahi hai. Ye sab hata do to margin akela engine dikhata hai. Isliye ye EBITDA ke saath rehta hai, uski jagah nahi: EBITDA me depreciation aur amortisation wapas jod diye jaate hain, isliye asset-light business me dono saath chalte hain aur factory wale business me alag ho jaate hain.

Ganit kaise chalta hai

Operating Margin = Operating Profit ÷ Revenue × 100. Revenue (rev) top line hai; Operating Profit (ebit) wo hai jo cost of goods sold aur operating expenses ke baad bachta hai, lekin interest aur tax se pehle. ₹45,000 revenue par ₹18,000 operating profit matlab 40% margin. 10% se upar aam taur par healthy core maana jaata hai; negative margin matlab operation khud paisa gawa raha hai aur koi financing isko theek nahi kar sakti.

Kab use karein

  • Jab mahina busy tha par bank me kuch nahi bacha, aur aap jaanna chahte ho problem operation me hai ya financing me.
  • Jab aap apne business ko competitor se compare kar rahe ho aur wo ek number chahiye jo funding ko ignore kare.
  • Jab aap buyer ya lender ke liye tayyaar ho rahe ho aur core business ko alag dikhana chahte ho.

Aam galtiyan

  • Operating profit ki jagah net profit daal dena. Interest aur tax operating cost nahi hain, isliye margin kam dikhta hai.
  • Do company ka margin bina ye dekhe compare karna ki unme operating kya ginta hai. Ek marketing ko operating me rakhti hai, doosri one-off maanti hai.
  • Ek quarter ko trend maan lena. One-off cost aur seasonality se margin hilta rehta hai; direction par bharosa karo.
