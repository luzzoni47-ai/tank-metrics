What this calculator is for

A franchise or store ROI calculator answers the one question that decides whether to open: how long until I get my money back? The upfront cost of a store - franchise fee, deposit, fit-out, initial stock - is large and mostly sunk, and the monthly profit is what pays it back. An outlet that costs ₹25 lakh to open and makes ₹1.5 lakh a month takes about 17 months to pay back, and that is the number to compare against the franchise agreement's term.

How the maths works

Payback Period in months = Starting Investment ÷ Monthly Profit. A ₹25 lakh investment earning ₹1,50,000 a month pays back in about 17 months. Return on investment is the yearly profit divided by the investment: ₹18,00,000 ÷ ₹25,00,000 = 72% a year, which looks excellent until you check whether the ₹1.5 lakh is the realistic month or the best month. Payback past the agreement's term means the model does not work.

When to reach for it

  • You are evaluating a franchise offer and want the payback before signing.
  • You are comparing two locations with different rents and expected sales.
  • You are deciding whether to open a second outlet.

Common mistakes

  • Using the franchisor's projected sales instead of a conservative figure of your own.
  • Leaving working capital out of the investment. The store needs stock and cash before it earns.
  • Ignoring the royalty and marketing fees, which come off monthly profit before you see it.

Ye calculator kis kaam ka hai

Franchise ya store ROI calculator wahi ek sawaal ka jawab deta hai jo kholne ka faisla karta hai: paisa wapas aane mein kitna waqt lagega? Store ki upfront cost - franchise fee, deposit, fit-out, shuru ka stock - badi hoti hai aur zyadatar sunk, aur monthly profit usi ko wapas karta hai. ₹25 lakh mein khulne wala outlet jo ₹1.5 lakh mahina kamata hai, takreeban 17 mahine mein payback karta hai, aur yahi number franchise agreement ki term se compare karna hai.

Ganit kaise chalta hai

Payback Period mahino mein = Starting Investment ÷ Monthly Profit. ₹25 lakh investment, ₹1,50,000 mahina profit: takreeban 17 mahine mein payback. ROI matlab saalana profit divided by investment: ₹18,00,000 ÷ ₹25,00,000 = 72% saalana, jo shaandaar lagta hai jab tak check na karo ki ₹1.5 lakh realistic mahina hai ya best mahina. Agreement ki term se aage payback matlab model kaam nahi karta.

Kab use karein

  • Franchise offer evaluate kar rahe ho aur sign karne se pehle payback chahiye.
  • Do locations compare kar rahe ho jinke rent aur expected sales alag hain.
  • Doosra outlet kholna hai ya nahi ye decide kar rahe ho.

Aam galtiyan

  • Franchisor ke projected sales use karna, apne conservative figure ki jagah.
  • Working capital investment se bahar chhod dena. Store ko stock aur cash chahiye isse pehle ki woh kamaye.
  • Royalty aur marketing fees ignore karna, jo monthly profit se pehle nikal jaati hain.
