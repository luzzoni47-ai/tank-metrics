What this calculator is for

A PPF calculator shows what your yearly Public Provident Fund contributions become over the 15-year lock-in, and the answer is more than most people expect because PPF compounds tax-free. You put in a fixed amount each year, the balance earns interest that is fully exempt, and the maturity amount is tax-free too - which makes it one of the very few EEE instruments in India. The cost is patience: the money is locked for 15 years, and that is the whole trade.

How the maths works

The balance grows at the PPF rate compounded annually on each year's contribution, and the maturity value is the sum at the end of 15 years. ₹1.5 lakh a year at 7.1% for 15 years matures at roughly ₹40.7 lakh, of which about ₹22.5 lakh is your own contributions and the rest is tax-free interest. Contributing early in the financial year earns interest on that money for the whole year, so April beats March.

When to reach for it

  • You are deciding between PPF and an equity SIP for a long-term goal.
  • You want to know what your current yearly contribution becomes at maturity.
  • You are using PPF for the 80C deduction and want to see the full picture, not just the tax saving.

Common mistakes

  • Depositing in March instead of April. You lose a full year's interest on that contribution.
  • Comparing the 7.1% PPF rate with an equity fund's long-run return without accounting for the fact that PPF's return is guaranteed and tax-free.
  • Forgetting the ₹1.5 lakh yearly cap, which cannot be exceeded in a single account.

Ye calculator kis kaam ka hai

PPF calculator dikhata hai ki aapki saalana Public Provident Fund contributions 15 saal ke lock-in mein kya banti hain, aur jawab zyadatar logon ki expectation se zyada hota hai kyunki PPF tax-free compound hota hai. Aap har saal ek fixed amount daalte ho, balance par interest milta hai jo poora exempt hai, aur maturity amount bhi tax-free - isse yeh India ke un gine-chune EEE instruments mein se ek banta hai. Keemat sabr hai: paisa 15 saal ke liye lock hai, aur yahi poora trade hai.

Ganit kaise chalta hai

Balance PPF rate par saalana compound hota hai har saal ki contribution par, aur maturity value 15 saal ke ant mein kul hoti hai. ₹1.5 lakh saalana 7.1% par 15 saal: maturity takreeban ₹40.7 lakh, jisme takreeban ₹22.5 lakh aapki khud ki contribution hai aur baaki tax-free interest. Financial year mein jaldi daalne se us paise par poore saal ka interest milta hai, isliye April March se behtar hai.

Kab use karein

  • PPF aur equity SIP ke beech choose kar rahe ho long-term goal ke liye.
  • Aapki current saalana contribution maturity par kya banti hai ye jaanna hai.
  • 80C deduction ke liye PPF use kar rahe ho aur poora picture dekhna hai, sirf tax saving nahi.

Aam galtiyan

  • March ki jagah April mein deposit karna. Us contribution par poore saal ka interest chala jaata hai.
  • PPF ke 7.1% ko equity fund ke long-run return se compare karna bina ye ginne ki PPF ka return guaranteed aur tax-free hai.
  • ₹1.5 lakh saalana cap bhool jaana, jise ek account mein badhaya nahi ja sakta.
