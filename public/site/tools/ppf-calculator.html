What this calculator is for

The PPF Calculator answers a single 15-year question: if you commit the same amount every year to a Public Provident Fund account, what does the account hold when the lock-in ends? PPF is one of the very few instruments in India that is exempt at all three stages - the contribution (under Section 80C), the yearly interest, and the maturity amount - so the maturity figure this tool returns is money you actually keep, with nothing trimmed for tax afterwards. You enter Yearly Contribution, Annual Interest Rate and Tenure; it returns Total Contribution and Estimated Maturity. What separates PPF from a fixed deposit at the same rate is not the rate, it is the tax and the discipline. A 7.1% FD is taxed every year on its interest at your slab, so a 30% taxpayer keeps closer to 5%; PPF's 7.1% is never touched. This calculator lets you see that gap directly: run the same Yearly Contribution at the same rate and put the maturity next to a taxed deposit's post-tax value. The account also carries a ₹1.5 lakh yearly ceiling and a 15-year term that can be extended in five-year blocks, which is why Tenure is an input rather than a fixed 15.

How the maths works

Each year's contribution compounds at the PPF rate for every year it stays in the account, so the maturity is the future value of an ordinary annuity, not a simple multiplication. The tool sets a = rate / 100 and n = years, then Estimated Maturity = Yearly Contribution x ((1 + a)^n - 1) / a, and Total Contribution = Yearly Contribution x n. At ₹1,00,000 a year, 7% and 15 years, a = 0.07 and n = 15, so (1.07^15 - 1) / 0.07 = 25.129; maturity = ₹25,12,900 and Total Contribution = ₹15,00,000. The tool credits each year's deposit at the end of the year; in the real account an April contribution earns interest for the whole year, which the model does not capture, so treat its figure as the conservative end of the range.

When to reach for it

  • You are deciding where the ₹1.5 lakh of your Section 80C limit should go, and want the tax-free maturity of PPF beside the taxed maturity of an FD or a debt fund at the same rate.
  • You have been contributing to a PPF account for some years and want to see what the current Yearly Contribution grows to by the 15-year maturity date, before you decide whether to extend.
  • You are weighing PPF against an equity SIP for a goal 15 years out, and want the guaranteed, tax-free floor the PPF side of that comparison provides.

Common mistakes

  • Entering the interest you hope for rather than the current PPF rate. The rate is reset by the government every quarter, so the maturity moves when it does - the answer is only as good as the rate typed into Annual Interest Rate.
  • Reading Estimated Maturity as interest earned. That figure includes your own Total Contribution; the gain is Estimated Maturity minus Total Contribution, and that is the number to compare against an FD's post-tax interest.
  • Treating the 15-year figure as spendable at year 15 without planning the extension. PPF matures after 15 years but extends in five-year blocks, and the Tenure input is how you test that longer run.

Ye calculator kis kaam ka hai

PPF Calculator ek hi 15-saal ka sawaal hal karta hai: agar main har saal Public Provident Fund mein same amount daalta rahoon, to lock-in khatam hone par account mein kitna hota hai? PPF India ke un gine-chune instruments mein se ek hai jo teenon stages par exempt hai - contribution (Section 80C ke tehat), saalana interest, aur maturity amount - isliye ye maturity figure wahi paisa hai jo aap actually rakhte ho, baad mein tax ka koi kata nahi. Aap Yearly Contribution, Annual Interest Rate aur Tenure daalte ho; ye Total Contribution aur Estimated Maturity deta hai. PPF ko same rate ke FD se alag rate nahi, tax aur discipline banata hai. 7.1% ka FD har saal apne interest par aapke slab ke hisaab se tax deta hai, isliye 30% taxpayer ke paas 5% ke aas-paas bachta hai; PPF ke 7.1% ko koi nahi chhoota. Calculator ye gap seedha dikhata hai: same Yearly Contribution aur same rate par chalao aur maturity ko ek taxed deposit ki post-tax value se compare karo. Account par ₹1.5 lakh saalana ceiling hai aur 15-saal ka term jo paanch saal ke blocks mein badhaya ja sakta hai, isliye Tenure ek input hai, fixed 15 nahi.

Ganit kaise chalta hai

Har saal ki contribution PPF rate par utne saal compound hoti hai jitne wo account mein rehti hai, isliye maturity ek ordinary annuity ka future value hai, seedha multiplication nahi. Tool a = rate / 100 aur n = years set karta hai, phir Estimated Maturity = Yearly Contribution x ((1 + a)^n - 1) / a, aur Total Contribution = Yearly Contribution x n. ₹1,00,000 saalana, 7% aur 15 saal par a = 0.07 aur n = 15, to (1.07^15 - 1) / 0.07 = 25.129; maturity = ₹25,12,900 aur Total Contribution = ₹15,00,000. Calculator har saal ki deposit saal ke ant mein credit karta hai; asli account mein April ki contribution poore saal ka interest kamaati hai, jo tool model nahi karta, isliye figure ko conservative maano.

Kab use karein

  • Section 80C ke ₹1.5 lakh kahan lagayein ye decide kar rahe ho, aur PPF ki tax-free maturity ko same rate ke FD ya debt fund ki taxed maturity ke saath dekhna chahte ho.
  • Kuch saal se PPF mein daal rahe ho aur jaanna chahte ho ki current Yearly Contribution 15-saal ki maturity tak kya banta hai, extend karne se pehle.
  • 15 saal door ke goal ke liye PPF ko equity SIP se compare kar rahe ho, aur PPF taraf ka guaranteed, tax-free floor jaanna chahte ho.

Aam galtiyan

  • Jo interest expect kar rahe ho wo daalna, current PPF rate nahi. Rate har quarter government reset karti hai, isliye maturity bhi badalti hai - calculator utna hi sahi hai jitna Annual Interest Rate mein type kiya.
  • Estimated Maturity ko interest earned samajhna. Us figure mein aapki khud ki Total Contribution bhi hai; gain = Estimated Maturity minus Total Contribution, aur yahi FD ke post-tax interest se compare karne wala number hai.
  • 15 saal ka figure year 15 par kharch karne layak maan lena bina extension plan kiye. PPF 15 saal par mature hota hai par paanch saal ke blocks mein extend ho sakta hai, aur Tenure input se wahi lamba run test hota hai.
