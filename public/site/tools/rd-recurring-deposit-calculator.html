What this calculator is for

A recurring deposit is a monthly commitment to the same rate, with the bank paying interest on each instalment from the day it lands. 5,000 rupees a month for 5 years at 7% grows to about 3.6 lakh, of which 3 lakh is your own money. It is the deposit equivalent of a SIP, and the calculator's job is to show the interest separately from the contributions so the return is honest.

How the maths works

Maturity = sum of P x (1 + r / 4)^(quarters remaining), over each monthly instalment. Interest = Maturity - (P x months).

When to reach for it

  • You want a fixed-return monthly habit and want to know the maturity before committing.
  • You are comparing an RD with a debt mutual fund for a 3-year goal.
  • You want to see what the last instalment earns versus the first - the difference surprises people.

Common mistakes

  • Expecting the advertised rate to apply to the whole balance. Each instalment earns only for the time it is in.
  • Missing an instalment and assuming no penalty. Banks charge it, and it can reset the rate.
  • Using an RD for a 10-year goal. Compounding equity usually wins over that horizon.

Ye calculator kis kaam ka hai

Recurring deposit har mahine ek hi rate par commitment hai, aur bank har instalment par us din se interest deta hai. 5,000 rupaye mahina 5 saal 7% par lagbhag 3.6 lakh banta hai, jisme 3 lakh aapka hi paisa hai. Ye deposits ka SIP jaisa hai, aur calculator ka kaam interest ko contributions se alag dikhana hai taaki return imaandaar lage.

Ganit kaise chalta hai

Maturity = sum of P x (1 + r / 4)^(bache quarters), har monthly instalment par. Interest = Maturity - (P x mahine).

Kab use karein

  • Aapko fixed-return monthly habit chahiye aur commit karne se pehle maturity jaanna hai.
  • 3 saal ke goal ke liye RD ko debt mutual fund se compare kar rahe ho.
  • Aap dekhna chahte ho ki aakhri instalment pehli se kitna kamati hai - farak logon ko chauka deta hai.

Aam galtiyan

  • Advertised rate poore balance par lagne ki ummeed rakhna. Har instalment sirf utne hi time kamata hai jitna wo andar hai.
  • Instalment miss karke penalty na maan lena. Bank lagata hai, aur rate reset ho sakta hai.
  • 10 saal ke goal ke liye RD use karna. Itne horizon par equity compounding usually jeet jati hai.
