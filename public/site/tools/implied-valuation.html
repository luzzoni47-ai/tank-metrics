What this calculator is for

Implied Valuation works through the arithmetic of a funding round or a company valuation. When someone says "50 lakh for 5%", find out what that says the whole company is worth. These are the numbers founders and investors argue about, and most of the arguments come down to one of the figures this tool calculates.

How the maths works

Valuation is a price agreed for the whole company. Pre-money is that price before new money goes in; post-money is after. When an investor puts in an amount for a stake, the post-money is the investment divided by the stake, and the pre-money is post-money minus the investment. Revenue multiples, EBITDA multiples and implied values are different ways of arriving at the same price from the company's own numbers.

When to reach for it

  • You are preparing for a fundraise and want to know what your numbers imply.
  • You have a term sheet and want to check the stake it actually gives away.
  • You are comparing your valuation against what similar companies raised at.

Common mistakes

  • Reading a valuation without the stake. A high valuation that costs half the company is worse than a lower one that costs a tenth.
  • Comparing a revenue multiple for a fast grower with one for a slow, profitable business.
  • Forgetting that a post-money valuation includes the new money, so the founder's percentage falls.
