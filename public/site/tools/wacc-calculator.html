What this calculator is for

WACC is the blended cost of every rupee a company uses, whether it came from shareholders or from a lender. Equity is not free: the people who put it in expect a return, and that expectation is a real cost to the business. Debt is cheaper twice over, because interest is paid before tax and the tax man effectively subsidises part of it. WACC weights the two costs by how much of the capital structure each one is, and produces the single discount rate a discounted cash flow valuation starts from. If a project cannot beat the WACC, it is destroying value even when it shows an accounting profit.

How the maths works

WACC = (E / V) x Cost of Equity + (D / V) x Cost of Debt x (1 - Tax Rate), where V is equity plus debt. On 90,00,000 of equity at 18% and 30,00,000 of debt at 12% with a 25% tax rate, the weights are 75% and 25%, and WACC = 0.75 x 18 + 0.25 x 12 x 0.75 = 15.75%. Note the tax shield: the debt's real cost is 9%, not 12%, which is why debt pulls the blended rate down.

When to reach for it

  • You are building a DCF and need the discount rate to bring future cash flows back to today.
  • You are weighing whether to fund a project with debt or fresh equity.
  • You want to check whether a quoted project return actually clears the cost of the capital it consumes.

Common mistakes

  • Using book value of equity instead of market value. The shareholder's cost is based on what the stake is worth, not what it was issued at.
  • Forgetting the tax shield on debt, which overstates the cost of capital and understates every valuation.
  • Treating cost of equity as the risk-free rate. It carries an equity premium for a reason.

Ye calculator kis kaam ka hai

WACC har us rupaye ki mili-juli cost hai jo company use karti hai, chahe wo shareholders se aaya ho ya lender se. Equity free nahi hoti: jo log paisa lagate hain wo return expect karte hain, aur wo expectation business ke liye asli cost hai. Debt do tarah se sasti hai, kyunki interest tax se pehle diate hai aur tax wala hissa uska ek hissa effectively subsidise kar deta hai. WACC in dono costs ko is hisaab se weight karta hai ki capital structure me har ek kitna hai, aur wahi ek discount rate nikalta hai jisse discounted cash flow valuation shuru hoti hai. Agar koi project WACC ko beat nahi kar paata, to wo accounting profit dikhane ke baad bhi value kha raha hai.

Ganit kaise chalta hai

WACC = (E / V) x Cost of Equity + (D / V) x Cost of Debt x (1 - Tax Rate), jahan V equity plus debt hai. 90,00,000 equity par 18% aur 30,00,000 debt par 12%, 25% tax rate ke saath, weights 75% aur 25% bante hain, aur WACC = 0.75 x 18 + 0.25 x 12 x 0.75 = 15.75%. Tax shield note karo: debt ki asli cost 9% hai, 12% nahi, aur isi wajah se debt blended rate ko neeche kheenchti hai.

Kab use karein

  • Jab aap DCF bana rahe ho aur future cash flows ko aaj tak laane ke liye discount rate chahiye.
  • Jab aap soch rahe ho ki project ko debt se fund karein ya nayi equity se.
  • Jab aap check karna chahte ho ki bataya gaya project return us capital ki cost ko clear karta hai ya nahi jo wo kha raha hai.

Aam galtiyan

  • Equity ki book value use karna market value ke bajaye. Shareholder ki cost is par hai ki stake ki value kitni hai, ye nahi ki wo kis rate par issue hui thi.
  • Debt ka tax shield bhool jaana, jo cost of capital ko badha deta hai aur har valuation ko kam kar deta hai.
  • Cost of equity ko risk-free rate maan lena. Usme equity premium hota hai, kisi wajah se.
