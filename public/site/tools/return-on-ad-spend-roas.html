These are the numbers investors ask about first. If the cost of winning a customer is close to what that customer is worth, growth burns cash instead of creating it.

What this calculator is for

Return on Ad Spend tells you how many rupees of revenue came back for every rupee spent on ads. Divide revenue from ads by the ad spend: ₹1,00,000 of revenue from ₹20,000 of ads is a ROAS of 5x. It is the fastest read on whether a campaign is working, but revenue is not profit. A 5x ROAS on a product with a thin margin can still lose money once cost of goods, shipping and returns are paid. Use ROAS to screen campaigns, then check the margin before scaling.

How the maths works

ROAS = Revenue from Ads ÷ Ad Spend. ₹1,00,000 ÷ ₹20,000 = 5x. To turn it into a break-even test, divide 1 by your gross margin: at a 25% margin you need a ROAS above 4x just to cover the cost of the product, before any profit. A high ROAS on a low-margin product can still be a loss.

When to reach for it

  • You are checking whether a campaign is worth scaling.
  • You are comparing channels with different costs and returns.
  • You are setting a target ROAS for an agency or a media buyer.

Common mistakes

  • Treating revenue-based ROAS as profit - it ignores cost of goods, shipping and returns.
  • Comparing ROAS across products with different margins as if the number were comparable.
  • Scaling on a good ROAS without checking that the extra orders can actually be fulfilled.

Ye calculator kis kaam ka hai

Return on Ad Spend batata hai ki ads pe kharch hue har rupee pe kitne rupee revenue wapas aaye. Ads se aayi revenue ko ad spend se divide karo: ₹20,000 ads se ₹1,00,000 revenue matlab ROAS 5x. Patli margin wale product pe 5x ROAS bhi cost of goods, shipping aur returns ke baad paisa kha sakta hai. ROAS se campaign screen karo, phir scale karne se pehle margin check karo.

Ganit kaise chalta hai

ROAS = Revenue from Ads ÷ Ad Spend. ₹1,00,000 ÷ ₹20,000 = 5x. Isko break-even test banane ke liye 1 ko gross margin se divide karo: 25% margin pe product ki cost cover karne ke liye hi ROAS 4x se upar chahiye, profit se pehle. Low-margin product pe high ROAS bhi loss ho sakta hai.

Kab use karein

  • Campaign scale karne layak hai ya nahi, ye check kar rahe ho.
  • Alag cost aur return wale channels compare kar rahe ho.
  • Agency ya media buyer ke liye target ROAS set kar rahe ho.

Aam galtiyan

  • Revenue-based ROAS ko profit maan lena - ye cost of goods, shipping aur returns ignore karta hai.
  • Alag margin wale products ka ROAS aapas me compare karna jaise number ek jaisa ho.
  • Achhe ROAS pe scale kar dena, ye check kiye bina ki extra orders fulfil ho sakte hain.
