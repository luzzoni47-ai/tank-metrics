What this calculator is for

Break-Even Point answers one question before you spend a rupee: how much must this business sell before it stops losing money? You give the tool three numbers — the fixed costs you pay whether you sell anything or not, the selling price of one unit, and the variable cost of making or buying that unit — and it returns the units and the revenue at which profit is exactly zero. Everything above that line is profit; everything below it is a loss you are funding from your own pocket. The number it produces is not a target, it is a floor. A Pune cafe paying ₹1,80,000 a month in rent, salaries and utilities, selling a ₹250 thali whose ingredients and packaging cost ₹150, has ₹100 of contribution per plate and must serve 1,800 plates a month just to stand still — about 60 a day. Read it that way and the question stops being abstract: is 60 a day realistic on a Tuesday in monsoon?

How the maths works

Contribution per unit is Selling Price minus Variable Cost, so ₹250 − ₹150 = ₹100. Break-Even Units is Fixed Costs ÷ Contribution: ₹1,80,000 ÷ ₹100 = 1,800 units. Break-Even Revenue is those units at the selling price, 1,800 × ₹250 = ₹4,50,000 a month. The tool calls the three inputs fixed, sp and vc and shows Break-Even Units and Break-Even Revenue. Every lever lives in the denominator: cut variable cost by ₹20 and break-even falls to 1,500 units, raise the price ₹20 and it falls to the same place, but each move costs you something with customers.

When to reach for it

  • You are about to sign a lease, hire, or take a loan and need the sales floor those fixed costs demand.
  • An investor or lender asks how much you must sell before the business stops bleeding.
  • You are choosing between two price-and-cost combinations and want to see which one reaches break-even sooner.

Common mistakes

  • Leaving fixed costs out. Rent, salaries, software subscriptions and loan interest are owed whether you sell one unit or none; a partial list flatters the break-even badly.
  • Using total revenue where contribution belongs. Break-even turns on what each unit leaves after its own variable cost, not on the size of the sale.
  • Treating break-even as the goal. At break-even you have made nothing; it is the gate you pass through before any profit exists.

Ye calculator kis kaam ka hai

Break-Even Point wo sales level batata hai jahan se pehle business paisa kha raha hai aur jahan ke baad kamane lagta hai. Tool ko teen numbers do — fixed costs jo har mahine dene hi padte hain, ek unit ka selling price, aur usi unit ka variable cost — aur ye bata deta hai kitni units aur kitni revenue par profit exactly zero hai. Is line ke upar sab profit hai, is line ke neeche jo bhi bikta hai wo aapki jeb se loss bhara ja raha hai. Ye number target nahi, floor hai. Ek Pune cafe ka rent, salary aur bijli milakar ₹1,80,000 mahina hai, aur wo ₹250 ka thali bechta hai jiska material aur packing ₹150 padta hai — yaani ₹100 contribution per plate. Break-even ke liye 1,800 plates mahina chahiye, roz lagbhag 60. Ye line padhne ke baad sawal abstract nahi rehta: monsoon ke Tuesday ko 60 plates bik sakte hain ya nahi?

Ganit kaise chalta hai

Contribution per unit = Selling Price − Variable Cost, yaani ₹250 − ₹150 = ₹100. Break-Even Units = Fixed Costs ÷ Contribution = ₹1,80,000 ÷ ₹100 = 1,800 units. Break-Even Revenue = 1,800 × ₹250 = ₹4,50,000 mahina. Tool ke inputs fixed, sp aur vc hain, aur output Break-Even Units aur Break-Even Revenue. Har lever denominator mein hai: variable cost ₹20 ghatane par break-even 1,500 units par aa jaata hai, price ₹20 badhane par bhi wahi hota hai — bas dono ka apna customer cost hai.

Kab use karein

  • Lease, hiring ya loan sign karne se pehle, aur dekhna hai ki wo fixed cost kitni sales maangti hai.
  • Investor ya lender poochta hai ki ghata band karne ke liye kitna bechna padega.
  • Do price aur cost ke options mein se chunna hai ki kaunsa pehle break-even karega.

Aam galtiyan

  • Kuch fixed costs chhod dena. Rent, salary, subscriptions aur loan interest tab bhi dena padta hai jab ek unit bhi na bike; adhoori list break-even ko jhoothi tasalli deti hai.
  • Contribution ki jagah total revenue use karna. Break-even har unit apni variable cost ke baad kitna chhodti hai, uspar tika hai.
  • Break-even ko hi goal samajhna. Break-even par kuch nahi kamaya; wo sirf wo darwaza hai jahan se profit shuru hota hai.
