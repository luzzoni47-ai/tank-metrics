Break-Even Point settles it with Contribution per unit is the selling price minus the variable cost of that unit: sp − vc. Change any field above and the answer updates with it.

What this calculator is for

Break-Even Point turns three monthly facts about one product into the number a first-time founder can act on today: how many units must leave the shelf before the month stops costing you money. fixed is everything that lands whether or not a single sale happens — shop rent, staff salary, the machine EMI, the software subscription, the deposit you are slowly writing off. sp is what the customer pays for one unit, and vc is what that same unit costs the moment it is sold: material, the tailor's charge, packaging, the courier, the three percent the payment app keeps. The tool divides fixed by sp − vc and returns Break-Even Units, then multiplies back up for Break-Even Revenue, which is the version to quote to a landlord, a lender or a franchisee. The answer is a floor, not a forecast, and it is only a plan if the floor fits inside your capacity. Divide 250 units by 26 working days and the real target appears: about 10 a day, a number you can test against a Tuesday afternoon. Because the arithmetic runs on one contribution per unit, it assumes one product at one price; a shop selling fifty items at fifty margins is asking a mix question, and the honest way to answer it is to weight each product's contribution by its share of sales and feed that average in as vc. Above the floor every extra unit adds exactly the contribution to profit, which is why the number after break-even is a straight line and not a curve.

How the maths works

Contribution per unit = sp − vc. Break-Even Units = fixed ÷ (sp − vc). Break-Even Revenue = Break-Even Units × sp. With fixed ₹1,00,000, sp ₹1,000 and vc ₹600 the contribution is ₹400, so the floor is 1,00,000 ÷ 400 = 250 units and 250 × ₹1,000 = ₹2,50,000 of revenue — roughly 10 units a working day. The three levers are not equal. Cutting vc by ₹100 lifts contribution to ₹500 and drops the floor to 200 units, exactly as raising sp to ₹1,100 does, but one needs a supplier renegotiation and the other needs the market to accept a higher price. Cutting fixed by ₹20,000 to ₹80,000 also lands on 200 units, and it is the only lever completely inside your control.

When to reach for it

  • You are about to sign a shop lease, hire the first employee or take on the machine EMI, and you want the monthly sales figure that has to follow that signature.
  • A lender or an angel asks how much you must sell before the business stops burning money, and you need a number you can defend with three inputs instead of a story.
  • Two ways of making the same product are on the table — a cheaper supplier with a big minimum order, or a premium finish — and you want to see which one reaches the floor with fewer units.

Common mistakes

  • Typing a GST-inclusive selling price. sp should be the amount you actually keep before tax, so a ₹1,180 sticker at 18% GST is sp ₹1,000; leave the tax in and the floor comes out roughly 15% too easy.
  • Putting the month's material bill in vc instead of one unit's cost. A ₹60,000 bill entered where ₹600 belongs makes break-even read 1.7 units, and a plan that easy is never tested.
  • Leaving out the costs that appear only when a sale happens. Marketplace commission, shipping and gateway fees are variable by definition and belong in vc; omitted, the floor is understated by whatever those take, often a tenth of the price.
  • Reading break-even revenue as cash break-even. GST collected, a customer deposit or loan principal repayment sit outside contribution, so a shop can clear 250 units and still be short of cash in the same month.

Ye calculator kis kaam ka hai

Break-Even Point teen mahine ke facts ko ek aise number mein badal deta hai jise pehla founder aaj hi use kar sake: mahina paisa kharch karna band karne se pehle kitni units bikni chahiye. fixed wo sab hai jo ek bhi sale na ho tab bhi jaata hai — dukaan ka rent, staff ki salary, machine ki EMI, software ka subscription, deposit jo dheere-dheere likha ja raha hai. sp ek unit ka customer price hai, aur vc usi unit ka cost sale hote hi — material, tailor ka charge, packing, courier, payment app ka teen percent. Tool fixed ko sp − vc se divide karta hai aur Break-Even Units deta hai, phir wapas multiply karke Break-Even Revenue — yahi version landlord, lender ya franchisee ko dikhana chahiye. Jawab ek floor hai, forecast nahi, aur plan tabhi hai jab floor aapki capacity ke andar ho. 250 units ko 26 working days se divide karo to asli target dikhta hai: roz lagbhag 10 — ek Tuesday dopahar se compare karne layak number. Ganit ek hi contribution per unit par chalta hai, isliye ye ek product, ek price maanta hai; pachaas alag margins wali dukaan asli mein mix ka sawaal poochti hai, aur uska imaandaar jawab ye hai ki har product ke contribution ko uski sale share se weight karke average ko vc mein daalo. Floor ke upar har extra unit poora contribution profit mein jodti hai, isliye break-even ke baad ka line seedha hota hai, curve nahi.

Ganit kaise chalta hai

Contribution per unit = sp − vc. Break-Even Units = fixed ÷ (sp − vc). Break-Even Revenue = Break-Even Units × sp. fixed ₹1,00,000, sp ₹1,000 aur vc ₹600 par contribution ₹400 hai, to floor 1,00,000 ÷ 400 = 250 units aur 250 × ₹1,000 = ₹2,50,000 revenue — roz lagbhag 10 units. Teen lever barabar nahi hain. vc se ₹100 ghatane par contribution ₹500 ho jaata hai aur floor 200 units, bilkul waise hi jaise sp ko ₹1,100 karne par; par ek mein supplier se dobara baat karni hai, doosre mein market ko mehnga price manana hai. fixed ko ₹20,000 ghata kar ₹80,000 karne par bhi 200 units aate hain, aur yahi ek lever hai jo poori tarah aapke haath mein hai.

Kab use karein

  • Dukaan ka lease, pehla employee ya machine ki EMI sign karne wale ho, aur jaanna hai ki us signature ke baad mahine ki kitni sale zaroori hai.
  • Lender ya angel poochta hai ki paisa jalna band karne se pehle kitna bechna padega, aur aapko kahani ki jagah teen inputs se defend karne wala number chahiye.
  • Ek hi product banane ke do tareeke saamne hain — sasta supplier bade minimum order ke saath, ya premium finish — aur dekhna hai kaunsa kam units mein floor tak pahunchta hai.

Aam galtiyan

  • GST ke saath wala selling price daal dena. sp wo amount hona chahiye jo tax se pehle aapke paas rehta hai, to 18% GST par ₹1,180 ka sticker sp ₹1,000 hai; tax andar chhoda to floor lagbhag 15% aasaan dikhta hai.
  • vc mein ek unit ki jagah poore mahine ka material bill daalna. ₹600 ki jagah ₹60,000 daalne par break-even 1.7 units aata hai, aur itna aasaan plan kabhi test nahi hota.
  • Sirf sale hone par lagne wali cost chhod dena. Marketplace commission, shipping aur gateway fee definition se variable hain aur vc mein aate hain; chhod dene se floor utna kam dikhta hai, aksar price ka dasva hissa.
  • Break-Even Revenue ko cash break-even samajhna. GST, customer deposit ya loan ki principal repayment contribution se bahar hote hain, to 250 units ke baad bhi usi mahine cash ki kami reh sakti hai.
