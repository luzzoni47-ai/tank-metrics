What this calculator is for

Dilution is the slice of the company a founder gives up in a deal, and the slice that is left. If you own 100% today and hand an investor 10%, you keep 90% - but the number that matters is what that 10% buys. Dilution compounds: a 10% round, then a 20% round, then an ESOP pool, and a founder who started at 100% can end up below half. This calculator shows the ownership after a single round, so the cost of a deal is visible before it is signed, not after.

How the maths works

Founder Equity After = Founder Equity Before − Equity Offered. 100% − 10% = 90%. Across several rounds, multiply the fractions rather than subtracting the percentages: 90% after round one, then a further 20% off leaves 72%, not 70%. The multiplication is why dilution surprises founders - each round is charged against a smaller base.

When to reach for it

  • You have a term sheet and want to see your ownership after the stake.
  • You are planning two rounds and want to know where you land.
  • You are setting an ESOP pool and need to see its effect on founders.

Common mistakes

  • Subtracting percentages across rounds instead of multiplying fractions, which understates the loss.
  • Forgetting the ESOP pool, which dilutes founders exactly like an investor does.
  • Looking at the stake alone without the valuation - giving 20% at a high price can cost less than 5% at a low one.

Ye calculator kis kaam ka hai

Dilution matlab deal me founder ka kitna hissa chala jaata hai, aur kitna bachta hai. Aaj 100% aapka hai aur investor ko 10% diya toh 90% aapke paas - par asli baat ye hai ki wo 10% kya khareedta hai. Dilution compound karti hai: 10% round, phir 20% round, phir ESOP pool - aur 100% se shuru karne wala founder aadhe se neeche pahunch sakta hai. Ye calculator ek round ke baad ki ownership dikhata hai, taaki deal ki keemat sign se pehle dikhe, baad me nahi.

Ganit kaise chalta hai

Founder Equity After = Founder Equity Before − Equity Offered. 100% − 10% = 90%. Kai rounds me percentages ghatane ke bajaye fractions multiply karo: pehle round ke baad 90%, phir 20% aur gaya toh 72% bachta hai, 70% nahi. Yahi multiplication founders ko chaunkata hai - har round chhoti base pe lagta hai.

Kab use karein

  • Term sheet aaya hai aur stake ke baad apni ownership dekhni hai.
  • Do rounds plan kar rahe ho aur pata karna hai kahan pahunchoge.
  • ESOP pool set kar rahe ho aur founders pe uska asar dekhna hai.

Aam galtiyan

  • Rounds ke beech percentages ghatana, fractions multiply karne ke bajaye - isse nuksaan kam dikhta hai.
  • ESOP pool bhool jaana, jo founders ko bilkul investor ki tarah dilute karta hai.
  • Sirf stake dekhna valuation ke bina - high price pe 20% dena, low price pe 5% se kam pad sakta hai.
