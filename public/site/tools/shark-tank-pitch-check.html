What this calculator is for

A Shark Tank pitch check runs your numbers against the questions the sharks actually ask, and tells you which ones are likely to get you grilled. On Shark Tank India the interrogation is remarkably consistent: what is your valuation and why, what are your revenue and margins, what does it cost to get a customer, and how many months of cash do you have left? A pitch that has clean answers to those four is a pitch that survives the room.

How the maths works

There is no single formula - the check looks at ratios. Valuation against revenue shows how expensive the ask is. LTV against CAC shows whether growth is profitable; below 3 to 1 the sharks push back. Runway in months shows how much time the business has. Gross margin shows whether the product can fund its own growth. A ₹10 crore valuation on ₹1 crore of revenue is a 10x multiple, and the pitch has to justify it.

When to reach for it

  • You are preparing a pitch and want to know your weak numbers first.
  • An investor has asked for your metrics and you want to sanity-check them.
  • You are deciding what to fix before you raise.

Common mistakes

  • Walking in with a valuation and no revenue to support it. The multiple is the first thing questioned.
  • Not knowing your CAC and LTV when asked, which reads as not knowing your own business.
  • Quoting a burn rate that implies a short runway without a plan for it.

Ye calculator kis kaam ka hai

Shark Tank pitch check aapke numbers ko un sawaalon par chalata hai jo sharks actually poochte hain, aur batata hai kaunse sawaalon par aapko grilling milegi. Shark Tank India par jaanch-paraakh kaafi consistent hai: aapki valuation kya hai aur kyun, revenue aur margins kya hain, customer paane mein kitna lagta hai, aur kitne mahine ka cash bacha hai? Jis pitch ke paas in chaaron ke saaf jawab hain, woh room mein tik jaati hai.

Ganit kaise chalta hai

Koi ek formula nahi hai - check ratios dekhta hai. Valuation vs revenue batata hai ask kitna mehnga hai. LTV vs CAC batata hai growth profitable hai ya nahi; 3:1 se neeche sharks push back karte hain. Runway mahino mein batata hai business ke paas kitna waqt hai. Gross margin batata hai ki product apni growth fund kar sakta hai ya nahi. ₹1 crore revenue par ₹10 crore valuation 10x multiple hai, aur pitch ko woh justify karna padta hai.

Kab use karein

  • Pitch taiyaar kar rahe ho aur pehle apne kamzor numbers jaanna chahte ho.
  • Investor ne metrics maange hain aur unhe sanity-check karna hai.
  • Raise karne se pehle kya theek karna hai ye decide kar rahe ho.

Aam galtiyan

  • Valuation lekar aana jise support karne ke liye revenue nahi hai. Multiple sabse pehle poochha jaata hai.
  • CAC aur LTV pata na hona jab poocha jaaye, jisse lagta hai apna business nahi jaante.
  • Aisa burn rate batana jisse runway chhota nikle aur uske liye plan na ho.
