What this calculator is for

Shark Tank Pitch Check puts a pitch through the same checks an investor would run. Enter your numbers and see which ones the Sharks are likely to question. It is a rehearsal, not a verdict — the point is to find the weak number before the meeting does.

How the maths works

Each figure in the pitch is scored against a range that investors treat as normal: growth against what similar companies do, margin against the category, runway against the time a raise takes, and the ask against what the traction justifies. The tool combines the scores and names the lines that pull the total down.

When to reach for it

  • You have a deck and want a second opinion before you send it.
  • You are practising the pitch and want to know which slide will draw questions.
  • You are deciding whether you are ready to raise or should wait a quarter.

Common mistakes

  • Reading a low score as a rejection. It is a list of things to fix or explain.
  • Tuning the inputs until the score looks good, which defeats the purpose.
  • Ignoring the weakest line because the total is high.
