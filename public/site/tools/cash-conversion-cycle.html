What this calculator is for

The cash conversion cycle is how many days your money is stuck in the business between paying for stock and getting paid by customers. A business can be profitable on paper and still run out of cash, and this is usually why. You buy inventory, hold it, sell it on credit, and only then collect. Every day in that chain is a day your money is working for someone else. Cutting the cycle is often faster than growing sales.

How the maths works

CCC = Days Inventory + Days Receivables − Days Payables. If stock sits for 60 days, customers take 45 days to pay, and you pay suppliers in 30 days, the cycle is 60 + 45 − 30 = 75 days. That means 75 days of costs have to be funded from somewhere - usually your own pocket or a loan. Negotiating 45-day supplier terms instead of 30 cuts the cycle to 60 days with no extra sales at all.

When to reach for it

  • You are profitable but constantly short of cash and want to see where it is stuck.
  • You are negotiating payment terms with a supplier or a large customer.
  • You are planning how much working capital you need before the busy season.

Common mistakes

  • Measuring the cycle on the best customer and not the average one. One slow payer can carry the whole number.
  • Extending payables to improve the ratio without noticing that suppliers then raise prices or stop supplying.
  • Confusing profit with cash. A profitable business with a 90-day cycle can still fail.

Ye calculator kis kaam ka hai

Cash conversion cycle batata hai ki stock ke liye paisa dene se lekar customers se paisa aane tak aapka paisa business mein kitne din phasa rehta hai. Business kagaz par profitable ho sakta hai aur phir bhi cash khatam ho sakta hai - aur aksar yahi wajah hoti hai. Aap inventory kharidte ho, rakhte ho, credit par bechte ho, aur tab jaakar collect karte ho. Us chain ka har din aapke paise ka din hai jo kisi aur ke liye kaam kar raha hai. Cycle ghataana aksar sales badhane se tez hota hai.

Ganit kaise chalta hai

CCC = Days Inventory + Days Receivables − Days Payables. Stock 60 din padha, customers 45 din mein pay karte hain, aur aap suppliers ko 30 din mein dete ho, toh cycle 60 + 45 − 30 = 75 din. Matlab 75 din ki cost kahin se fund karni padegi - aksar apni jeb se ya loan se. Supplier terms 30 ki jagah 45 kar do toh cycle 60 din ho jaata hai, bina ek rupee extra sales ke.

Kab use karein

  • Profitable ho par hamesha cash ki kami rahe, aur dekhna hai paisa kahaan phasa hai.
  • Supplier ya bade customer ke saath payment terms negotiate kar rahe ho.
  • Busy season se pehle kitna working capital chahiye ye plan kar rahe ho.

Aam galtiyan

  • Cycle best customer par measure karna, average par nahi. Ek slow payer poora number kheench leta hai.
  • Ratio sudharne ke liye payables badhana bina ye dekhe ki suppliers phir price badha dete hain ya supply band kar dete hain.
  • Profit ko cash samajh lena. 90-din cycle wala profitable business bhi doob sakta hai.
