The margin is the number to watch. A high revenue with a thin margin leaves little room for a bad month, a discount war or a late payment.

What this calculator is for

Contribution Margin shows what a business actually keeps, not what it collects. Revenue is the easy number and the least useful one on its own. How much each unit you sell contributes towards covering your fixed costs. This tool puts the costs back in, so you can see the margin that funds the next order, the next hire and the next bad month.

How the maths works

Profit is revenue minus every cost that went into earning it. Margin is that profit divided by revenue, shown as a percentage. A 20% margin means twenty paise of every rupee of sales stays with you; the rest is eaten by goods, ads, salaries, fees and tax. Change one cost and the margin moves, which is why the tool lets you vary each line separately rather than only the total.

When to reach for it

  • You are setting a price and want to know if the margin survives your real costs.
  • A month looked busy but the bank balance did not move, and you want to find out why.
  • You are comparing two products or two clients to decide where to put your effort.

Common mistakes

  • Counting revenue as profit. A crore of sales at a 2% margin is twenty lakh of profit — and one bad month can wipe it out.
  • Forgetting the costs that arrive late: GST, marketplace fees, returns, payment-gateway charges.
  • Setting a price by copying a competitor whose costs you cannot see.

Ganit kaise chalta hai

Profit matlab revenue se har wo cost ghata do jo use kamane me lagi. Margin matlab wahi profit revenue se divide, percentage me. 20% margin ka matlab hai ki har rupee ki sale me bees paise aapke paas rehte hain; baaki goods, ads, salaries, fees aur tax kha jaate hain. Ek cost badlo to margin hilta hai, isliye tool har line alag-alag badalne deta hai, sirf total nahi.

Kab use karein

  • Jab aap price set kar rahe ho aur dekhna chahte ho ki asli costs ke baad margin bachta hai ya nahi.
  • Jab mahina busy laga lekin bank balance nahi badla, aur aap wajah dhoondhna chahte ho.
  • Jab do products ya do clients compare kar rahe ho ye decide karne ke liye ki mehnat kahan lagayein.

Aam galtiyan

  • Revenue ko profit maan lena. 2% margin par ek crore ki sale ka matlab hai bees lakh profit — aur ek kharab mahina use mita sakta hai.
  • Der se aane wali costs bhool jaana: GST, marketplace fees, returns, payment-gateway charges.
  • Aise competitor ko dekh kar price set karna jiski costs aapko dikhti hi nahi.
