What this calculator is for

The EBITDA Multiple divides a valuation by EBITDA — earnings before interest, tax, depreciation and amortisation. It is the multiple most small-business deals are actually priced on, because it ignores how the current owner financed the business and how the accountant spread the cost of equipment. Two identical shops with different loans and different depreciation policies show the same EBITDA, so the multiple compares like with like.

How the maths works

EBITDA Multiple = Valuation ÷ EBITDA. The tool divides the valuation you enter by the EBITDA figure and shows the result in times. A 6x multiple means the buyer pays six rupees for every one rupee of yearly operating profit. Because EBITDA is a profit measure and revenue is not, this multiple is always lower than the revenue multiple for the same deal.

When to reach for it

  • You are selling or buying a business and want a price that a lender or an investor will recognise as normal.
  • You are comparing an offer against the 4-8x range most small business deals fall in.
  • You want to check what EBITDA the business must reach for the asking price to be reasonable.

Common mistakes

  • Adding back costs that are real. A one-off saving the owner made by not paying himself is not a permanent add-back, and buyers discount it.
  • Comparing an EBITDA multiple to a revenue multiple. They measure different things; a 6x EBITDA price is not 'cheaper' than a 3x revenue price without the margin between them.
  • Using a stale EBITDA. One good year lifts the multiple's denominator and makes an average business look cheap.

Ye calculator kis kaam ka hai

EBITDA Multiple valuation ko EBITDA se divide karta hai — earnings before interest, tax, depreciation aur amortisation. Chhote business deals me zyadatar price isi multiple par tay hota hai, kyunki ye ignore karta hai ki current owner ne business ko kaise finance kiya aur accountant ne equipment ki cost kaise phailayi. Do same dukaan, alag loan aur alag depreciation policy ke saath, ek hi EBITDA dikhati hain, isliye multiple like-to-like compare karta hai.

Ganit kaise chalta hai

EBITDA Multiple = Valuation ÷ EBITDA. Tool aapki valuation ko EBITDA se divide karke times me dikhata hai. 6x ka matlab hai buyer saalana operating profit ke har ek rupaye par chhah rupaye de raha hai. Kyunki EBITDA profit ka measure hai aur revenue nahi, isliye same deal par ye multiple revenue multiple se hamesha kam hota hai.

Kab use karein

  • Jab aap business bech ya kharid rahe ho aur aisa price chahiye jo lender ya investor ko normal lage.
  • Jab aap kisi offer ko 4-8x range se compare kar rahe ho jisme zyadatar chhote business deals aate hain.
  • Jab aap dekhna chahte ho ki asking price sahi lagne ke liye business ko kitna EBITDA chahiye.

Aam galtiyan

  • Aisi costs add back karna jo asli hain. Owner ne khud ko salary na dekar jo one-off bachat ki, wo permanent add-back nahi hoti, aur buyers usse discount karte hain.
  • EBITDA multiple ko revenue multiple se compare karna. Dono alag cheez naapte hain; 6x EBITDA price, 3x revenue price se 'sasta' nahi hai bina margin dekhe.
  • Purana EBITDA use karna. Ek achha saal multiple ke denominator ko badha deta hai aur average business sasta dikhta hai.
