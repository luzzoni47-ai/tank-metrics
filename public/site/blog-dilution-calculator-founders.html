Frequently asked questions

How do I calculate founder dilution?

Multiply, do not subtract. Equity after a round = equity before × (1 − equity given). 100% after a 10% round is 90%; a further 20% round leaves 72%.

Does the ESOP pool dilute founders?

Yes. The employee option pool is carved out of the cap table and dilutes founders exactly like an investor's stake does, unless the deal says otherwise.

How much dilution is normal per round in India?

Seed rounds typically take 10-20%, Series A another 15-25%. Across three rounds a founder can end up holding 50-65% of the company, which is normal and not a failure.

In Shark Tank India Season 1, a founder gave away 20% for ₹1 crore and felt lucky. Two years later a second round took another 20%, and an ESOP pool for senior hires took 10% more. The founder who once owned the whole company was now under 60% - and had never once sat down and multiplied it out. Dilution compounds, and each round is charged against a smaller and smaller base. This calculator shows what you keep after the round, the ESOP pool, and the rupees of company you actually gave up.

What is dilution calculator for founders?

Dilution is the slice of the company a founder gives away, and the slice that is left. If you own 100% today and hand an investor 10%, you keep 90%. That part is simple - the part that catches founders out is what happens across several rounds and an ESOP pool.

Dilution compounds because each round is charged against a smaller base. Lose 10% in round one and you are at 90%. Lose 20% in round two and you are at 72%, not 70%. Add a 10% ESOP pool and you are at 64.8%. A founder who started with everything can end up below two-thirds without a single round looking large.

The number that matters is not the stake alone but the stake next to the valuation. Giving 20% at a high price can cost less of the company's value than giving 5% at a low one. This calculator shows the stake after the round, the ESOP pool, and the total equity given up, so the trade-off is visible before the term sheet is signed.

Formula

The formulaFounder Equity After = Equity Before × (1 − Equity Given ÷ 100) × (1 − ESOP Pool ÷ 100)

Multiply, do not subtract, across rounds. 100% after a 10% round is 90%; a further 20% round leaves 72%, not 70%. The ESOP pool dilutes founders exactly like an investor does.

Why Indian Calculation is Different (US vs India)

Indian founder agreements often create the ESOP pool before the round rather than after, which means the pool dilutes the founders and not the incoming investor. That ordering is a real difference from many US deals and it changes the final number.

Indian rounds frequently use CCPS (Compulsorily Convertible Preference Shares) with anti-dilution clauses, and a down round can trigger them - meaning the founder's dilution is larger than the headline percentage suggests.

Indian family businesses that bring in an outside investor rarely write down how the founding family's stake will move across future rounds. This calculator is how you show your CA and your family exactly where the stake lands.

Common Mistakes

  • Subtracting percentages across rounds instead of multiplying fractions, which understates the total dilution.
  • Forgetting the ESOP pool, which dilutes founders exactly like an investor does and is often created before the round.
  • Watching the stake and ignoring the valuation - giving 20% at a high price can cost less than 5% at a low one.
