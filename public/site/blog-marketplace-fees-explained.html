Marketplace Fees Explained: Why an ₹800 Sale Pays You ₹580

Commission, shipping, closing and return fees quietly eat a quarter of your selling price. Here is the arithmetic, in rupees.

Pricing & CostsOctober 21, 2026

Most first-time sellers price a product by adding a markup to their cost, list it, and then get a settlement far smaller than they expected.

The gap is marketplace fees. Commission, shipping, closing, storage and returns are each small on their own, and together they can turn a healthy-looking price into a thin margin — or a loss.

What marketplace fees actually include

A marketplace takes money in several separate ways, and the rate card is a long table nobody reads until the first settlement arrives.

  • Referral or commission fee. A percentage of the selling price, set by category. Electronics may sit near 5%, while apparel and beauty can reach 20% to 25%.
  • Closing fee. A small fixed amount per unit, charged whatever the price.
  • Shipping fee. What the marketplace charges you to move the parcel, based on weight and zone. For a light item this is often ₹60 to ₹80.
  • Storage and long-term fees. Charged on stock that sits in the warehouse past a grace period.
  • Return and RTO costs. On a returned order you lose the forward shipping and usually pay the return leg as well.

A worked example: an ₹800 sale

Say you sell a product at ₹800. Your category carries a 15% commission, shipping is ₹60, and other fees — closing and payment — come to ₹40.

  1. Commission: 15% of ₹800 = ₹120.
  2. Total fees: ₹120 + ₹60 + ₹40 = ₹220.
  3. You receive: ₹800 − ₹220 = ₹580.

So an ₹800 sale pays you ₹580, or 72.5% of the sticker price. The marketplace kept ₹220, which is more than a quarter of what the customer paid.

The number that matters: your real margin

The settlement amount is not profit. Your product still has to be bought or made.

In the example above, suppose the product costs you ₹400. Your profit is ₹580 − ₹400 = ₹180, which is 22.5% of the ₹800 selling price.

That is the number to price against. If your cost were ₹600 instead, the same sale would leave you a ₹20 loss — even though the order shipped and the customer paid in full.

Five fees sellers forget

  1. Payment gateway charges. Roughly 2% of the order value, and easy to leave out of your calculation.
  2. Weight revisions. If the marketplace measures your parcel heavier than you declared, the shipping fee is recalculated — and back-charged.
  3. Return shipping. A customer return is not just a lost sale. You pay to bring the item back and it may come home unsellable.
  4. Storage ageing. Slow-moving stock gets more expensive every month it stays in the warehouse.
  5. Advertising. Sponsored listings are not a fee, but they are a cost per order. Treat them as part of your margin, not as marketing you can switch off.

How to protect your margin

Price from the settlement, not the sticker. Work backwards: decide the amount you need per unit, then add fees to reach the list price.

Keep the cheaper-to-ship range. Cutting 200 grams off packaging can drop you into a lower shipping slab, which is pure margin on every order.

Watch commission by category. The same product filed under a different category can carry a very different rate. File it correctly and deliberately.

Decide your own exit point. If a category's total fees push your margin under 15%, that listing is buying revenue, not profit. Fix the price or leave the category.

The short version

Add commission, shipping and closing fees before you decide a price. On an ₹800 sale at 15% commission with ₹60 shipping and ₹40 of other fees, you receive ₹580 — 72.5% of the sticker price. If the product costs ₹400, your profit is ₹180, or 22.5%.

Price from that settlement figure, not from the sticker price, and a thin margin stops being a surprise.

Jo log pehli baar online bech rahe hain, ve aam taur par lagat par markup jodkar keemat tay karte hain, product list karte hain, aur phir settlement ummeed se bahut kam milta hai.

Yeh antar marketplace fees ki vajah se hota hai. Commission, shipping, closing, storage aur returns — har ek akele chhota lagta hai, par milkar ye ek theek-thaak keemat ko patle margin mein badal dete hain — ya nuksan mein.

Marketplace fees mein asal mein kya-kya aata hai

Marketplace kai alag-alag tareekon se paisa leta hai, aur rate card ek lambi table hoti hai jise pehla settlement aane tak koi nahi padhta.

  • Referral ya commission fee. Bikri keemat ka ek pratishat, category se tay hota hai. Electronics mein yeh lagabhag 5% hota hai, jabaki apparel aur beauty mein 20% se 25% tak pahunch sakta hai.
  • Closing fee. Har unit par ek chhoti tay rakam, keemat koi bhi ho.
  • Shipping fee. Parcel bhejane ke liye marketplace jo leta hai, vazan aur zone ke hisaab se. Halke cheez ke liye yeh aksar ₹60 se ₹80 hota hai.
  • Storage aur long-term fees. Jo maal tay avadhi se zyada warehouse mein pada rehta hai, us par lagta hai.
  • Return aur RTO kharch. Laute order par forward shipping ka nuksan hota hai, aur aam taur par vaapasi ki lagat bhi aapke khaate mein aate hai.

Ek udaharan: ₹800 ki bikri

Maan lijiye aap ek product ₹800 mein bechate hain. Aapki category mein 15% commission hai, shipping ₹60 hai, aur baaki fees — closing aur payment — ₹40 banate hain.

  1. Commission: ₹800 ka 15% = ₹120.
  2. Kul fees: ₹120 + ₹60 + ₹40 = ₹220.
  3. Aapko milta hai: ₹800 − ₹220 = ₹580.

To ₹800 ki bikri par aapko ₹580 milate hain, yani keemat ka 72.5%. Marketplace ne ₹220 rakhe, jo graahak ke diye paise ka ek chauthai se zyada hai.

Asli aankda: aapka asli margin

Settlement ki rakam munafa nahi hai. Aapka product khareedna ya banana ab baaki hai.

Upar ke udaharan mein maan lijiye product banane mein aapko ₹400 lagte hain. Aapka munafa banata hai ₹580 − ₹400 = ₹180, jo ₹800 ki bikri keemat ka 22.5% hai.

Isi aankde par keemat tay karni chahiye. Agar lagat ₹600 hoti, to wahi bikri ₹20 ka nuksan kara dete — bhale hi order bhej diya gaya aur graahak ne pura paisa diya.

Paanch fees jo log bhool jaate hain

  1. Payment gateway charges. Order value ka lagabhag 2%, aur hisaab se chhoot jaana aasan hai.
  2. Weight revision. Agar marketplace aapke parcel ka vazan ghoshit se zyada naapata hai, to shipping fee dobaara lagti hai — aur baad mein kaat lee jaati hai.
  3. Return shipping. Graahak ki vaapasi sirf bik gayi cheez nahi chheenti. Aapko maal vaapas laane ka kharch bhi dena hota hai, aur woh bikne layak na bache to nuksan alag.
  4. Storage ageing. Dheeme chalane wala maal har mahine warehouse mein aur mehanga padta hai.
  5. Advertising. Sponsored listing koi fee nahi hai, par har order par ek lagat hai. Ise margin ka hissa maanein, band karne layak marketing nahi.

Apna margin kaise bachayein

Keemat settlement se tay karein, keemat ke sticker se nahi. Ulta chalen: pehle tay karein ki har unit par aapko kitna chahiye, phir fees jodkar list price banayein.

Sasti shipping wali seedhi mein rahein. Packaging se 200 gram ghatana aapko neeche ki shipping slab mein daal sakta hai, jo har order par seedha margin hai.

Category ke hisaab se commission dekhein. Wahi product alag category mein daalne par bahut alag rate leta hai. Ise sahe aur soch-samajhkar bharen.

Apni exit limit tay karein. Agar kisi category ki kul fees aapka margin 15% se neeche le jaati hai, to woh listing revenue khareed rahi hai, munafa nahi. Keemat theek karein ya category chhod dein.
