MSME Loan Eligibility: What Banks Check

The two numbers a bank reads before it says yes.

Small BusinessNovember 7, 2026

An MSME loan is not approved on how much money you need. It is approved on whether your business already earns enough to pay the instalments out of its own cash. A lender reads two numbers before it reads anything else: your DSCR, which shows whether earnings cover the repayment, and your working capital, which shows whether the business has enough cash to keep running while it repays. Get both into a healthy range and most of the paperwork is a formality. Get them wrong and no amount of explaining will fix it.

This guide shows how each number is worked out, what a lender wants to see, and the mistakes that quietly sink an application.

The first number: DSCR

DSCR stands for Debt Service Coverage Ratio. It answers one question: for every ₹1 of loan repayment due, how many rupees of operating income does the business bring in? The formula is simple.

DSCR = Net Operating Income ÷ Total Debt Service

Net operating income is what the business earns in a period before interest and tax. Total debt service is everything the business must repay in that same period, including interest and principal on every loan. A ratio above 1 means earnings cover the repayment. A ratio below 1 means the business is paying the loan out of cash it does not have.

Most banks in India want a DSCR of at least 1.25x. Below that, the lender sees a business that would have to dip into reserves or borrow again to pay each instalment.

Working out the DSCR for a small shop

Say a small shop earns ₹8,000 of net operating income in a month, and its total loan repayment is ₹5,000 a month.

  1. Net operating income: ₹8,000.
  2. Total debt service: ₹5,000.
  3. DSCR = ₹8,000 ÷ ₹5,000 = 1.6x.

At 1.6x the shop earns ₹1.60 for every ₹1 it has to repay, comfortably above the 1.25x floor. Now watch what happens when income slips. If the same shop has a weak month and net operating income falls to ₹5,000, the DSCR is ₹5,000 ÷ ₹5,000 = 1.0x. The business now earns exactly what it must repay — no cushion at all, and the lender treats that as a warning sign.

The second number: working capital

Working capital is the cash a business has available for day-to-day running, once its short-term bills are set aside. The formula is:

Working Capital = Current Assets − Current Liabilities

Current assets are the things that will turn into cash within a year: cash in the bank, stock on the shelf, and money customers owe you. Current liabilities are the bills due within a year: supplier payments, short-term dues and the loan instalment itself. A positive figure means the business can meet its near-term obligations. A negative figure means it is short, and a shortfall is the single most common reason an otherwise profitable business cannot service a new loan.

The two numbers on one page

Take the same shop. On its books it holds ₹9,000 of current assets — say ₹3,000 in cash and ₹6,000 of stock — against ₹6,000 of current liabilities.

  • Current assets: ₹3,000 cash + ₹6,000 stock = ₹9,000.
  • Current liabilities: ₹6,000.
  • Working capital: ₹9,000 − ₹6,000 = ₹3,000.

So the lender sees a shop that earns ₹8,000 a month against a ₹5,000 repayment, a DSCR of 1.6x, and holds ₹3,000 of positive working capital. Both numbers are healthy, and the application stands on its own. If either number were weak — a DSCR near 1.0x, or negative working capital — the lender would ask why, and a good answer is harder to give than a good number.

Mistakes that get an MSME loan rejected

  • Quoting revenue instead of net operating income. Lenders look at what is left after operating costs, not the top line.
  • Forgetting the new instalment in the debt service. Adding a loan raises total debt service, which lowers the DSCR you are being judged on.
  • Leaving stock and unpaid invoices out of current assets. Both are real, and both improve the working capital picture.
  • Applying on a weak month. A single month's figures can make a seasonal business look far worse than it is.
  • Mixing personal and business accounts. When income and expenses are tangled, the lender cannot read either number.

What to fix before you apply

  1. Work out your DSCR on a normal month, not your best one. If it is under 1.25x, reduce existing debt or raise income before applying.
  2. Check your working capital. If it is negative, collect overdue invoices or trim stock before you approach the bank.
  3. Keep twelve months of clean, separate records, so both numbers can be verified.
  4. Apply in a month that reflects your typical trading, not a seasonal dip.

Pehla aankda: DSCR

DSCR ka matlab hai Debt Service Coverage Ratio. Yeh ek sawal ka jawab deta hai: har ₹1 ki kisht ke saamane, karobar kitne rupaye ki opareting aamdani laata hai? Formula aasan hai.

DSCR = Net Operating Income ÷ Total Debt Service

Net operating income yani karobar ek avadhi mein byaj aur tax se pehle jo kamata hai. Total debt service yani usi avadhi mein chukaane padane wali har rakam, jismein har lon ka byaj aur mooladhan dono shaamil hain. 1 se upar ka anupaat matlab kamai kisht dhak lete hai. 1 se neeche matlab karobar aise nakad se lon chuka raha hai jo usake paas hai hi nahi.

Bhaarat mein zyadatar bank kam se kam 1.25x ka DSCR chahte hain. Usse neeche lendar ko aisa karobar dikhta hai jise har kisht ke liye bhandaar chhoona padega ya dobaara karz lena padega.

Chhoti dukaan ka DSCR nikaalana

Maan lijiye ek chhoti dukaan mahine mein ₹8,000 ki net operating income kamaate hai, aur uski kul kisht ₹5,000 mahina hai.

  1. Net operating income: ₹8,000.
  2. Total debt service: ₹5,000.
  3. DSCR = ₹8,000 ÷ ₹5,000 = 1.6x.

1.6x par dukaan har ₹1 ki kisht ke saamane ₹1.60 kamaate hai, jo 1.25x ki seema se upar hai. Ab dekhie jab kamai girate hai. Agar usi dukaan ka kamzor mahina aaye aur net operating income ₹5,000 rah jaye, to DSCR ₹5,000 ÷ ₹5,000 = 1.0x hoga. Ab karobar utna hi kamata hai jitna chukaana hai — koi gunjaish nahi, aur lendar ise chetaavane maanata hai.

Doosara aankda: working capital

Working capital yani woh nakad jo karobar ke paas roz ke kharch ke liye bachate hai, alpakaalik bil alag rakhane ke baad. Formula yeh hai:

Working Capital = Current Assets − Current Liabilities

Current assets ve cheezein hain jo saal bhar mein nakad banenge: bank mein paisa, shelph par pada maal, aur graahakon se vasoolane wali rakam. Current liabilities ve bil hain jo saal bhar mein dena hai: saplaayar ke paise, alpakaalik denadaare aur khud lon ki kisht. Dhanaatmak aankda matlab karobar apni nazadeeke denadaare chuka sakta hai. Rinatmak matlab woh chuka nahi sakta, aur yahi sabse aam vajah hai jisase munafe wala karobar bhi naya lon nahi utha paata.

Dono aankde ek panne par

Wahi dukaan lijiye. Kitaabon mein usake paas ₹9,000 ki current assets hain — maan lijiye ₹3,000 nakad aur ₹6,000 maal — aur ₹6,000 ki current liabilities.

  • Current assets: ₹3,000 nakad + ₹6,000 maal = ₹9,000.
  • Current liabilities: ₹6,000.
  • Working capital: ₹9,000 − ₹6,000 = ₹3,000.

To lendar ko aise dukaan dikhate hai jo ₹5,000 ki kisht ke saamane mahine mein ₹8,000 kamaate hai, jisaka DSCR 1.6x hai, aur jisake paas ₹3,000 ki dhanaatmak working capital hai. Dono aankde theek hain, aur aavedan apne dam par khada hai. Agar koi ek aankda kamzor ho — DSCR 1.0x ke aasapaas, ya rinatmak working capital — to lendar poochhega kyon, aur achha aankda dene se achha jawab dena zyada mushkil hai.

Ve galatiyaan jo MSME lon radd karaate hain

  • Net operating income ki jagah revenue batana. Lendar opareting kharch ke baad jo bachta hai woh dekhta hai, upar ki kul bikri nahi.
  • Nayi kisht ko debt service mein bhool jaana. Lon jodne se total debt service badhti hai, jisase aapka DSCR gir jaata hai.
  • Maal aur bakaaya bilon ko current assets mein na ginana. Dono asli hain, aur dono working capital sudhaarate hain.
  • Kamzor mahine mein aavedan karna. Ek hi mahine ke aankde mausame karobar ko asal se kaheen kharaab dikha dete hain.
  • Nije aur karobari khaate mila dena. Jab kamai aur kharch ulajhe hon, lendar koi bhi aankda theek se nahi padh paata.

Aavedan se pehle kya theek karein

  1. Apna DSCR samanya mahine par nikalein, sabse achhe mahine par nahi. Agar yeh 1.25x se kam hai, to aavedan se pehle maujooda karz ghatayein ya aamdani badhayein.
  2. Apni working capital jaanchein. Agar rinatmak hai, to bank jaane se pehle bakaaya bil vasoolen ya maal ghatayein.
  3. Baarah mahine ke saaf, alag rikord rakhein, taaki dono aankde satyaapit ho saken.
  4. Aise mahine mein aavedan karein jo aapke samanya karobar ko dikhaae, mausame giraavat ko nahi.
