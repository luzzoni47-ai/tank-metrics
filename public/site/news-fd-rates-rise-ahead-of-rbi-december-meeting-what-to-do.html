FD Rates Are Rising: Where a Shop Owner Parks Cash

Banks raised FD rates across November after the RBI's October hike. Here is the arithmetic of locking in now versus waiting for the December 4 decision.

Small BusinessNovember 22, 2026

Banks have started paying more for deposits. HDFC Bank, ICICI Bank, Axis Bank and several others have raised fixed deposit rates on selected tenures during November 2026, after the RBI raised the repo rate by 25 basis points to 5.50% on 7 October. For a shop owner sitting on idle cash, that changes the maths on where the surplus should sit.

Why the banks are paying more

Two forces are pushing deposit rates up at the same time. The first is the repo rate, the rate at which the RBI lends to banks. When it rises, banks pay more for overnight money, so they can afford to pay more for your deposit. The second is that loans are growing faster than deposits. Banks are lending more than they are collecting, so they have to compete for household savings to fund the gap. That competition, not the repo rate alone, is why the best rates in November come tied to a particular tenure.

The rates on the table in November 2026

The headline numbers are not far apart, but the tenure is where the money is. On NDTV Profit's November list, HDFC Bank pays 6.55% for 2 to 3 years and 6.60% on its 55-month deposit. SBI pays 6.40% for 2 to less than 3 years. ICICI Bank pays 6.60% for 3 years and 1 day to 5 years, and Canara Bank pays 6.75% on its 555-day deposit. YES Bank offers the highest rate on that list, 7.25%, on 18 months to 25 months. Bank of Baroda sits at the bottom, 6.25% for 1 to 3 years.

Read that list twice. The difference between 6.25% and 6.75% is half a percentage point, and that is real money on a deposit you were going to make anyway.

The arithmetic of waiting for a better rate

The RBI meets on 2 to 4 December 2026, and the market expects another 25-basis-point hike to 5.75%. A basis point is one hundredth of a percentage point, so 25 basis points is 0.25%. On a ₹2,00,000 deposit, 0.25% is ₹500 a year.

That is the prize for waiting. The cost of waiting is bigger. ₹2,00,000 at 6.5% earns about ₹1,000 a month. If you hold the money in a savings account for two months to catch the next hike, you give up roughly ₹2,000 of interest to chase ₹500 a year. The hike has to be far larger than the market expects before waiting pays.

Tax narrows the gap further. FD interest is taxed at your income slab. In the 30% bracket, ₹26,000 of interest leaves about ₹18,000. The ₹10,000 deduction under Section 80TTA applies to savings account interest, not to a fixed deposit.

What the same hike does to your loan

A hike is not only good news. The repo rate sets the floor for lending rates, and banks pass it on to borrowers with a lag. HDFC Bank raised its marginal cost of funds-based lending rate by 5 to 15 basis points from 7 November 2026, which lifts interest on loans priced off it. On a ₹10,00,000 loan, a full 0.25% pass-through adds ₹2,500 a year in interest, and it does so while the loan is still running.

For a shop with a cash credit limit or a machinery loan, the two effects partly cancel: you earn a little more on the deposit and pay a little more on the borrowing. The spread between what you earn and what you pay is what actually moved.

What to do with the spare cash

The practical answer is to ladder, not to guess. Split the surplus into three parts. Put the first part into a deposit now at today's rate, because you know that rate. Keep the second part for the December meeting and the third for the quarter after. If rates rise, you catch them with the later parts. If they do not, you still earned on the first part instead of leaving it idle.

Match the tenure to the money's job. Working capital that buys stock in six weeks does not belong in a 5-year deposit, because most banks pay a lower rate if you break the deposit early. Money you will not touch for two years can take the longer tenure and the higher rate. An FD can also be pledged as security for an overdraft, so one deposit can earn interest and support a credit line at the same time.

None of this needs a forecast. The rate is already in front of you, the December date is already fixed, and the arithmetic of waiting is small and knowable. Price the deposit before you decide, not after.

Banks ne deposits par zyada interest dena shuru kar diya hai. HDFC Bank, ICICI Bank, Axis Bank aur kuch doosre banks ne November 2026 ke dauraan chuni hui tenures par fixed deposit rates badha diye hain. Iski wajah hai RBI ka 7 October ka 25 basis point hike, jisse repo rate 5.50% ho gaya. Jis dukaandaar ke paas idle cash hai, uske liye paisa kahan rakhein, yeh sawaal ab naya ho jaata hai.

Banks zyada kyun de rahe hain

Do cheezein ek saath deposit rates ko upar dhakel rahi hain. Pehli hai repo rate, jis par RBI banks ko udhaar deta hai. Jab woh badhta hai, banks ko overnight paisa mehnga padta hai, isliye woh aapki deposit par bhi zyada de sakte hain. Doosri hai demand: loans deposits se tezi se badh rahe hain. Banks jitna jama ho raha hai usse zyada udhaar de rahe hain, isliye unhe gharelu savings ke liye ek doosre se compete karna pad raha hai. Isi competition ki wajah se November ke sabse achhe rates kisi khaas tenure ke saath aate hain.

November 2026 mein kaun sa rate mil raha hai

Numbers dekhne mein paas paas lagte hain, lekin asli fark tenure mein hai. NDTV Profit ki November list ke hisaab se HDFC Bank 2 se 3 saal par 6.55% aur apni 55 mahine ki deposit par 6.60% deta hai. SBI 2 saal se 3 saal se kam par 6.40% deta hai. ICICI Bank 3 saal 1 din se 5 saal par 6.60%, aur Canara Bank apni 555 din ki deposit par 6.75% deta hai. Us list mein sabse zyada YES Bank de raha hai, 18 se 25 mahine par 7.25%. Sabse kam Bank of Baroda hai, 1 se 3 saal par 6.25%.

Is list ko do baar padhiye. 6.25% aur 6.75% mein aadha percentage point ka fark hai, aur woh us paise par sach mein fark karta hai jo aap waise bhi deposit karne wale the.

Behtar rate ka intezaar karne ka hisaab

RBI ki meeting 2 se 4 December 2026 hai, aur market ko ek aur 25 basis point hike ki ummeed hai, jisse repo rate 5.75% ho jayega. Ek basis point percentage point ka sauva hissa hota hai, to 25 basis point matlab 0.25%. ₹2,00,000 ki deposit par 0.25% ka matlab saal mein ₹500 hai.

Intezaar karne ka inaam itna hi hai. Intezaar ki keemat usse badi hai. ₹2,00,000 par 6.5% se mahine ka takreeban ₹1,000 banta hai. Agar aap agle hike ke liye paisa do mahine savings account mein rakhte hain, to saal ka ₹500 pakadne ke liye takreeban ₹2,000 ka interest chhod dete hain. Intezaar tabhi fayda deta hai jab hike market ki ummeed se kaafi bada ho.

Tax is gap ko aur chhota kar deta hai. FD ka interest aapke income slab par tax hota hai. 30% bracket mein ₹26,000 ke interest par takreeban ₹18,000 bachta hai. Section 80TTA ke tehat ₹10,000 ki chhoot savings account ke interest par milti hai, fixed deposit par nahi.

Isi hike ka asar aapke loan par

Hike sirf achhi khabar nahi hai. Repo rate lending rates ki neev set karta hai, aur banks ise borrowers tak thodi der se pahunchate hain. HDFC Bank ne 7 November 2026 se apna MCLR 5 se 15 basis point badhaya, jisse us par tay hone wale loans ka interest badhta hai. ₹10,00,000 ke loan par poora 0.25% pass-through saal ka ₹2,500 interest jodta hai, aur yeh tab karta hai jab loan abhi chal raha hota hai.

Jis dukaan par cash credit limit ya machinery loan hai, wahan dono asar kuch had tak kat jate hain: deposit par thoda zyada milta hai aur udhaar par thoda zyada dena padta hai. Asli badlav us spread mein hota hai jo aap kamate hain aur jo aap dete hain.

Bache hue paise ka kya karein

Amli jawab yeh hai ki ladder banaiye, andaaza mat lagaiye. Surplus ko teen hisson mein baantiye. Pehla hissa aaj ki rate par deposit kar dijiye, kyunki woh rate aapko pata hai. Doosra hissa December ki meeting ke liye rakhiye, teesra uske baad ki quarter ke liye. Agar rates badhein, to aap baad ke hisson se pakad lete hain. Agar na badhein, to bhi pehle hisse par kamai ho gayi, paisa yun hi pada nahi raha.

Tenure ko paise ke kaam se milaiye. Jo working capital chhe hafte mein stock kharidne ke liye chahiye, woh 5 saal ki deposit mein nahi jaata, kyunki zyadatar banks jaldi deposit todne par kam rate dete hain. Jo paisa do saal tak nahi chahiye, woh lambi tenure aur zyada rate le sakta hai. FD ko overdraft ke liye security bhi banaya ja sakta hai, to ek hi deposit interest bhi kamata hai aur credit line bhi sambhalta hai.

Ismein kisi forecast ki zaroorat nahi. Rate aapke saamne hai, December ki date tay hai, aur intezaar ka hisaab chhota aur saaf hai. Faisla karne se pehle deposit ka number nikaal lijiye, baad mein nahi.
