ARPU: Average Revenue Per User, Explained Simply

What ARPU means, how to work it out from your own numbers, and the levers that quietly move it.

Customers & Unit EconomicsOctober 27, 2026

If you run an app, a subscription, or any business where the same customer pays you again and again, ARPU is the number that tells you whether you are getting more out of every person you already have. It is short, easy to calculate, and very easy to calculate wrongly.

This guide shows what ARPU means, how to work it out from your own figures, how it differs from the numbers people confuse it with, and what actually moves it.

What does ARPU mean?

ARPU stands for average revenue per user. It is your total revenue for a period divided by the number of users you had in that same period. It answers one question: on average, how much does one user bring in?

Investors and subscription businesses watch it closely because it is a quick read on how much each user is worth to you, before you start counting what it costs to win them.

The ARPU formula

ARPU = Total revenue ÷ Number of users

Two rules decide whether the number means anything:

  • Match the period. Use the revenue and the users from the same month (or quarter, or year). Mixing a month of revenue with a year of users gives a number that describes nothing.
  • Count active users, not sign-ups. A user who installed the app once and never came back is not bringing in revenue. Counting them drags your ARPU down and hides the real picture.

A worked example

Say you run a small subscription app. For one month:

  • Total subscription revenue: ₹6,000
  • Active users: 300

ARPU = ₹6,000 ÷ 300 = ₹20 per user for the month.

Now suppose you keep the same 300 users but improve what each one pays — a slightly better plan mix, one small add-on — so ARPU rises 25%, from ₹20 to ₹25. The same 300 users now bring in ₹25 × 300 = ₹7,500, up from ₹6,000. You added ₹1,500 of revenue without adding a single user.

That is the whole appeal of ARPU: it shows how much growth is available from the users you already have.

ARPU vs ARPPU: the same revenue, two very different numbers

ARPPU is average revenue per paying user. It divides the same revenue by only the users who actually paid.

In the example above, if 120 of the 300 users pay and the other 180 are on a free plan:

  • ARPU = ₹6,000 ÷ 300 = ₹20
  • ARPPU = ₹6,000 ÷ 120 = ₹50

Both are correct. They answer different questions. ARPU tells you what an average user is worth across your whole base; ARPPU tells you what a paying user is worth. If the gap between them is wide, you have a lot of free users you have not converted yet — which is an opportunity, not necessarily a problem.

ARPU vs LTV and average order value

  • ARPU — revenue per user for one period. A rate.
  • LTV (customer lifetime value) — revenue per user across their whole life with you. ARPU is one of its inputs: roughly, LTV is ARPU multiplied by how many periods a user stays.
  • Average order value (AOV) — the typical value of one order, used by shops rather than subscriptions. A customer can place many orders, so AOV and ARPU are not the same thing.

Read together, ARPU and LTV tell you whether a customer is worth more than it costs to win them.

Four ways to raise your ARPU

  1. Raise the price carefully. A small, well-explained increase lifts ARPU for every existing user at once. Test it on new users first.
  2. Sell a better plan. Most users sit on your cheapest tier. Making the middle tier obviously better moves people up without a price change.
  3. Add a small add-on. One extra feature at a low monthly price is often easier to sell than a full upgrade, and it lifts the average for everyone who takes it.
  4. Convert free users. Every free user you turn into a paying one raises ARPU, even if you change nothing about your prices.

Common ARPU mistakes

  1. Counting sign-ups as users. The single most common error. Use active users.
  2. Mismatching the period. Monthly revenue over annual users, or the reverse.
  3. Comparing ARPU across different businesses. A ₹20 ARPU app and a ₹20 ARPU marketplace are not comparable — their costs are completely different.
  4. Treating ARPU as profit. ARPU is revenue per user, not money you keep. A high ARPU with a high acquisition cost can still lose money on every user.

The short version

ARPU is your total revenue divided by your active users for the same period. It tells you how much each user is worth, and it moves when you raise prices, sell better plans, add small extras, or convert free users.

Just remember it is revenue, not profit — pair it with your acquisition cost and lifetime value before you draw conclusions.

ARPU ka matlab kya hai?

ARPU ka pura naam hai average revenue per user. Yeh aapki ek avadhi ki kul revenue ko usi avadhi ke users ki sankhya se bhaag dekar nikalta hai. Yeh ek hi sawal ka jawab deta hai: ausatan, ek user kitna laata hai?

Niveshak aur subscription karobar ise dhyaan se dekhte hain kyunki yeh jhatapat batata hai ki har user aapke liye kitne ka hai — isase pehle ki aap yeh ginen ki use laane mein kitna kharch hua.

ARPU ka formula

ARPU = kul revenue ÷ users ki sankhya

Do niyam tay karte hain ki yeh number kuch matlab rakhta hai ya nahi:

  • Avadhi milaaen. Ek hi mahine (ya timaahe, ya saal) ka revenue aur users istemal karein. Ek mahine ka revenue aur saal bhar ke users milaane se aisa number banata hai jo kuch bhi nahi batata.
  • Active users ginen, sign-ups nahi. Jis user ne app ek baar install kiya aur lauta hi nahi, woh revenue nahi laata. Use ginane se aapka ARPU neeche khinch jaata hai aur asli tasveer chhip jaati hai.

Ek udaharan

Maan lijiye aap ek chhota subscription app chalate hain. Ek mahine ka hisaab:

  • Kul subscription revenue: ₹6,000
  • Active users: 300

ARPU = ₹6,000 ÷ 300 = ₹20 prati user, mahine ke liye.

Ab maan lijiye wahi 300 users rehte hain, par har ek jo deta hai woh thoda behtar ho jaata hai — plan ka mel thoda sudhar jaata hai, ek chhota add-on jud jaata hai — to ARPU 25% badhakar ₹20 se ₹25 ho jaata hai. Wahi 300 users ab ₹25 × 300 = ₹7,500 laate hain, jo pehle ₹6,000 tha. Aapne ek bhi naya user jode bina ₹1,500 ka revenue badha diya.

ARPU ki poore khoobe yahi hai: yeh dikhata hai ki jo users aapke paas pehle se hain, unase kitni growth mil sakate hai.

ARPU banam ARPPU: ek hi revenue, do bahut alag number

ARPPU hai average revenue per paying user. Yeh wahi revenue sirph un users se bhaag deta hai jinhonne sach mein paisa diya.

Upar ke udaharan mein, agar 300 mein se 120 users paisa dete hain aur baaki 180 free plan par hain:

  • ARPU = ₹6,000 ÷ 300 = ₹20
  • ARPPU = ₹6,000 ÷ 120 = ₹50

Dono sahe hain. Dono alag savaalon ke jawab hain. ARPU batata hai ki aapke poore base mein ek ausat user kitne ka hai; ARPPU batata hai ki ek paisa dene wala user kitne ka hai. Agar dono ke beech ka antar bada hai, to aapke paas bahut se free users hain jinhen aapne abhe joda nahi — yeh mauka hai, zaroore nahi ki samasya ho.

ARPU, LTV aur average order value

  • ARPU — ek avadhi mein prati user revenue. Ek dar.
  • LTV (customer lifetime value) — aapke saath poore zindage mein prati user revenue. ARPU isaka ek hissa hai: mote taur par LTV yani ARPU guna utane periods jitne tak user tikata hai.
  • Average order value (AOV) — ek order ki aam keemat, jo subscription nahi balki dukaanen istemal karti hain. Ek graahak kai order de sakta hai, isliye AOV aur ARPU ek baat nahi hain.

Saath padhane par ARPU aur LTV batate hain ki ek graahak use laane ke kharch se zyada ka hai ya nahi.

ARPU badhane ke chaar tarike

  1. Daam soch-samajhkar badhayein. Thode-see, theek se samajhaaee gayi badhotri har maujooda user ka ARPU ek saath upar kar dete hai. Pehle nae users par aazamaaen.
  2. Behtar plan bechen. Zyadatar users aapke sabse saste tier par baithe hain. Beech wale tier ko saaf taur par behtar banane se log daam badle bina upar aa jaate hain.
  3. Ek chhota add-on jodein. Kam mahine ke daam par ek atirikt feature poore upgrade se bechana aksar aasan hota hai, aur jo bhi ise leta hai uska ausat upar chadh jaata hai.
  4. Free users ko jodein. Har free user jise aap paisa dene wala banate hain, ARPU badhaata hai — bhale hi aap apne daamon mein kuch na badlein.

Aam ARPU galatiyaan

  1. Sign-ups ko users maan lena. Sabse aam galat. Active users istemal karein.
  2. Avadhi ka mel na hona. Mahine ka revenue upar saal bhar ke users, ya ulta.
  3. Alag-alag karobaron ka ARPU aapas mein tulna karna. ₹20 ARPU wala app aur ₹20 ARPU wala marketplace ek jaise nahi hain — unake lagat bilkul alag hai.
  4. ARPU ko munafa samajh lena. ARPU prati user revenue hai, aapke paas bacha paisa nahi. Ooncha ARPU aur oonche acquisition cost phir bhi har user par nuksan kar sakte hain.

Chhoti baat

ARPU aapki kul revenue hai, jise usi avadhi ke active users se bhaag diya gaya. Yeh batata hai ki har user kitne ka hai, aur yeh tab badhta hai jab aap daam badhaate hain, behtar plan bechate hain, chhote add-ons jodate hain, ya free users ko jodate hain.

Bas yaad rakhein yeh revenue hai, munafa nahi — nateeje nikalne se pehle ise apni acquisition cost aur lifetime value ke saath padhein.
