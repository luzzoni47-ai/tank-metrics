Mutual Fund Exit Load Explained

What an exit load really costs when you sell a mutual fund early.

Everyday Money BasicsNovember 22, 2026

You invest ₹5,000 in a mutual fund, the fund grows, and a few months later you redeem. The amount you get back is a little smaller than the value you saw on the screen. That gap is usually the exit load: a fee the fund charges for selling early.

Exit load is small, but it is not random. It follows a published rule, it depends only on how long you held the units, and you can avoid it completely by waiting. This guide shows how the charge is calculated, which funds use it, and what to check before you redeem.

What an exit load actually is

An exit load is a percentage deducted from your redemption proceeds when you sell units before a stated holding period ends. It is written in the scheme information document, so it is not a surprise charge - it is part of the fund's rules from the day you invest.

Two things make it easier to live with. First, the money does not go to the fund house as profit: it is put back into the scheme's pool, which protects the investors who stay. Second, it is a one-time charge on the amount you redeem, not a yearly fee. Once the holding period is over, the load is zero.

How the exit load is calculated

The formula is simple:

  • Exit load = redemption value × exit load rate
  • Amount you receive = redemption value - exit load

The rate is applied to the value of the units you are selling at that day's NAV, not to your original investment. Suppose you invested ₹5,000 and the units are now worth ₹6,000. If the scheme charges 1% within the first year and you redeem at month eight, the load is ₹60 and you receive ₹5,940. Had you waited past twelve months, the rate would be zero and you would receive the full ₹6,000.

Which funds charge an exit load

Most equity mutual funds charge up to 1% if you redeem within twelve months. Many debt funds charge nothing at all. Liquid funds often use a graded load that is highest on the day of purchase and falls to zero within a week, because they are built for very short holding periods.

Some schemes have no exit load because they already have a lock-in. ELSS tax-saving funds lock your money for three years, so there is nothing to redeem early and no load to charge. Always read the exit load line in the scheme document rather than assuming all funds behave the same way.

The holding period decides the rate

The clock starts on the date the units are allotted, not the date you decided to invest. Units bought through a monthly SIP each carry their own purchase date, so one folio can hold units that are two years old and units that are two weeks old at the same time.

The holding period is counted per unit. That is why a SIP investor who redeems after a few years usually pays no load on the earliest instalments while the newest ones may still be inside the window.

FIFO: which units get redeemed first

When you redeem part of a folio, the fund sells your oldest units first. This is the first-in, first-out rule, and it works in your favour: the units that have already crossed the holding period are the ones that leave, so the load is often nil even when the folio is a mix of old and new investments.

The rule also explains a common confusion. An investor sees a one-year-old folio and assumes every unit is free of load, but a recent SIP instalment inside it is still young. Check the purchase dates of the units being sold, not just the age of the folio.

Exit load is not the only cost

Three other charges are easy to confuse with the exit load:

  • Expense ratio - a yearly fee built into the NAV, charged whether you sell or not.
  • Securities transaction tax - a small tax on equity redemptions, separate from the load.
  • Capital gains tax - tax on the profit, based on how long you held the units.

Only the exit load is a fee for selling early. The others exist whether you redeem in month two or year five, so plan for them separately.

How to avoid paying an exit load

  1. Hold past the load-free date written in the scheme document - usually twelve months for equity funds.
  2. Redeem the oldest units first. FIFO already does this, so a partial redemption after a long SIP usually costs nothing.
  3. Do not switch funds in a hurry. A switch is a redemption followed by a purchase, so the exit load applies to the scheme you leave.
  4. Check the rule before you invest, not after. Two similar funds can have very different exit loads.

What to check before you redeem

Open the scheme information document and find the exit load table. Note the rate, the holding period that removes it, and whether the load is graded by day. Then check the purchase date of the units you plan to sell, because that date - not the date you first opened the folio - decides what you pay.

If the difference between redeeming today and redeeming in a few weeks is small, waiting is usually the cheaper choice. Exit load is one of the few investment costs you can plan to zero with nothing more than a calendar.

Aap myoochual phand mein ₹5,000 lagaate hain, phand badhta hai, aur kuch mahinon baad aap rideem karte hain. Skreen par dikh rahi rakam se thoda kam paisa haath mein aata hai. Yeh antar aamataur par egzit lod hota hai: jaldi bechane par phand jo shulk leta hai.

Egzit lod chhota hota hai, lekin manamaana nahi. Yeh ek likhit niyam se chalta hai, sirf is par nirbhar karta hai ki aapne yoonit kitne samay rakheen, aur thoda intazaar karke aap ise poore tarah taal sakte hain. Yeh guide dikhati hai ki shulk kaise nikalta hai, kaun se phand ise lete hain, aur rideem karne se pehle kya dekhna chahiye.

Egzit lod asal mein kya hai

Egzit lod ek pratishat hai jo tay holding avadhi poore hone se pehle yoonit bechane par aapki ridempashan rakam se kaata jaata hai. Yeh skeem ki jaanakaare wale dastaavez mein likha hota hai, isliye yeh achaanak lagane wala shulk nahi - nivesh ke pehle din se yeh phand ke niyamon ka hissa hai.

Do baatein ise aasan banati hain. Pehli, yeh paisa phand haaus ke munafe mein nahi jaata: yeh vaapas skeem ke pool mein daala jaata hai, jisase ruke hue niveshakon ko fayda hota hai. Doosare, yeh rideem ki gayi rakam par ek baar ka shulk hai, saalana fees nahi. Holding avadhi khatm hone ke baad lod shoony ho jaata hai.

Egzit lod kaise nikaala jaata hai

Formula seedha hai:

  • Egzit lod = ridempashan vailyoo × egzit lod dar
  • Haath mein aane wali rakam = ridempashan vailyoo - egzit lod

Dar us din ki enaeve par beche ja rahi yoonit ki vailyoo par lagti hai, aapke mool nivesh par nahi. Maan lijiye aapne ₹5,000 lagaae aur yoonit ab ₹6,000 ki hain. Agar skeem pehle saal mein 1% lete hai aur aap aathaven mahine mein rideem karte hain, to lod ₹60 hai aur aapko ₹5,940 milate hain. Baarah mahine poore hone tak rukte, to dar shoony hoti aur poore ₹6,000 milate.

Kaun se phand egzit lod lete hain

Zyadatar ikvite myoochual phand baarah mahine ke bheetar rideem karne par 1% tak lete hain. Kai det phand kuch bhi nahi lete. Likvid phand aksar ghatta hua lod lete hain jo khareed ke din sabse zyada hota hai aur ek hafte mein shoony ho jaata hai, kyunki ye bahut chhoti avadhi ke liye bane hain.

Kuch skeemon mein egzit lod nahi hota kyunki unamen pehle se lok-in hai. Eelaesaes tax-seving phand teen saal ke liye paisa lok karte hain, isliye jaldi rideem karne ki gunjaish hi nahi aur lod lagaane ki bhi nahi. Sabhe phand ek jaise maanane ke bajaay skeem dastaavez mein egzit lod ki laain padhein.

Holding avadhi se dar tay hoti hai

Ghade us taareekh se chalti hai jis din yoonit aavantit huee, us taareekh se nahi jis din aapne nivesh karne ka man banaaya. Har mahine ki esaaeepe se khareede yoonit ki apni khareed taareekh hoti hai, isliye ek hi pholiyo mein do saal puraane aur do hafte puraane yoonit saath rah sakate hain.

Holding avadhi har yoonit ke hisaab se gine jaati hai. Iseelie kuch saal baad rideem karne wale esaaeepe niveshak ko shuruaati kiston par aamataur par koi lod nahi lagta, jabaki sabse nayi kisten abhe avadhi ke andar ho sakate hain.

Pheepho: pehle kaun see yoonit bikate hai

Pholiyo ka kuch hissa rideem karne par phand sabse puraane yoonit pehle bechata hai. Yeh pehle-aaya-pehle-gaya niyam hai aur aapke paksh mein kaam karta hai: jo yoonit holding avadhi paar kar chuke hain wahi baahar jaati hain, isliye puraane aur nae nivesh wale pholiyo mein bhi lod aksar shoony hota hai.

Yahi niyam ek aam bhram bhi samjhata hai. Niveshak dekhta hai ki pholiyo ek saal puraana hai aur maan leta hai ki har yoonit par lod nahi hai, lekin usake andar haal ki esaaeepe kist abhe nayi hai. Pholiyo ki umr nahi, beche ja rahi yoonit ki khareed taareekh dekhein.

Egzit lod hi ekamaatr kharch nahi

Teen aur kharchon ko egzit lod se jodkar dekhna aasan hai:

  • Eksapens reshiyo - enaeve mein shaamil saalana fees, jo bechen ya na bechen, lagti rahate hai.
  • Sikyoriteez traanjaikshan tax - ikvite ridempashan par chhota tax, lod se alag.
  • Kaipital gens tax - munafe par tax, jo is par nirbhar karta hai ki yoonit kitne samay rakheen.

Sirf egzit lod jaldi bechane ka shulk hai. Baaki tab bhi lagte hain jab aap doosare mahine rideem karein ya paanchaven saal, isliye inake yojana alag se banaaen.

Egzit lod se kaise bachen

  1. Skeem dastaavez mein likhe lod-mukt taareekh tak rukein - ikvite phand mein aamataur par baarah mahine.
  2. Sabse puraane yoonit pehle rideem karein. Pheepho yahi karta hai, isliye lambi esaaeepe ke baad aanshik ridempashan par aamataur par kuch nahi lagta.
  3. Jaldabaaze mein phand na badlein. Svich ek ridempashan aur phir khareed hai, isliye jis skeem se nikalte hain us par egzit lod lagta hai.
  4. Niyam nivesh se pehle dekhein, baad mein nahi. Do milate-julate phandon ke egzit lod bahut alag ho sakte hain.

Rideem karne se pehle kya dekhein

Skeem ka jaanakaare dastaavez kholen aur egzit lod ki taalika dhoondhen. Dar, woh holding avadhi jisake baad lod khatm hota hai, aur kya lod din ke hisaab se ghatta hai - yeh teenon not karein. Phir beche jaane wali yoonit ki khareed taareekh dekhein, kyunki wahi taareekh tay karti hai ki aapko kya dena hai - pholiyo kholane ki taareekh nahi.

Agar aaj rideem karne aur kuch hafte baad rideem karne mein farq chhota hai, to intazaar karna aamataur par sasta rehta hai. Egzit lod un gine-chune nivesh kharchon mein hai jinhen sirf kailendar dekhakar shoony kiya ja sakta hai.
