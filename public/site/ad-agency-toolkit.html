Frequently asked questions

What calculators are in Ad Agency Toolkit?

Ad Agency Toolkit holds 9 calculators: CPM to CPC Converter, CPC to CPA Converter, Break-Even Ad Spend, Client Acquisition Cost (CAC), Client Lifetime Value (LTV), Agency Profit Margin, Client Profitability, Employee Utilization Rate and Churn Rate. Each one has its own page with the formula and a worked Indian example.

Are the Ad Agency Toolkit calculators free?

Yes. Every one of them is free, needs no account and shows no paywall. There is no limit on how often you use them.

Is my data sent anywhere when I use these tools?

No. Every calculation runs inside your browser. The numbers you type are never uploaded, stored or sold.

Do these use Indian lakh and crore formatting?

Yes. Amounts are shown in the Indian numbering system, and the tax and salary tools use current Indian slabs and rules rather than a converted US template.
