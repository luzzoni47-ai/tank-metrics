GST Council Scrapped Arrest Powers and Raised the Prosecution Limit to ₹5 Crore

The 57th GST Council meeting eased compliance for small businesses without touching tax rates. Here is what actually changed and what it means for your shop or startup.

Tax RulesOctober 21, 2026

On 8 October 2026, the 57th GST Council meeting approved a set of reforms that change how the tax office treats you rather than how much tax you pay. Rates were left untouched. What moved was enforcement, refunds and paperwork.

For a small seller or a first-time founder, this is the more useful kind of change. A lower rate saves you a little every month. Fewer ways to get into trouble saves you a lot, once.

What changed, in plain terms

Arrest powers under GST were removed. That is the headline. Tax officers can no longer arrest someone as an enforcement tool in the ordinary course of a dispute.

The prosecution threshold rose from ₹1 crore to ₹5 crore. Below that amount, a dispute is far less likely to turn criminal.

The general penalty dropped from ₹25,000 to ₹10,000. A small procedural mistake now costs less than half of what it did.

Refund acknowledgement fell from 15 days to 10 days, and the system will provisionally sanction 90% of a claim after risk assessment, with the order issued within three working days.

The small-business option worth knowing about

The Council approved, in principle, an optional annual return with quarterly payment scheme for qualifying business-to-consumer businesses with turnover up to ₹5 crore. Instead of a steady drumbeat of filings, a small seller could file once a year and pay quarterly.

Nothing here changes how you calculate GST on a bill. A ₹1,000 item at 18% is still ₹1,180, and the tax inside a ₹1,180 bill is still ₹180. The calculator above does that split either way.

What to actually do this week

Check whether your turnover puts you inside the ₹5 crore band for the annual-return option. If it does, ask your accountant whether it suits your cash cycle — paying quarterly can help a business that earns unevenly.

If you export or sell zero-rated goods, expect refunds to arrive faster. Plan your working capital on the new timelines rather than the old ones.

And keep your returns consistent. The Council also moved to reduce mismatches between what you report and what you claim as input tax credit. Easier enforcement is not absent enforcement.

What did not change

Rates. The 5%, 12%, 18% and 28% slabs are exactly where they were, and the Council has signalled it wants rate changes to settle into a once-a-year cycle instead of arriving unpredictably.

Registration thresholds and the basic filing obligation are untouched too. If you were registered last month, you are registered today, and the returns you owe have not changed shape.

8 October 2026 ko 57th GST Council meeting ne aise reforms approve kiye jo ye badalte hain ki tax office aapke saath kaise pesh aata hai, ye nahi ki aap kitna tax dete ho. Rates wahi rahe. Jo badla woh hai enforcement, refunds aur paperwork.

Ek chhote seller ya pehli baar founder ke liye yahi zyada kaam ki cheez hai. Kam rate har mahine thoda bachata hai. Mushkil mein padne ke kam raste ek baar mein bahut bachate hain.

Kya badla, seedhe shabdon mein

GST ke arrest powers hata diye gaye. Yahi headline hai. Tax officers ab aam vivaad mein arrest ko tool ki tarah istemal nahi kar sakte.

Prosecution threshold ₹1 crore se ₹5 crore ho gayi. Is amount se neeche vivaad ke criminal banne ke chance kaafi kam hain.

General penalty ₹25,000 se ₹10,000 ho gayi. Ek chhoti procedural galti ab pehle se aadhe se bhi kam ki padti hai.

Refund acknowledgement 15 din se 10 din ho gaya, aur system risk assessment ke baad claim ka 90% provisional sanction karega, order teen working days mein.

Chhote business ka ek option jo jaanna chahiye

Council ne in-principle optional annual return with quarterly payment scheme approve kiya hai un B2C businesses ke liye jinki turnover ₹5 crore tak hai. Lagataar filings ki jagah ek chhota seller saal mein ek baar file kar sakta hai aur quarterly pay kar sakta hai.

Yahan kuch bhi ye nahi badalta ki aap bill par GST kaise nikalte ho. ₹1,000 ka item 18% par ab bhi ₹1,180 hai, aur ₹1,180 ke bill mein tax ab bhi ₹180 hai. Upar wala calculator dono taraf se ye split karta hai.

Is hafte asli mein kya karein

Dekho ki aapki turnover aapko annual-return option ke ₹5 crore band mein laati hai ya nahi. Agar haan, apne accountant se poocho ki ye aapke cash cycle ke liye theek hai — quarterly pay karna us business ki madad karta hai jiski kamai uneven ho.

Agar aap export ya zero-rated goods bechte ho, to refund jaldi aane ki umeed rakho. Working capital purane timeline ki jagah naye timeline par plan karo.

Aur apne returns consistent rakho. Council ne ye bhi kaha hai ki jo aap report karte ho aur jo input tax credit claim karte ho, usme mismatch kam ho. Aasan enforcement ka matlab enforcement gayab nahi hai.

Kya nahi badla

Rates. 5%, 12%, 18% aur 28% slabs bilkul wahin hain jahan the, aur Council ne ishara diya hai ki rate changes ek saal mein ek baar wale cycle mein aane chahiye, achanak nahi.

Registration thresholds aur basic filing obligation bhi waise hi hain. Agar aap pichhle mahine registered the, to aaj bhi ho, aur jo returns aapko dene hain unka shape nahi badla.
