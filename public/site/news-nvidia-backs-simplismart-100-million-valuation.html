Nvidia Backs Simplismart at a $100 Million Valuation

Nvidia joined a $14 million round for Bengaluru's Simplismart at a reported $100 million valuation. We split the two numbers apart for Indian founders.

FundingOctober 29, 2026

Bengaluru's Simplismart has raised $14 million in an all-primary Series B round led by Dallas Venture Capital, with Nvidia, Accel, Shastra VC and Micromax also investing. Reports put the four-year-old company's valuation at about $100 million, including the fresh money.

The pitch is that its software helps companies run AI models on the GPUs they already own rather than buying more of them. For a founder reading the headline, the chip giant's name is not the interesting part. The interesting part is what a $14 million cheque against a $100 million valuation says about who owns what.

What Simplismart actually sells

Simplismart sits between an AI model and the machines it runs on. Its software decides how a model is deployed, how fast it answers, how many requests it can hold at once, and how much computing power it burns. Customers can run it on Simplismart's own platform or inside their own cloud and in-house servers.

It also offers fine-tuning, automatic scaling as demand moves, and performance monitoring. Cloud providers can build their own AI inference services on top of it. Indian cloud provider Yotta already uses it to power a serverless inference service.

The arithmetic of a $100 million valuation

A post-money valuation is simply the pre-money value plus the new money. So a $100 million post-money figure minus the $14 million raised leaves a pre-money value of $86 million. The new investors together own $14 million divided by $100 million, which is 14% of the company. Everyone who was already on the cap table keeps the other 86%.

That is the whole trick of a funding headline, and it is easy to run on your own numbers. Suppose you raise Rs 1 crore at a post-money valuation of Rs 10 crore. The investor ends up with 10% of the business and the pre-money value is Rs 9 crore. If the same Rs 1 crore came in at a Rs 5 crore post-money valuation, the investor would take 20% instead. Same cheque, double the ownership, because only the valuation changed.

Why fewer GPUs is the whole pitch

Running AI is expensive because the chips are expensive. Simplismart says it helped the video platform InVideo cut its GPU serving costs by 56% using its software. Put that in rupee terms: a bill of Rs 10,000 a month would fall to roughly Rs 4,400, a saving of about Rs 5,600, without buying a single new machine.

The company says its annualised revenue run rate grew 14-fold in six months, to an eight-figure sum, and that more than half of its revenue now comes from the United States even though it has more customers in India. That gap between where the customers are and where the money is matters to any Indian founder selling software abroad.

What it means for you

Two lessons fit a shop or a startup. First, a valuation is a split, not a prize: the bigger the number, the smaller the slice you hand over for the same cheque. Second, cost per unit is where small companies win. If your AI or your software bill can drop by half on the hardware you already own, that saving goes straight to your margin.

Before you celebrate a headline number, work out what it implies for your own cap table. A valuation you can explain in one line is worth more than one you cannot.

Bengaluru ki Simplismart ne $14 million ka Series B round uthaya hai, jise Dallas Venture Capital lead kar raha hai. Nvidia, Accel, Shastra VC aur Micromax bhi is round mein shamil hue. Reports ke mutabik chaar saal purani company ki valuation, naye paise ko milakar, lagbhag $100 million hai.

Company ka pitch yeh hai ki uski software companies ko apne existing GPU par hi AI models chalane mein madad karti hai, naye khareedne ki zarurat nahi. Ek founder ke liye headline mein chip company ka naam interesting nahi hai. Interesting baat yeh hai ki $14 million ka cheque aur $100 million ki valuation milkar batate hain ki company mein kiska hissa kitna hai.

Simplismart asal mein kya bechti hai

Simplismart AI model aur uske chalne wali machines ke beech baithti hai. Uski software tay karti hai ki model kaise deploy hoga, kitni tezi se jawab dega, ek saath kitni requests sambhalega, aur kitni computing power kharch karega. Customer ise Simplismart ke platform par ya apne cloud aur apne server par chala sakta hai.

Iske saath fine-tuning, demand ke hisaab se automatic scaling, aur performance monitoring bhi milti hai. Cloud providers iske upar apni AI inference service bana sakte hain. Indian cloud provider Yotta pehle se ise use karta hai.

$100 million valuation ka hisaab

Post-money valuation matlab pre-money value plus naya paisa. Toh $100 million post-money mein se $14 million ghatao, pre-money value $86 million bachti hai. Naye investors ke paas milkar $14 million divided by $100 million, yaani company ka 14% hota hai. Baaki 86% purane shareholders ke paas rehta hai.

Funding headline ka pura khel yahi hai, aur aap apne numbers par bhi ise chala sakte hain. Maan lijiye aap Rs 1 crore uthate hain Rs 10 crore ki post-money valuation par. Investor ko business ka 10% milta hai aur pre-money value Rs 9 crore hoti hai. Wahi Rs 1 crore agar Rs 5 crore post-money par aata, toh investor 20% le leta. Same cheque, double ownership, sirf valuation badalne se.

Kam GPU hi pura pitch hai

AI chalana mehnga hai kyunki chips mehngi hain. Simplismart kehti hai ki usne video platform InVideo ki GPU serving cost 56% kam karne mein madad ki. Rupees mein dekhiye: Rs 10,000 ka monthly bill lagbhag Rs 4,400 ho jayega, yaani karib Rs 5,600 ki bachat, bina ek bhi nayi machine khareede.

Company kehti hai ki uski annualised revenue run rate chhah mahine mein 14 guna badhi, aur aath figure ke sum tak pahunchi. Aadhe se zyada revenue ab United States se aata hai, halanki zyada customers India mein hain. Customers kahan hain aur paisa kahan se aata hai, yeh antar kisi bhi Indian founder ke liye maayne rakhta hai.

Aapke liye iska matlab

Do seekh, dukaan aur startup dono ke liye. Pehla, valuation ek baant hai, inaam nahi: number jitna bada, utna hi kam hissa aap same cheque ke liye dete hain. Doosra, per-unit cost wahi jagah hai jahan chhoti company jeetti hai. Agar aapka AI ya software bill aapki existing hardware par hi aadha ho jaye, toh bachat seedha margin mein jaati hai.

Kisi bhi headline number par khush hone se pehle, dekhiye ki woh aapki cap table ke liye kya matlab rakhta hai. Ek line mein samjhane wali valuation, na samjhane wali se zyada keemti hai.
