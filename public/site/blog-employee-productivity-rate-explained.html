Employee Productivity Rate: The Formula

Output divided by people, and what the number really tells you.

Small BusinessNovember 19, 2026

A productivity number answers one plain question: how much does each person on your payroll actually produce? For a small Indian business this is the cheapest efficiency check there is, because both halves of the sum — what you make or sell, and how many people it took — are already sitting in your books.

This guide gives the formula, works one unit through it end to end, and shows the two ways the number misleads an owner who reads it on its own.

What the productivity rate measures

Employee productivity rate is output divided by the people who produced it. Output can be counted in units — shirts, boxes, deliveries, invoices — in rupees of sales, or in rupees of value added. Pick one and stay with it, because a rate that switches units every month cannot be compared with anything.

  • Units per person — the clearest version inside one workshop, where everyone does similar work.
  • Sales per person — the practical version for a trading or retail business with a mixed team.
  • Value added per person — sales minus bought-in material, and the fairest version when material costs are large.

The rate is a ratio, not a target. On its own it says nothing about whether you are doing well. It only says something when you compare this month with last month, or your unit with a similar one.

The formula, in one line

  • Productivity rate = Output ÷ Number of employees
  • Use the same period on both sides: 40 shirts ÷ 5 tailors = 8 shirts per tailor.
  • To turn the rate into money, multiply by the price per unit, or divide the wage bill by the output.

Two derived figures come out of the same numbers and belong next to the rate. Labour cost per unit = total wages ÷ total output. Output per rupee of wage = output ÷ total wages. The first tells you what a unit costs you in labour; the second tells you how hard each rupee of wage is working.

A worked example: five tailors and 40 shirts

A small tailoring unit has five tailors. Each is paid ₹800 a day and finishes eight shirts a day.

  1. Daily wage bill: 5 × ₹800 = ₹4,000.
  2. Daily output: 5 × 8 = 40 shirts.
  3. Productivity rate: 40 ÷ 5 = 8 shirts per tailor per day.
  4. Labour cost per shirt: ₹4,000 ÷ 40 = ₹100.
  5. Output per rupee of wage: one tailor costs ₹800 a day and makes eight shirts that sell at ₹1,000 each, so each ₹1 of wage produces ₹10 of output.

Now the owner buys a machine for ₹4,000. Output rises to ten shirts a tailor, or 50 shirts a day, with the same five people and the same ₹4,000 wage bill.

  • Productivity rate: 50 ÷ 5 = 10 shirts per tailor per day, a gain of 25%.
  • Labour cost per shirt: ₹4,000 ÷ 50 = ₹80, down from ₹100.
  • Payback: ₹20 saved on every shirt, so the ₹4,000 machine pays for itself after 200 shirts — four working days at the new rate.

Every figure reconciles: 5 × ₹800 = ₹4,000, and ₹4,000 ÷ 40 = ₹100, ₹4,000 ÷ 50 = ₹80, ₹20 × 200 = ₹4,000.

Output per person is not output per rupee

The two move apart whenever the mix of people changes. Put two supervisors on the floor and units per person may fall while labour cost per unit falls faster, because fewer shirts are reworked. That is a good month with a bad-looking rate.

The reverse happens just as easily. A cheap hire lifts output per person on paper while the wage bill climbs faster than the output does, and profit quietly shrinks. This is why the rate is worth reading beside revenue per employee and beside your labour cost per unit — one number alone can be flattered by either direction.

What a falling number actually tells you

A drop of 10% in output per person is not a verdict on the team. It is a signal to check the four things that usually cause it:

  1. Volume fell, but people stayed. Fewer orders with the same payroll looks exactly like lower productivity.
  2. New people are still learning. A hire costs full wages from day one and produces little for the first month or two.
  3. Work is waiting, not missing. Material that arrives late leaves paid people idle, which the rate records as lost output.
  4. The work itself changed. A new product line with more steps per unit lowers units per person without anyone slowing down.

Each of those has a different fix, and none of them is "push harder". Measure the rate monthly, note the volume and headcount beside it, and the cause is usually visible in the same row.

Three ways to raise it without hiring

  • Remove one waiting step. In the tailoring unit, cloth cut the evening before removed the morning's idle hour. Same people, more shirts.
  • Give the bottleneck the help. Adding a person to the slowest station raises total output without touching anyone's speed.
  • Cut rework. Every shirt remade costs the full labour of two. A ₹4,000 machine that halves rejects often beats a second shift.

All three raise output per person without adding a salary, which is why they are the first place to look before a hiring decision.

The short version

  • Productivity rate = output ÷ number of employees, in units or in rupees, measured over the same period.
  • Read it beside labour cost per unit and output per rupee of wage; one number alone can mislead.
  • A fall usually means volume dropped, a new hire is still learning, material arrived late, or the product mix changed.
  • Raise it by removing a waiting step, helping the bottleneck, or cutting rework — before you add a salary.

Prodaktivite ka aankda ek seedha sawal poochhta hai: aapki perol par baitha har vyakti asal mein kitna prodyoos karta hai? Chhote bhaarateey karobar ke liye yeh sabse sasta chek hai, kyunki hisaab ke dono hisse — aap kya banate ya bechte hain, aur usamen kitne log lage — aapki kitaabon mein pehle se maujood hain.

Yeh guide formula dete hai, ek yoonit ka pura hisaab karke dikhati hai, aur yeh bhi bataate hai ki akele aankda padhane par maalik kin do jagahon par galat nateeja nikaal leta hai.

Prodaktivite ret kya naapate hai

Employe prodaktivite ret yani aautaput ko un logon ki sankhya par bhaag dena jinhonne woh aautaput banaaya. Aautaput yoonit mein gina ja sakta hai — shart, boks, dileevare, invoice — ya bikri ke rupayon mein, ya vailyoo eded ke rupayon mein. Ek tarika chunakar usi par tike rahein, kyunki har mahine yoonit badalane wale aankde ki kisi se tulna nahi ho sakate.

  • Prati vyakti yoonit — ek hi varkashop ke andar sabse saaf tarika, jahaan sabaka kaam ek jaisa hai.
  • Prati vyakti bikri — mile-jule tim wale treding ya ritel karobar ke liye vyaavahaarik tarika.
  • Prati vyakti vailyoo eded — bikri mein se khareeda hua maal ghataakar, aur jab maal ki lagat bade ho to sabse nyaayapoorn tarika.

Ret ek anupaat hai, lakshya nahi. Akele yeh nahi bataate ki aap achha chal rahe hain ya nahi. Yeh tabhe kuch kahate hai jab aap is mahine ko pichhale mahine se, ya apni yoonit ko milate-julate yoonit se tulna karein.

Formula, ek laain mein

  • Prodaktivite ret = aautaput ÷ karmachaariyon ki sankhya
  • Dono taraf ek hi avadhi rakhein: 40 shart ÷ 5 darze = 8 shart prati darze.
  • Ret ko paise mein badalane ke liye prati yoonit keemat se guna karein, ya vetan bil ko aautaput par bhaag dein.

Inheen aankdon se do aur cheezein nikalti hain aur ret ke saath likhe jaane chahiye. Prati yoonit mazadoore lagat = kul vetan ÷ kul aautaput. Prati rupaya vetan aautaput = aautaput ÷ kul vetan. Pehla batata hai ki ek yoonit par mazadoore mein kitna kharch hota hai; doosara batata hai ki vetan ka har rupaya kitni mehnat kar raha hai.

Ek udaharan: paanch darze aur 40 shart

Ek chhoti telaring yoonit mein paanch darze hain. Har ek ko ₹800 roz milate hain aur har ek din mein aath shart poore karta hai.

  1. Roz ka vetan bil: 5 × ₹800 = ₹4,000.
  2. Roz ka aautaput: 5 × 8 = 40 shart.
  3. Prodaktivite ret: 40 ÷ 5 = 8 shart prati darze prati din.
  4. Prati shart mazadoore lagat: ₹4,000 ÷ 40 = ₹100.
  5. Prati rupaya vetan aautaput: ek darze ka din ₹800 ka hai aur woh aath shart banata hai jo ₹1,000 mein bikate hain, yani vetan ka har ₹1 das rupaye ka aautaput banata hai.

Ab maalik ₹4,000 ki ek machine khareedata hai. Aautaput badhakar das shart prati darze, yani 50 shart roz ho jaata hai — wahi paanch log aur wahi ₹4,000 ka vetan bil.

  • Prodaktivite ret: 50 ÷ 5 = 10 shart prati darze prati din, yani 25% ki badhat.
  • Prati shart mazadoore lagat: ₹4,000 ÷ 50 = ₹80, jo ₹100 se kam hai.
  • Pebaik: har shart par ₹20 bache, to ₹4,000 ki machine 200 shart mein apni keemat nikaal lete hai — nayi raftaar se chaar kaam ke din.

Har aankda milta hai: 5 × ₹800 = ₹4,000, aur ₹4,000 ÷ 40 = ₹100, ₹4,000 ÷ 50 = ₹80, ₹20 × 200 = ₹4,000.

Prati vyakti aautaput aur prati rupaya aautaput ek nahi hain

Jab bhi logon ka mishran badalta hai, dono alag chalane lagte hain. Flor par do suparavaaizar bitha dein to prati vyakti yoonit gir sakate hai, lekin prati yoonit mazadoore lagat usse tez girate hai, kyunki reevark kam hota hai. Yeh achha mahina hai jisaka aankda kharaab dikhta hai.

Ulta bhi utna hi aasan hai. Sasta haayar kaagaz par prati vyakti aautaput badha deta hai, jabaki vetan bil aautaput se tez badhta hai aur munafa chupchaap sikud jaata hai. Iseelie ret ko prati karmachaare bikri aur prati yoonit mazadoore lagat ke saath padhna chahiye — ek akela aankda dono taraf se badha-chadhaakar dikha sakta hai.

Girata aankda asal mein kya batata hai

Prati vyakti aautaput mein 10% ki giraavat tim par faisala nahi hai. Yeh chaar cheezein jaanchane ka ishaara hai, jinase aam taur par aisa hota hai:

  1. Kaam ghata, log wahi rahe. Kam order aur utni hi perol kam prodaktivite jaise hi dikhate hai.
  2. Nae log abhe seekh rahe hain. Naya haayar pehle din se pura vetan leta hai aur shuruaati ek-do mahine kam prodyoos karta hai.
  3. Kaam ruka hai, gaya nahi. Maal der se aaye to vetan paate log khaale baithate hain, aur ret ise khoya aautaput maan lete hai.
  4. Kaam khud badal gaya. Zyada step wali nayi product laain prati vyakti yoonit ghata dete hai, bina kisi ke dheema hue.

Har ek ka ilaaj alag hai, aur kisi ka bhi ilaaj "zyada zor lagaao" nahi hai. Ret har mahine naapen, usake saath volyoom aur hedakaaunt bhi likhen, aur vajah aam taur par usi pankti mein dikh jaati hai.

Bina bharte kiye ise badhane ke teen tarike

  • Intazaar ka ek step hataaen. Telaring yoonit mein kapada pichhale shaam katane se subah ka khaale ghanta hat gaya. Wahi log, zyada shart.
  • Botalanek ko madad dein. Sabse dheeme steshan par ek aadame jodne se kul aautaput badhta hai, kisi ki raftaar badle bina.
  • Reevark ghatayein. Dobaara banaaee gayi har shart do shart ki poore mazadoore kha jaati hai. Rijekt aadhe karne wali ₹4,000 ki machine aksar doosare shift se behtar saabit hoti hai.

Teenon vetan badhaae bina prati vyakti aautaput badhaate hain, iseelie bharte ka faisala lene se pehle yahin dekhna chahiye.

Chhota saaraansh

  • Prodaktivite ret = aautaput ÷ karmachaariyon ki sankhya, yoonit mein ya rupayon mein, ek hi avadhi par naape gayi.
  • Ise prati yoonit mazadoore lagat aur prati rupaya vetan aautaput ke saath padhein; akela aankda bhataka sakta hai.
  • Giraavat ka matlab aam taur par volyoom girana, naya haayar ka seekhana, maal ka der se aana, ya product miks badalana hai.
  • Vetan jodne se pehle intazaar ka step hataakar, botalanek ki madad karke, ya reevark ghataakar ise badhayein.
